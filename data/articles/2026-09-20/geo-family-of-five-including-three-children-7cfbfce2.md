KARACHI: The bodies of a family of five — a husband, wife and three children — were recovered from a flat in Site's Ahsanabad area on Sunday, with police saying the bodies were several days old and that suicide was suspected.
In a statement, Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) East Zubair Nazir Sheikh said the bodies were found in a single room on the seventh floor of the building, with the flat's door locked from the inside.
He said the deceased man was a doctor and the family's sole provider, adding that the bodies had been transferred to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital after completion of legal formalities.
"The actual cause of death will only be determined after the postmortem," he said, adding that investigations were ongoing from multiple angles to identify the deceased and establish the full circumstances.
A neighbour told police that the doctor had been unemployed for a considerable period after his clinic shut down, but had recently secured employment at East City Hospital.
Meanwhile, Sindh Home Minister Zia-ul-Hassan Lanjar sought details from SSP East and directed him to immediately brief him on all initial police actions taken.
He said the investigation should be conducted effectively and its conclusions made public.
Sindh Inspector General of Police sought a detailed report from SSP East and directed that the investigation be conducted transparently, impartially and completed as swiftly as possible on the basis of merit and evidence.
