# After Aleema, Imran’s sisters Uzma and Noreen also detained in Lahore under MPO for 30 days

Former prime minister and PTI founder Imran Khan’s sisters, Dr Uzma Khan and Noreen Niazi, were detained for 30 days on Monday, ahead of the party’s September 27 protest call.
The development follows the detention of Imran’s other sister, Aleema Khan, after Lahore’s deputy commissioner (DC) ordered her detention or arrest for 30 days on Sunday.
The detention has come amid reports of a crackdown on the PTI ahead of its September 27 countrywide protest and march to Islamabad, which the party has called to demand Imran’s release from jail and mobilise people for the supremacy of the Constitution.
In an order dated Sept 20, the Lahore Deputy Commissioner’s Office ordered that Noreen and Uzma be detained for 30 days under the Punjab Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance, 1960, stating that the two were “prejudicial to the public safety and maintenance of public order”.
The MPO ordinance allows the authorities to order detention and restrict judicial review of such orders.
The two will remain in custody under the superintendent, Central Jail, Kot Lakhpat, it said.
The order stated that the two were involved in the May 9 protest and “motivated the workers to damage public and government property”.
“Moreover, they are again mobilising the workers through social media and public meetings to create a law and order situation, raising/chanting objectionable slogans amongst the general public against the government/institution in order to provoke and incite them,” the order read.
“Accordingly, multiple cases including 96/23 PS Race Course were registered,” it said, stating that Uzma and Noreen were “potential dangers to public peace and tranquillity”.
In a post on X in the early hours of Monday, the party’s Central Information Secretary Sheikh Waqas Akram had accused Punjab police of “arresting both sisters of Imran, along with their children and the sons of Aleema Khan”.
Akram claimed that the individuals had “been placed in an armoured vehicle and are being taken to an unknown location”.
PTI lawyer Awais Younas Chaudhry confirmed to Dawn that all three of Imran’s sisters had been arrested.
“Earlier Noreen Niazi and Uzma Khan were confined to their house, but on Monday morning they were taken to an unknown place,” he claimed.
PTI, in a post on X, condemned the alleged arrest, calling it a “blatant abuse of authority, a violation of fundamental rights, and an act of political victimisation”.
“Such conduct is illegal, unconstitutional, and is condemned in the strongest possible terms,” the party said.
Meanwhile, Imran’s son Kasim Khan, in a post on X, alleged that Uzma and Noreen had been “abducted”.
“This is a disgrace,” Kasim wrote, claiming that authorities had “broken into my aunties and cousins houses and forced them into vehicles with no explanation”.
“No reasons for arrest have been stated. No news has been released as to where they have been taken,” he wrote.
Aleema’s detention
On Sunday, the office of Lahore’s deputy commissioner (DC) ordered Aleema’s detention or arrest for 30 days.
The September 20 order, a copy of which is available with Dawn, said DC Muhammad Ali Ijaz had ordered the detention or arrest of Aleema and a man named Muhammad Ameer under the Punjab MPO.
The MPO ordinance allows the authorities to order detention and restrict judicial review of such orders.
Following the arrest, Aleema’s sister Noreen addressed a press conference in Lahore, flanked by her sister’s counsel, Advocate Mudassir Umar.
Noreen claimed Aleema was “picked up” along with her driver after she left her residence on Saturday, and they later learnt that Aleema’s vehicle was parked at a police station. Meanwhile, the lawyer said they had yet to receive official confirmation of her arrest.
Police sources had confirmed to Dawn on Sunday that Aleema was taken to Lahore’s Kot Lakhpat jail.
Detentions ahead of Sept 27 protest
The detentions come after a string of reported actions against the PTI in Punjab ahead of its September 27 protest.
On Sunday, Sahiwal police arrested Asad Khan Baloch, former mayor of the municipal corporation and ex-ticket holder for PTI, along with the party’s city president in Sahiwal, Muhammad Shahid Chohan.
PTI also claimed that MNAs Sanaullah Mastikhel, Ahsan Jamal Khan and Mian Ghous were taken into custody, according to the party’s information secretary.
District authorities had also issued preventive detention orders against dozens of PTI workers under MPO a week ago, including 31 in one Punjab notification and a further 14 in Toba Tek Singh.
Similar reports have come from Islamabad, where the PTI plans to march on the protest day.
However, the government has vowed to stop the march, and the Islamabad High Court (IHC) has ruled that no political party or its leadership has the “lawful right to occupy public roads, highways, interchanges, toll plazas and buildings in Islamabad”.
