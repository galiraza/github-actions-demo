# 16 terrorists killed in separate security forces, police operations across Balochistan: state media

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 23 Sep 2026 15:32:56 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2032069/16-terrorists-killed-in-separate-security-forces-police-operations-across-balochistan-state-media
- Collected: 2026-09-23 12:31:03 UTC

---

At least 16 terrorists were killed during separate military and police operations in Balochistan’s Mastung, Turbat and Quetta districts on Wednesday, state media reported.
According to Radio Pakistan, security forces killed two terrorists affiliated with Fitna al Hindustan during an intelligence-based operation in the Jausak area of Turbat.
The state has designated Balochistan-based terrorist groups as Fitna al Hindustan to highlight India’s alleged role in terrorism and destabilisation across Pakistan.
Meanwhile, Pakistan Television reported that security forces continued operations against terrorists in Balochistan under Raddul Fitna-3.
In Mastung, nine terrorists were killed after they attempted to attack a checkpost, according to the state broadcaster. Heavy weapons, ammunition and three motorcycles were also recovered, it added.
“Operations will continue until the threat is eliminated,” Pakistan TV said, quoting the security forces.
Meanwhile, Balochistan police killed five terrorists in a gun battle in Quetta’s Aghbarg area after terrorists attempted to attack a police station, officials said.
Officials said the terrorists targeted the Aghbarg police station, located on the outskirts of Quetta, in the early hours of Wednesday. Police personnel deployed at the station immediately returned fire.
“In a heavy exchange of fire, five terrorists belonging to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan were killed by police,” Saddar Senior Superintendent of Police Shafqat Janjua said. He added that police had foiled the attack and seized weapons and ammunition.
According to Janjua, the attackers arrived in the area on motorcycles before targeting the police station.
Separately, the Edhi Foundation said one of its ambulances was allegedly hijacked by armed men in Balochistan on Wednesday.
In a statement, the foundation said the ambulance was returning to the Edhi Kalat Centre at around 7:15am after transporting the body of a woman from an area near Gidar, about 110 kilometres from Civil Hospital, Kalat.
The foundation said unidentified armed men assaulted the Edhi worker and took control of the ambulance.
On Tuesday, Radio Pakistan reported that six terrorists were killed and nine suspected individuals were arrested during an intelligence-based operation (IBO) by security forces in Balochistan’s Saran Tangi area.
On Monday, security forces killed four more terrorists belonging to a banned organisation in an intelligence-based operation carried out in two areas of Panjgur district.
Last week, security forces killed 11 terrorists in separate operations in Panjgur and Chagai districts.
The number of terrorist attacks in the province increased by seven per cent from 83 in July to 89 in August, according to a monthly assessment by the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies.
