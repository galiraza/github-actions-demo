# Pakistan secure another Asian Games medal as Noor, Ashab reach squash semis

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 25 Sep 2026 16:54:19 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2032570/pakistan-secure-another-asian-games-medal-as-noor-ashab-reach-squash-semis
- Collected: 2026-09-25 12:31:31 UTC

---

Pakistan secured another medal at the Asian Games on Friday after Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan won their respective singles matches to reach the semi-finals of the squash competition.
Third-seeded Noor defeated Hong Kong’s Chi Hin Henry Leung, while fifth-seeded Ashab overcame India’s Veer Chotrani at the Nagoya Kinjo Futo Arena to put Pakistan into the medal rounds of the event.
Noor, Pakistan’s top-ranked player in the draw, was pushed by Leung but held his nerve to secure a place in the last four. Ashab also produced a strong performance against the fourth-seeded Indian, recovering from losing the opening game to win 3-1.
Ashab prevailed 6-11, 11-8, 11-6, 11-6 to avenge Pakistan’s defeat to India in the men’s team final at the previous Asian Games.
The victories mean Pakistan are assured of at least a bronze medal in the men’s singles competition, with the losing semi-finalists awarded bronze.
It is the first time since 2002 that two players for Pakistan have made it to the Asian Games semi-finals
The two Pakistanis had reached the quarter-finals without dropping a game, with Noor defeating Kuwait’s Ammar Altamimi 11-4, 11-9, 11-4 and Ashab overcoming Saudi Arabia’s Mohammad Al Alnafsan 11-9, 11-3, 11-5 in the last 16 on Thursday.
Noor and Ashab will now contest their respective semi-finals on Saturday, with a place in Sunday’s final at stake.
