# Bahawalpur teenager sent to jail for alleged AI-assisted attempt to poison father

- Collected: 2026-09-30 13:17:39 UTC

LAHORE: A judicial magistrate on Wednesday sent a teenage suspect to jail on a 14-day judicial remand in a case of attempting to poison his father after seeking information from artificial intelligence.
The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) produced the 17-year-old suspect before Judicial Magistrate Naeem Wattoo on the completion of his three-day physical remand.
The magistrate asked the investigating officer (IO) whether the suspect’s father had any objection to the case.
The officer replied that the father of the suspect had no objection. He also informed the magistrate that the teenager was his parents’ only son.
He said the father told the investigators that his son, a second-year student in Bahawalpur, used to conduct various kinds of research.
The IO said the physical custody of the suspect was no longer required.
At this, the magistrate sent the suspect to jail on a 14-day judicial remand and directed the IO to submit a charge-sheet within the stipulated time.
‘Breaking Bad’
The NCCIA arrested the teenager on Sep 23. The agency said at the time that the suspect had reportedly developed strained relations with his father and allegedly planned to kill him following a disagreement.
During the investigation, authorities learned that the teenager had developed an interest in chemistry after watching the American crime drama ‘Breaking Bad’. He had reportedly set up a small laboratory at home to conduct chemical experiments, the NCCIA spokesperson said.
The suspect allegedly obtained chemical material from Australia and Islamabad and concocted a substance at home with the intention of poisoning his father. He reportedly administered the substance to his father on one occasion, but the attempt was not successful, the spokesperson said.
Subsequently, the teenager allegedly sought further guidance from AI, asking how to administer a poison capable of causing his father’s death without detection, he added.
The spokesperson said the Punjab NCCIA received the information about the suspect through an automated system. Following this, NCCIA Punjab Director Hissam Bin Iqbal directed the formation of a team to investigate the matter.
The team later conducted an operation in Bahawalpur and arrested the suspect. Further investigation had been initiated to determine the circumstances surrounding the incident, the nature of the chemicals involved and the suspect’s online activities, the spokesperson said.
AI chatbots and agents have been linked to cases of harm and death, leading to concerns about their usage and the imposition of tighter regulations and controls.
The European Union last month introduced sweeping laws that give it the power to enforce landmark AI rules and to fine any companies that break the law. Pakistan has also previously called for the use of AI to be regulated by the United Nations charter.
Last year, American artificial intelligence firm OpenAI said it would add parental controls to its chatbot ChatGPT after an American couple said the system had encouraged their teenage son to kill himself.
