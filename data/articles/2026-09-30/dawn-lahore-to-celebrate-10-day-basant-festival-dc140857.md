# Lahore to celebrate 10-day Basant festival from Jan 29 to Feb 7 next year

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 30 Sep 2026 15:12:56 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2033737/lahore-to-celebrate-10-day-basant-festival-from-jan-29-to-feb-7-next-year
- Collected: 2026-09-30 13:17:39 UTC

---

LAHORE: The Punjab capital will celebrate Basant for 10 days from January 29 to February 7 next year, with a one-day break on Feb 5 for Kashmir Day, under a schedule approved by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz on Wednesday.
Lahore celebrated the Basant festival earlier this year from Feb 6 to 8 after an 18-year hiatus.
The festival was banned in 2007 because of an increasing number of deaths and serious injuries caused by sharp strings, particularly to motorcyclists and pillion riders, as well as by celebratory gunfire.
During a meeting, the chief minister directed the authorities to make arrangements for the 10-day celebration, according to a statement released by the government.
She described the festival as a gift for the people of Lahore to mark the arrival of 2027 and as a means of promoting Punjab’s cultural heritage and the historic traditions of the provincial capital.
Under the schedule approved by the chief minister, Basant festivities will begin on Jan 29 and continue until Feb 7. However, celebrations will remain suspended on Feb 5 to observe Kashmir Day.
“Ten-day Basant will promote Punjab’s cultural colours and Lahore’s centuries-old traditions across the world,” CM Maryam was quoted as saying.
According to the statement, the chief minister directed the administration to ensure better arrangements for public safety and convenience than those made during the previous festival.
She also appealed to citizens to celebrate responsibly, saying the authorities and the public should set a new example through a sense of duty, responsibility and commitment to protecting human lives.
CM Maryam directed the relevant departments to begin preparations well in advance and ensure that the extended festivities did not compromise public safety, the statement said.
She stressed that the success of the 10-day event would depend on strict adherence to safety regulations and responsible behaviour by citizens.
“The chief minister said Lahore’s cultural identity and traditions should be promoted while ensuring that celebrations remained safe and convenient for residents and visitors,” the statement concluded.
In August, CM Maryam announced that the festival would be held from Mar 12 to 14, 2027. However, in the latest announcement, the government has revised the schedule.
The Punjab government, in May, had also announced a new set of rules and regulations for kite-flying during next year’s Basant festival.
