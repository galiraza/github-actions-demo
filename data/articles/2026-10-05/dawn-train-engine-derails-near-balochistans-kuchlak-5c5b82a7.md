# Train engine derails near Balochistan's Kuchlak

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Mon, 05 Oct 2026 07:34:34 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2034857/train-engine-derails-near-balochistans-kuchlak
- Collected: 2026-10-05 06:31:45 UTC

---

QUETTA: The engine of the newly launched Jinnah Peoples Train, operating between Kuchlak and Sariab, derailed near Kuchlak Railway Station on Sunday while leaving for its next destination towards Quetta.
No casualties were reported and all passengers remained safe.
Railway authorities immediately arranged a replacement engine and the train departed for Sariab after a brief delay.
According to railway officials, engine No. 5120 derailed on Line No. 3 on Sunday while reversing during routine shunting operations. Officials initially attributed the incident to the alleged theft of a fishplate, saying an investigation was under way to determine the actual cause.
However, Kuchlak police disputed the railway authorities’ claim and said the derailment was caused by the poor condition of the track, where railway workers had been carrying out work. Railway authorities have ordered an inquiry into the derailment.
