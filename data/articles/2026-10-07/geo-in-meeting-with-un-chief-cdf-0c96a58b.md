Published October 07, 2026
ISLAMABAD: United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres called on Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) and Chief of Army Staff (COAS) Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir at the General Headquarters (GHQ) in Rawalpindi on Wednesday.
The meeting focused on matters of mutual interest, the prevailing regional security environment and the UN's role in promoting international peace and security, the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said in a statement.
The discussions also covered regional stability, the Jammu and Kashmir dispute, India's continued abeyance of the Indus Waters Treaty (IWT) and terrorism emanating from Afghanistan, the military's media wing said.
During the meeting, Field Marshal Munir reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to international peace and highlighted the country's contributions to United Nations peacekeeping missions.
He also praised the UN's role, under the leadership of Secretary-General Guterres, in advancing global peace, stability and humanitarian cooperation.
The UN chief paid tribute to the sacrifices made by Pakistan and its armed forces in the war against terrorism and acknowledged their contributions to UN peacekeeping operations, according to the military's media wing.
Guterres also recognised Pakistan's continued commitment to international peace and security and appreciated its efforts towards the peaceful resolution of regional conflicts.
