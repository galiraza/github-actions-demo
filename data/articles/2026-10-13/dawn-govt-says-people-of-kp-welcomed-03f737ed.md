# Govt says people of KP ‘welcomed’ governor's rule as it gave them ‘hope’

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 13 Oct 2026 12:08:45 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2036847/govt-says-people-of-kp-welcomed-governors-rule-as-it-gave-them-hope
- Collected: 2026-10-13 07:19:46 UTC

---

Information Minister Ataullah Tarar on Tuesday claimed that the people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa had “welcomed” the federal government’s move to impose governor’s rule and declare an emergency in the province, saying the measures had given them “hope”.
He made the remarks while addressing a press conference alongside Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar.
Ataullah noted, “Had the people of KP supported the pro-terrorist stance of the PTI government, you would have seen millions on the street.”
However, the minister went on to claim that “barely a dozen” people gathered in protest.
Defending the move, the information minister said, “The people of KP have welcomed the step because they believe there is now hope for them; they believe they will now be provided safety and security, their lives and livelihood will be protected, and this political-crime nexus will be eliminated.”
At the outset of the press briefing, the law minister noted that the province in the recent past had witnessed a hike in terrorist incidents, noting a “195 per cent” rise, with 8,600 terrorist attacks recorded in 2024-2026 compared to 2,900 in 2020-2024.
Azam emphasised that it was essential that counterterrorism efforts taken by federal and provincial agencies be “harmonised”, adding that such an approach was standard practice globally.
“All these arrangements are made to run the country in a way that people feel safe,” he remarked.
At this, the law minister noted the “non-serious” attitude of the previous PTI-led KP government, which said, “has now ceased to exist”.
He recalled that the PTI, allegedly using government resources, “launched an onslaught on the capital”. However, at the time, the imposition of governor’s rule and emergency did not come under discussion.
