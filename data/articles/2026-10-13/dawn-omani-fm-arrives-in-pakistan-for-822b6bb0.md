# Omani FM arrives in Pakistan for Joint Ministerial Commission session

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 13 Oct 2026 21:16:42 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2036916/omani-fm-arrives-in-pakistan-for-joint-ministerial-commission-session
- Collected: 2026-10-13 19:49:20 UTC

---

Omani Foreign Minister Sayyid Badr Albusaidi arrived in Pakistan on a two-day visit on Tuesday, during which he would head his country’s delegation at the eighth Pakistan-Oman Joint Ministerial Commission (JMC) session.
According to the Foreign Office (FO), he was received upon his arrival by the Economic Affairs Division secretary and the foreign ministry’s additional secretary (Middle East).
He would also hold a bilateral meeting with Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar during his stay in Pakistan, the FO statement said.
In an earlier statement, the FO had said the JMC session would “serve as an important platform to review the entire spectrum of Pakistan-Oman bilateral relations and to further strengthen cooperation in diverse fields”.
The visit “reflects the longstanding fraternal relations between Pakistan and Oman and is expected to further deepen mutually beneficial cooperation across multiple sectors”, it had added.
On Saturday, DPM Dar chaired an inter-ministerial meeting in Islamabad to review the preparations for the upcoming JMC session, according to state-run Radio Pakistan.
The meeting reviewed the agenda, bilateral agreements and arrangements for the co-chairing of the JMC session, it said.
According to Radio Pakistan, the session will focus on “deepening cooperation in trade, investment, industry, agriculture, finance, oil and gas, labour, consular affairs, security, defence, climate change, civil aviation, education, media, and culture”.
Dar highlighted the importance of the commission in strengthening bilateral relations. He directed stakeholders to ensure thorough preparations and effective follow-up to “secure tangible outcomes”, the report added.
