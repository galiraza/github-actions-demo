# PTI Chairman Gohar moves PHC against emergency, governor's rule in KP

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 14 Oct 2026 17:35:21 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2037118/pti-chairman-gohar-moves-phc-against-emergency-governors-rule-in-kp
- Collected: 2026-10-14 12:37:22 UTC

---

PESHAWAR: PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan on Wednesday moved the Peshawar High Court (PHC) against the imposition of emergency and governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, requesting the court to declare the move unconstitutional.
Advocate Shah Faisal Ilyas submitted the petition on behalf of the PTI chairman.
The petitioner requested the PHC to declare the Oct 11 emergency proclamation as “unconstitutional, ultra vires, coram non judice, tainted with malice in law and fact, devoid of objective material, and of no legal effect”; and urged it to set the proclamation aside.
