Published October 15, 2026
ISLAMABAD: The Federal Constitutional Court (FCC) on Thursday disposed of MNA Sher Afzal Marwat’s petition challenging the election of Sohail Afridi as chief minister of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
The FCC bench, headed by Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan, declared the petition infructuous following the imposition of governor’s rule and an emergency in the province.
