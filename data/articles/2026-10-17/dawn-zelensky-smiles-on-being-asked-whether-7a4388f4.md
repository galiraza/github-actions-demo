# Zelensky smiles on being asked whether Trump is scapegoating him

- Published: Sat, 17 Oct 2026 08:12:50 +0500

BRUSSELS: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky gave a knowing smile and stayed mum on Friday when asked if the US president was scapegoating him for troubles in the US.
“This is my answer,” Zelensky said, as journalists broke into laughter at a press conference in Brussels after he met EU leaders.
Zelensky had been asked if Donald Trump was lashing out at the Ukrainian leader as a way to deflect from his own political strife ahead of next month’s midterm elections as he is bogged down with Iran and diesel prices have soared.
The question came after Trump said he found it easier to deal with Russian President Vladimir Putin than Zelensky as ties have soured with Kyiv.
“Yesterday was a thousand drones in Ukraine, a thousand, but the American side said that it is difficult to speak with Zelensky, it’s less difficult to manage with Putin,” Zelensky said.
