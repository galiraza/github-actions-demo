# Out-of-control truck kills 13 at religious festival in India's Madhya Pradesh

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 18 Oct 2026 12:02:23 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2038010/out-of-control-truck-kills-13-at-religious-festival-in-indias-madhya-pradesh
- Collected: 2026-10-18 11:55:09 UTC

---

A speeding truck in India killed 13 people when it ploughed into a crowd at a Hindu festival, officials said on Sunday, with President Droupadi Murmu describing the deaths as “heartbreaking”.
The heavily loaded truck reportedly lost control in the early hours of Sunday and hit devotees gathered at a temple in Gwalior, in the central state of Madhya Pradesh.
The Press Trust of India (PTI) news agency cited hospital officials as saying 13 people had died.
“I express my deepest condolences to the bereaved families and pray for the swift recovery of those injured,” Murmu said on social media.
The Indian Vice President C.P. Radhakrishnan expressed sorrow at the loss of life.
“Deeply saddened by the tragic loss of lives in the accident involving devotees in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. My heartfelt condolences to the bereaved families. I wish a speedy recovery to all those injured,” he wrote in a post on X.
The incident comes a day after at least 17 people were killed and two dozen more injured on Saturday in eastern India after a stampede that was likely caused by sudden rainfall during a Hindu religious gathering, officials said.
India, with 1.4 billion people, recorded more than 183,000 road traffic deaths in 2025, or over 500 a day, according to the roads ministry.
Many are linked to inadequate road infrastructure, ill-maintained vehicles and exhausted drivers, according to official data.
