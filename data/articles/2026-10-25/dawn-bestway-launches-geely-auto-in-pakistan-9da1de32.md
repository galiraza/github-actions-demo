# Bestway launches Geely Auto in Pakistan

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 25 Oct 2026 09:12:42 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2039572/bestway-launches-geely-auto-in-pakistan
- Collected: 2026-10-25 05:38:20 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: Bestway Group on Saturday launched Chinese automaker Geely Auto in Pakistan, acquiring the Proton plant at Port Qasim, Karachi, for assembly.
Bestway Automotive, Geely’s sole authorised distributor, will handle sales and after-sales service.
The Group Managing Director, Irfan Sheikh, said the company would focus on localisation and build a charging network to support its long-term presence.
The launch includes three models: the EX2 compact electric hatchback, the EX5 electric SUV, and the Starray EM-i with range-extended EV technology.
Bestway said it has invested over $350 million in Pakistan and created 20,000 jobs in the last three years.
Geely Auto International Corporation Vice President Evin Ye said Pakistan was Geely’s first market in South Asia, adding that it was the only automaker with a low-orbit satellite network.
