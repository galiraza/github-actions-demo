# Israeli strike on Gaza university kills 3; another 4 Palestinians killed in other attacks

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 25 Oct 2026 21:13:06 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2039736/israeli-strike-on-gaza-university-kills-3-another-4-palestinians-killed-in-other-attacks
- Collected: 2026-10-25 17:00:58 UTC

---

An Israeli strike on a university in Gaza City killed three people on Sunday, staff and a local hospital said, while the civil defence agency reported four others killed elsewhere in the territory.
Reached for comment, the Israeli military said it was looking into the reports.
Al Shifa Hospital said it received three bodies and treated eight people who were wounded in the Gaza City drone strike, with two sources at the Islamic University saying those killed were administrative staff and the injured were students.
Gaza’s education ministry said in a statement that the students had been present for classes.
It went on to condemn the strike and said: “The global silence regarding attacks on students and universities serves as encouragement for the continuation of these crimes.”
“This strike occurs at a time when the higher education sector is facing total annihilation, with 95 per cent of the buildings belonging to academic institutions having been damaged as a result of the war,” it continued.
“This heinous assault is part of a continuous series of attacks on education; over the past six days, 10 students and educators have been killed by the occupation’s fire.”
Gaza civil defence spokesman Mahmoud Bassal said four other people were killed on Sunday, two in an airstrike in the southern city of Khan Younis, one in central Deir al Balah and one in the north of the Strip.
Earlier this week, Israeli strikes and gunfire killed at least four people in the Gaza Strip on Thursday.
A truce has been in effect in Gaza for over a year, but has failed to completely halt Israeli attacks in the territory. At least 1,500 Palestinians have been killed in Gaza in Israeli airstrikes since the ceasefire, according to the territory’s health officials, while Israel says four soldiers have been killed.
Palestinian authorities say more than 74,000 people have been killed in Israel’s assault on Gaza.
