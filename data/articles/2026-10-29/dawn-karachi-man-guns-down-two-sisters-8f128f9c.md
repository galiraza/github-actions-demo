# Karachi man guns down two sisters for ‘using TikTok’

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 29 Oct 2026 09:38:09 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2040548/karachi-man-guns-down-two-sisters-for-using-tiktok
- Collected: 2026-10-29 06:44:02 UTC

---

KARACHI: Two young sisters were allegedly shot dead by their brother over their use of social media in Drigh Road, within the jurisdiction of Shah Faisal Colony police, on Wednesday, police said.
Korangi SSP Fida Husain Janwari told Dawn that Sobia, 25, and Hira, 20, were gunned down inside their home in New Rehmanabad by their brother, Rehan.
The senior officer said the initial investigation suggested that the suspect had warned his sisters against using TikTok, which appeared to be a possible motive behind the double murder.
He said the suspect had fled the crime scene and efforts were on to ensure his arrest.
The SSP added that the alleged killer had a criminal record and had been arrested multiple times and sent to jail in connection with criminal cases.
The bodies were shifted to the Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC) for medico-legal formalities.
Firing on under-construction building leaves watchman dead
A watchman was gunned down and two labourers were wounded in an armed attack on an under-construction building in the Garden area.
The Soldier Bazaar police said two armed men riding a motorcycle resorted to indiscriminate firing on the under-construction Jumani Square located on Nishter Road near Alibhai Auditorium. As a result, three persons sustained critical injuries and were taken to the Civil Hospital Karachi where Aslam, 30, was pronounced dead on arrival.
The police said the victim was watchman of the building. The injured labourers were identified as Shahid Ali, 25, and Shakir Ali, 28.
The police in a statement said the incident appeared to be related to extortion.
