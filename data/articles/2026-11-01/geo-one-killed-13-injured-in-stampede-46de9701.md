# One killed, 13 injured in stampede at TikToker's mehndi ceremony in Sialkot

---

Published November 01, 2026
At least one person was killed and 13 others injured after a stampede broke out during a TikToker's mehndi ceremony in the Sialkot district of Punjab.
According to rescue sources, the mehndi ceremony was held at a stadium on Daska Road, where the organisers had extended an open invitation to the public.
A prize draw announced for the event reportedly attracted a large crowd, with a considerable number of people gathering outside the stadium.
Meanwhile, police have registered a case in connection with the death during the mehndi ceremony.
According to police, eight nominated and 45 unidentified individuals have been named in the case.
Police said six suspects had been arrested, while efforts were under way to apprehend the prime suspect, who remains at large.
