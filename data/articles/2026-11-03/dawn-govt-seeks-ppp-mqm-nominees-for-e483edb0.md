# Govt seeks PPP, MQM nominees for electoral reforms panel

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 03 Nov 2026 07:52:45 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2041753/govt-seeks-ppp-mqm-nominees-for-electoral-reforms-panel
- Collected: 2026-11-03 07:32:03 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: Taking ownership of an initiative to bring all parliamentary parties, including the opposition, to the negotiating table on electoral reforms, the government on Monday formally approached its allies to join the proposed Parliamentary Committee on Electoral Reforms (PCER).
The government contacted its key allies, the PPP and the MQM, seeking nominations for their representatives on the proposed committee.
The PPP has submitted the names of its legislators for the PCER. However, the names have not yet been made public.
Federal Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Tariq Fazal Chaudhry wrote separate letters to the chief whips of the PPP and MQM in the National Assembly, Ijaz Jakhrani and Aminul Haq, respectively, seeking nominations for the committee.
Talking to Dawn, Mr Chaudhry said National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq had received the names from the PPP. However, he said the government would announce the names of all committee members at a later stage.
He said the ruling PML-N would have nine members, while the PPP would have five and the MQM one.
Explaining the formula for representation of parliamentary parties on the committee, the minister said one member would be allocated for every 16 members of the National Assembly.
He said that, under the agreed criteria, smaller parties allied with the ruling coalition did not qualify for representation on the committee. The PML-N had therefore decided to allocate one seat each to its smaller allies, including the Balochistan Awami Party (BAP) and Pakistan Muslim League-Zia (PML-Z), from its own quota of nine seats.
Asked whether the government would also directly approach the opposition to seek nominations for the committee, Mr Chaudhry said the PPP had already contacted opposition parties and that they were now required to submit the names of their representatives directly to the National Assembly speaker.
The parliamentary affairs minister clarified that the initiative to form the committee had been taken by Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq, not the PPP.
He said he would begin meeting parliamentary leaders of all parties from today (Tuesday) to obtain nominations for the electoral reforms committee.
Mr Chaudhry said there was no reason to believe that the PML-N-led government would not complete its five-year constitutional term. He added that the PPP had met opposition leaders at the initiative of the National Assembly speaker.
“The PPP can only meet opposition leaders as a political move, but the party has no mandate to do anything else,” he said. Responding to a question, Mr Chaudhry acknowledged that the PPP was creating difficulties for the government in legislative business.
“The PPP is pinching us in making legislation, but the party cannot keep itself away from the system,” he said.
He insisted that the opposition should join the parliamentary committee, saying the initiative would open the door to consultations on other important national issues.
