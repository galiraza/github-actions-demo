# Lawyer says Shah Mahmood Qureshi taken to hospital days after suffering fall

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 10 Nov 2026 18:03:42 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2043526/lawyer-says-shah-mahmood-qureshi-taken-to-hospital-days-after-suffering-fall
- Collected: 2026-11-10 15:23:20 UTC

---

LAHORE: The lawyer representing Shah Mahmood Qureshi said on Tuesday that the senior PTI leader, who is currently incarcerated at Kot Lakhpat jail, was taken to the Punjab Institute of Cardiology (PIC) days after suffering a fall.
Qureshi has been indicted in multiple cases pertaining to the May 9, 2023 riots and has remained behind bars since August 2023. In July 2024, the PTI leader was indicted by a Lahore anti-terrorism court in a case registered by the Shadman police over allegedly attacking and burning the police station.
Speaking to Dawn, his counsel Rana Mudassar Umar said: “[Qureshi] was taken to PIC today as he fainted and suffered a fall a few days ago. We had submitted an application in the anti-terrorism court; however, due to holidays, the issue was delayed.”
He said that Qureshi was being held under observation for 24 hours, “after which it will be decided whether he has to be admitted to the hospital”.
Last week, Qureshi’s daughter, Meher Bano, in a post on the social media platform X, said that she had learnt about her father’s fall from “family after they met him in Kot Lakhpat jail”.
“Three-and-half years ago a hale and hearty lion heart was sent to jail on trumped up charges … For over two years, he has been in Kot Lakhpat jail now facing ludicrous and false charges relating to May 9. He has been acquitted multiple times but [remains] behind bars due to the government’s malafide,” she alleged.
Qureshi’s son, Zain, said on Wednesday that he met his father in jail, where he mentioned that he had collapsed while walking within his jail barracks.
“I request the prison authorities to immediately arrange for a CT scan so that his health condition can be thoroughly assessed. Furthermore, he should be taken to a qualified dentist for the treatment of a cracked tooth,” he said on X.
On Nov 6, a meeting of the PTI core committee also expressed concern over Qureshi’s “fall and the resulting impact on his health”.
The committee called for the relevant courts, the government and the prison administration to immediately transfer Qureshi to a hospital and ensure his complete medical examination and necessary treatment.
In December last year, Qureshi, who is also a former foreign minister, underwent a procedure to remove his gallbladder at the Pakistan Kidney and Liver Institute. Prior to that, he was taken to the PIC in May after complaining of heart pain.
