# Mum's the word as PTI lawyers meet Imran at Adiala jail following IHC orders

- Published: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 19:48:52 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-11 14:53:05 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: Lawyers representing PTI met party founder and former prime minister Imran Khan on Wednesday, a day after the Islamabad High Court (IHC) issued orders allowing the meeting.
On Tuesday, the IHC, while hearing appeals filed by Imran and his wife, Bushra Bibi, against their convictions in the £190 million reference, directed authorities to facilitate meetings between Imran and his legal team.
PTI lawyer Awais Younus Chaudhry, speaking to Dawn, said Barrister Salman Safdar and Advocate Latif Khosa held a meeting with Imran.
“As per orders of the court, the meeting was held to discuss under-trial cases,” Chaudhry said.
The lawyers remained at Adiala for four hours and left without speaking to the media or PTI lawyers, Chaudhry said.
“As their cell phones are switched off, it’s too early to say whether they were allowed to meet Imran together or separately and for how long,” he said.
The meeting took place hours after the ex-premier was again taken to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (Pims) for his eye ailment.
According to the hospital, Imran was brought to Pims last night for a medical check-up and follow-up eye treatment. It added that his vital parameters were “normal”, he was clinically “stable” and his general physical examination was “normal”.
Imran has been in prison since August 5, 2023, while his wife was arrested on January 17, 2025, in a separate case in which the couple were sentenced.
The IHC has allowed Imran twice-a-week meetings — on Tuesdays and Thursdays — with his family, lawyers and other associates. Despite multiple court directives, Imran’s meetings with his family and party leaders have been largely restricted.
On Nov 2, Safdar met Imran after a gap of seven months for less than an hour. When he had met Imran in April, months after the ex-premier’s eye condition had surfaced, the Supreme Court had designated Safdar as amicus curiae. However, on Nov 2, he met Imran as his lawyer.
PTI Central Information Secretary Sheikh Waqas Akram, speaking to Dawn after the Nov 2 meeting, said that no political issues were discussed as Safdar spoke about the cases and asked about future strategy.
When asked about Imran’s health, Akram replied that although health issues were discussed, the PTI would only speak about the legal cases.
