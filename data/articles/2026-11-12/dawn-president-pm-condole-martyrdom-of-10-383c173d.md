# President, PM condole martyrdom of 10 policemen in Kohat, vow to continue fight against terrorism

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 12 Nov 2026 10:27:54 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2044005/president-pm-condole-martyrdom-of-10-policemen-in-kohat-vow-to-continue-fight-against-terrorism
- Collected: 2026-11-12 07:56:16 UTC

---

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Thursday expressed grief over the martyrdom of 10 police personnel in a terrorist attack on a police team in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s Kohat district.
Eighteen terrorists were killed and 10 police personnel were martyred on Wednesday when a police team came under attack while returning from an operation against elements involved in illegal gold mining.
In his message, the president said the sacrifices of the KP police in the fight against terrorism were a source of pride for the entire nation.
The president extended his heartfelt condolences to the families of the martyred personnel and expressed solidarity with them.
He said cowardly terrorist attacks could not weaken the nation’s resolve, which remained unwavering in its commitment to eliminating terrorism from the country.
President Zardari said police personnel and other law enforcement agencies were making sacrifices to protect the lives and property of the people.
He also prayed for the speedy recovery of those injured in the attack, saying the sacrifices of those who laid down their lives for the peace, stability and security of the country would always be remembered.
Prime Minister Shehbaz reiterated the government’s resolve to eradicate terrorism from the country.
The prime minister, who is currently in Turkiye on a three-day official visit to attend the COP31 summit, strongly condemned the attack and paid tribute to the martyred KP police personnel.
Calling them national heroes, he said the brave officers and personnel of the provincial police force had set an example of devotion to duty and courage by laying down their lives for the peace and security of the country.
The premier also extended his heartfelt condolences to the families of the martyrs and prayed for the elevation of the ranks of the departed souls and strength for the bereaved families.
He said the killing of 18 terrorists during the operation demonstrated the professional capabilities, courage and unwavering resolve of the KP Police in the fight against terrorism.
He also described the “nexus between criminal elements and Fitna al-Khawarij” as a nefarious attempt to spread unrest in the country, vowing that such elements would not be allowed to succeed in their designs under any circumstances.
Fitna-al-Khawarij is a term the state uses for terrorists belonging to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan.
PM Shehbaz also reiterated that no compromise would be made on Pakistan’s peace and stability.
The prime minister further said the courage and steadfastness shown by police personnel and other law enforcement agencies in the fight against terrorism were commendable, adding that the entire nation stood shoulder to shoulder with the country’s security forces.
In a statement released yesterday, the KP Police said police personnel were conducting a joint operation with the local civil administration in the Darabukach locality near Kohat city.
“When the police party was returning after completing the operation, Fitna-al-Khawarij attacked the police party at the behest of criminal elements,” the statement read.
“Police personnel fought the attackers with great bravery and courage, as a result of which 18 khawarij were sent to hell,” it noted, adding that 10 personnel of KP Police “embraced martyrdom while fighting bravely”.
KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has sought a detailed report on the incident.
According to images shared by KP Police on their X account, the martyred cops included Deputy Superintendent of Police Haidar Zaman.
KP has been facing security challenges for long.
According to Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies, the country’s security environment continued to show considerable volatility from Oct 30 to Nov 5, with renewed terrorist activity in parts of KP and the former tribal districts, alongside intensified counterterrorism operations and changing patterns of militant violence.
It is worth mentioning that KP has been under governor’s rule and emergency since Oct 11, when the federal government took the measures, citing the PTI-led KP government’s “deliberate disengagement” from the counterterrorism fight.
