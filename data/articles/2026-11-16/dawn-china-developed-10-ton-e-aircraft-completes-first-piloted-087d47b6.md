# China-developed 10-ton e-aircraft completes first piloted flight

- Published: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 09:43:30 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-16 07:36:50 UTC

SHANGHAI: China’s independently developed 10-ton electric commercial aircraft CE-25A completed its maiden piloted flight on Saturday, according to the Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (COMAC).
The aircraft carried two pilots and one flight-test engineer during the flight in Binzhou, East China’s Shandong province. The aircraft operated normally, and all performance indicators met expectations, COMAC said.
The CE-25A is COMAC’s first electric commercial aircraft. Designed mainly for intercity commercial flights, it can carry 19 passengers and has a maximum takeoff weight of 10 metric tons. It has a cruising speed of 300 km per hour, a cruising altitude of 2,400 metres and a range of 400 km. The aircraft completed its first unmanned autonomous flight on Feb 2 this year.
Developed jointly by COMAC, Chinese battery maker CATL, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and other partners, the CE-25A has completed 19 unmanned autonomous flight validations.
Instead of conventional turbofan or turboprop engines, the CE-25A uses a distributed electric propulsion system consisting of 10 electric ducted fans mounted along the top of its wings. This layout aims to reduce noise and enable takeoffs and landings on shorter runways.
The Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC) has formally accepted COMAC’s type certificate application under transport-category regulations, initiating the inspection process required before commercial passenger service can begin.
