# Jay Shah re-elected unopposed as ICC chairman for second term

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 11:43:54 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2044904/jay-shah-re-elected-unopposed-as-icc-chairman-for-second-term
- Collected: 2026-11-16 07:36:50 UTC

---

Jay Shah was re-elected unopposed as chairman of the International Cricket Council (ICC) for a second two-year term on Monday, extending his leadership of the governing body as cricket prepares for its return to the Olympic Games at Los Angeles in 2028.
The decision was announced following the ICC Board meetings in Bali, Indonesia, where directors proposed Shah’s reappointment.
The 38-year-old first took charge of the ICC on Dec 1, 2024, after serving as secretary of the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI). He became the youngest person to head the global governing body at the age of 35. He is the son of India’s Home Minister Amit Shah.
Shah’s second term will cover an important period for international cricket, with the sport preparing for its Olympic return after more than a century. Cricket will feature at the 2028 Los Angeles Games, with the T20 format selected for the competition.
His first tenure saw the ICC oversee several major developments in the international game, including the 2025 Champions Trophy, which was staged under a hybrid model after India declined to travel to Pakistan. The arrangement saw India’s matches played in Dubai, while Pakistan hosted the remainder of the tournament.
The coming years will also include the 2027 Men’s ODI World Cup, to be jointly hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia, as well as further expansion of the ICC’s international competitions.
In a statement after his re-election, Shah thanked the board members for their continued confidence and said the sport was entering an important phase of its development.
“Cricket’s return to the Olympic Games at LA28 presents an extraordinary opportunity to introduce our game to new audiences and inspire a new generation of players and fans,” he said.
He added that the ICC’s responsibilities extended beyond organising major tournaments, stressing the need to build a sustainable future for the game.
The governing body said its priorities would include strengthening international cricket, expanding opportunities for Associate Members, supporting the growth of women’s cricket and protecting the integrity of the sport.
England and Wales Cricket Board chairman Richard Thompson welcomed Shah’s reappointment, saying the international calendar and the competitiveness of bilateral cricket would remain important considerations in the years ahead.
ICC deputy chairman Tavengwa Mukuhlani also backed the decision, pointing to the opportunities the 2027 World Cup could create for cricket across Africa.
Shah’s second term will run through a period in which the ICC will have to balance the demands of its member boards with the commercial growth of the game and its expanding international programme.
