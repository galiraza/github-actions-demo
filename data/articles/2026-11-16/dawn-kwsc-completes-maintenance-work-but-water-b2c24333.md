# KWSC completes maintenance work but water shortage persists in Karachi

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 10:22:25 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2044785/kwsc-completes-maintenance-work-but-water-shortage-persists-in-karachi
- Collected: 2026-11-16 07:36:50 UTC

---

KARACHI: Although the Karachi Water and Sewerage Corporation (KWSC) claimed to have restored water supply across the city after completing its annual maintenance work at the Dhabeji pumping station, many areas were still without water for a third consecutive day on Sunday.
The utility had announced that the maintenance work that began on Nov 13 would cause a shortfall of around 130 million gallons per day (MGD) in water supply.
On Sunday, it said in a statement that various technical and maintenance works, including the installation of valves at key locations and repairs to water pipelines, were completed during the annual maintenance operation.
It added all 21 pumps at the Dhabeji pumping station were now operational and water supply across the city had been restored.
It added that water supply to some affected areas, including Landhi, Korangi, Malir and Shah Faisal towns, was being gradually brought back to normal. However, the statement did not mention any time frame for when the supply would be normalised.
According to a KWSC spokesperson, during the annual maintenance work, repairs to water pipelines were carried out at Dhabeji, Pipri, Shahrah-i-Bhutto and the Haleji conduits to improve, stabilise and enhance the efficiency of the water supply system.
The spokesperson added that a new 60-inch-diameter valve had been installed at the Dhabeji pumping station during the maintenance work.
Similarly, necessary repairs were carried out at all pump houses at the Pipri pumping station. While approximately 150-foot-long water pipeline along the alignment of Shahrah-i-Bhutto was replaced, a leakage in the Haleji conduit was also repaired.
