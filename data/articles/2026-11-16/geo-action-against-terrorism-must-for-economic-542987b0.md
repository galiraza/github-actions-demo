# Action against terrorism must for economic cooperation with Afghanistan, Pakistan tells ECO

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar on Monday stressed that action by the Afghan Taliban regime against terrorism and ensuring a stable and secure environment were "absolutely essential" for sustained regional economic cooperation.
"Pakistan desires peaceful, prosperous and stable Afghanistan, fully integrated within the ECO framework," he said while chairing the 30th meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Council of Foreign Ministers in Islamabad.
The DPM said Afghanistan at peace with itself and all its neighbours would not only benefit its own people but also contribute to peace and prosperity across the wider region.
DPM Dar called on the ECO member states to strengthen regional connectivity amid prevailing global uncertainty and rapid technological advancements.
He reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to the principles, objectives and vision outlined in the ECO Charter, saying Pakistan, as a founding member of the organisation, attached particular importance to regional cooperation.
"Our guiding principles are equality of sovereignty, mutual respect, non-interference and cooperation based on mutual benefit," DPM Dar said.
Highlighting the region's geographical significance, he said it occupied a unique position at the crossroads of South Asia, Central Asia and West Asia.
He noted that the region brought together major markets, abundant natural resources and a historical foundation for trade.
The DPM called for efforts to transform this geographical advantage into stronger economic connectivity and shared prosperity.
He also expressed Pakistan's commitment to making its ECO chairmanship productive and meaningful, saying the country was proud to assume the organisation’s presidency for 2026-2027.
Earlier, the Senior Officials Meeting (SOM) of the ECO was held, under the chairmanship of Foreign Secretary Ambassador Asim Iftikhar Ahmad.
According to the Foreign Office, Senior officials of ECO member states, representatives of the ECO secretariat, and delegates from ECO specialised agencies and regional institutions attended the meeting.
The senior officials deliberated on the agenda and preparations for the 30th meeting of the ECO Council of Ministers (COM). They finalised the agenda and the draft Islamabad Communiqué for consideration by the ministers, the FO said in a statement.
The meeting also reviewed the activities undertaken by the ECO secretariat during the year, including reports on the organisation's ongoing programmes and initiatives, outcomes of sectoral ministerial meetings, and the work of subsidiary bodies and specialised institutions.
The participants also exchanged views on the administrative and financial matters of the organisation and discussed ways to enhance the its effectiveness.
The ECO senior officials' meeting served as a key preparatory forum for the COM, providing an opportunity for member states to review progress, deliberate on matters of mutual interest, and build consensus on proposals and draft outcome documents ahead of ministerial-level deliberations.
Earlier, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and other foreign dignitaries arrived in Islamabad as Pakistan hosts the 30th ECO Council of Ministers meeting.
In a statement, the Foreign Office said that the Iranian foreign minister was received at the airport by Additional Foreign Secretary Muhammad Tariq Wazir and other officers of the foreign ministry.
Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar on Sunday hosted a welcome dinner in honour of the foreign ministers and heads of delegations of ECO member states.
Addressing the gathering, DPM Dar highlighted the close bonds among ECO member states, reaffirming Pakistan's commitment to strengthening regional cooperation and advancing shared aspirations for peace, progress and prosperity.
Delegates from friendly countries and regional organisations began arriving on Saturday for the huddle as diplomacy took centre stage in Pakistan.
Tahsin Ertugruloglu, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus, reached the federal capital on Saturday to participate in the meeting.
A day later, Turkiye's Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs and head of the Turkish delegation, Musa Kulaklikaya, arrived in the federal capital.
Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Deputy Secretary General Ahmad Saidmurodzoda also landed in Islamabad to attend the high-level meeting, the FO said.
Among those arriving were Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) Secretary General Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed, Azerbaijan’s Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs Elnur Mammadov and Kazakhstan’s Deputy FM Arman Issagaliyev.
DPM Dar later held a meeting with ECO Secretary General Dr Asad Majeed Khan on the sidelines of the huddle and underscored the importance of strengthening regional cooperation, connectivity and economic integration within the ECO region.
He highlighted Pakistan's priorities during its chairmanship under the theme "Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO" and reaffirmed Pakistan’s commitment to hosting the ECO Summit in 2027.
The secretary general appreciated Pakistan's excellent arrangements, hospitality and coordination for the 30th ECO-COM and congratulated Pakistan on its upcoming chairmanship and hosting of the ECO-COM and ECO Summit in 2027.
Separately, DPM Dar also held a meeting with the OIC secretary general and discussed matters of mutual interest.
Welcoming the secretary general on his first visit to Islamabad since assuming office, the DPM reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to strengthening the OIC as a unified and effective voice of the Muslim Ummah.
He assured the secretary general of Pakistan's continued support and cooperation in advancing the OIC's objectives.
The secretary-general appreciated Pakistan’s diplomatic efforts during the Iran-US conflict to promote peace and stability in the region.
He also commended Islamabad's active role in the OIC, including its contributions through Comstech to promoting scientific and technological cooperation among the member states.
