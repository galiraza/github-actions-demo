Published November 16, 2026
ISLAMABAD: A local court in Islamabad on Monday handed over the wife of a senior police officer and a religious teacher (Qari) to police on a four-day physical remand in connection with the death of a 13-year-old domestic worker, police said.
Judicial Magistrate Naima Iffat granted the four-day remand after the suspects were produced before the court.
The domestic worker, identified as Fatima, was allegedly tortured to death at the residence of a senior police officer in the Police Lines Headquarters.
According to police sources, the girl was shifted to Quaid-e-Azam Hospital in critical condition but succumbed to her injuries during treatment, The News reported on Monday.
A murder case has been registered at the Shams Colony police station on the complaint of her father, Asghar.
Senior police officer Muhammad bin Ashraf told Geo News that the girl had been living with the family for a considerable period. He said the family itself had contacted the police after the incident.
Meanwhile, it was learnt that complaints had reportedly been made against the senior cop Muhammad bin Ashraf during his tenure as district police officer (DPO) of Chakwal.
According to the reports, the officer had sent a female constable to the DPO House to look after his children. After the constable refused, the officer's wife allegedly had her transferred to Kallar Kahar police station as punishment.
Ex-Punjab IGP Rao Sardar visited the police station around midnight and was surprised to find the female constable posted there. He later took notice of the matter after asking her about the circumstances of her transfer.
