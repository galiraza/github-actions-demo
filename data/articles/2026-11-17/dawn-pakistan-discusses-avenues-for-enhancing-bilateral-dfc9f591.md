# Pakistan discusses avenues for enhancing bilateral defence cooperation with UK, Turkiye

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 17 Nov 2026 18:16:41 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045178/pakistan-discusses-avenues-for-enhancing-bilateral-defence-cooperation-with-uk-turkiye
- Collected: 2026-11-17 16:38:58 UTC

---

Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir discussed avenues for enhancing defence cooperation with both the United Kingdom and Turkiye, in separate meetings with the British army chief and the Turkish air chief in Rawalpindi, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said on Tuesday.
According to the military’s media wing, a high-level delegation from the UK, led by Chief of General Staff General Sir Charles Roland Vincent Walker, called on the CDF at General Headquarters (GHQ).
The visiting official was presented with a Guard of Honour by a smartly turned-out Pakistan Army contingent upon arrival.
“Matters of mutual interest, the prevailing regional security environment and avenues for enhancing bilateral defence and security cooperation” were discussed in the meeting, the ISPR said. The CDF underscored Pakistan’s commitment to further expanding defence ties with the UK.
“Both sides expressed satisfaction over the longstanding, cordial relations between Pakistan and the United Kingdom and reaffirmed their commitment to further strengthening bilateral military-to-military cooperation,” it added.
The visiting dignitary, in turn, appreciated the “professionalism and operational preparedness” of Pakistan’s armed forces, acknowledging their contributions towards peace and stability in the region.
According to the statement, the visit reflected the shared resolve of Pakistan and the UK to deepen defence cooperation and strengthen bilateral military ties.
Separately, Commander of the Turkish Air Force General Rafet Dalkıran also called on the CDF at GHQ, where matters of mutual interest, regional security dynamics and avenues to enhance bilateral defence cooperation were discussed, the military said.
CDF Munir highlighted the “deep-rooted historical, cultural and strategic ties between the two countries “and appreciated the strong defence partnership between their armed forces.
The visiting dignitary reciprocated the strengthening of bilateral defence ties, appreciated the professionalism of the Pakistan Armed Forces and acknowledged the country’s contributions to regional peace and stability, the ISPR added.
Earlier this month, Pakistan and the UK also agreed to strengthen security cooperation when Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi held delegation-level talks with visiting British Security Minister Dan Jarvis in Islamabad, focusing on counterterrorism, narcotics trafficking, cybercrime and illegal immigration.
They also held their annual bilateral dialogue on counter-terrorism in Islamabad, in which they reviewed ongoing bilateral and multilateral counter-terrorism cooperation and reaffirmed their shared commitment to combating terrorism in all its forms.
Pakistan and Turkiye, meanwhile, are co-signatories to multiple defence agreements, including the Makkah Defence Pact signed in August this year with Saudi Arabia, as well as the multilateral Badr defence cooperation pact in October alongside Qatar and Indonesia.
In addition, Pakistan and Turkiye are part of the Regional Four (R4) Group along with Saudi Arabia and Egypt, which emerged in March and has met several times since then.
