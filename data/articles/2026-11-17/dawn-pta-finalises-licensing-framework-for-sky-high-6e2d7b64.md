# PTA finalises licensing framework for 'sky-high' internet, telecommunication services

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 17 Nov 2026 19:50:29 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045196/pta-finalises-licensing-framework-for-sky-high-internet-telecommunication-services
- Collected: 2026-11-17 16:38:58 UTC

---

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) said on Tuesday that it had finalised a licensing framework for satellite-based in-flight telecommunication services, “paving way for internet connectivity and other digital services aboard aircraft”.
“Under the framework, licensed in-flight telecommunication services providers would be able to use satellites to offer connectivity to Pakistani-registered aircraft, subject to registration with the Pakistan Space Activities Regulatory Board and compliance with PTA’s licensing requirements,” the authority said in a press release on Tuesday.
It added that transit aircraft passing through Pakistani airspace would require a no-objection certificate from the authority, subject to applicable compliance requirements.
It said that the introduction of in-flight telecommunication services would “enhance passenger experience through seamless in-flight internet connectivity, enabling access to digital services, entertainment, personal communication and work-related activities”.
It added that the initiative would further strengthen the authority’s efforts to expand digital connectivity in Pakistan by “extending telecommunication services beyond terrestrial networks and enabling seamless connectivity during air travel”.
