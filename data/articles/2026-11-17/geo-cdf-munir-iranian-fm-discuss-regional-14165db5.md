Published November 17, 2026
A high-level Iranian delegation led by Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi called on Chief of Army Staff and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir in Rawalpindi, the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said in a statement on Tuesday.
According to the military's media wing, matters of mutual interest, regional security dynamics and evolving geopolitical developments were discussed in the meeting.
Both sides exchanged views on exploring avenues for addressing common security challenges, it added.
CDF Munir underscored the importance of sustained engagement, mutual trust and close coordination between the two brotherly countries for promoting regional peace and stability.
The Iranian foreign minister appreciated Pakistan's constructive role in promoting regional peace and stability and expressed his country's desire to further strengthen bilateral relations.
Iranian FM Araghchi visited Islamabad to attend a meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO), a regional grouping of countries from Central Asia, South Asia and the Middle East.
Addressing the meeting a day earlier, Araghchi called for stronger regional cooperation through the ECO, emphasising trade, transport and energy as strategic priorities.
He praised DPM Dar's leadership within the organisation and Pakistan's warm hospitality.
He thanked countries that had condemned US and Israeli aggression against Iran's territorial integrity and expressed solidarity with the Iranian people.
Araghchi called for the ECO to become an effective platform for collective action, stronger cooperation and a shared regional strategy.
The Iranian foreign minister welcomed the establishment of a technical negotiating committee to reform the ECO Trade Agreement, urging member states to participate in negotiations aimed at developing a new and effective trade agreement.
Turning to regional security, Araghchi reaffirmed Iran's commitment to the United Nations Charter and the principles of international law.
He said that Tehran had remained committed to diplomatic engagement and the peaceful resolution of disputes over the past two years.
However, he accused the US of pursuing aggression, threats, targeted killings, economic pressure and a blockade against Iran, describing these actions as "blatant violations" of international law and the UN Charter.
The Iranian top diplomat said that regional countries should ensure that their territories, airspace and installations were not used for attacks against one another.
He maintained that Iran's defensive measures were directed only against the targets and military bases of those responsible for the aggression.
He called for a collective regional strategy based on non-aggression and regional dialogue to ensure security.
He also invited foreign ministers of ECO member states to attend the 18th summit in Tehran.
