# ‘Have the courage to listen to the debate you started’: Bilawal sticks to his guns after speaking his ‘truth’

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 20:54:42 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045449/have-the-courage-to-listen-to-the-debate-you-started-bilawal-sticks-to-his-guns-after-speaking-his-truth
- Collected: 2026-11-18 16:08:16 UTC

---

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari on Wednesday maintained that his recent remarks on the country’s political situation were the “truth”, urging government representatives to have the “courage” to listen to his point of view.
Speaking in an interview on Hum News programme ‘Faisla Aap Ka’, Bilawal reiterated his stance from his Tuesday’s speech, cautioning against new provinces and calling for a “political reset” so public issues could be addressed.
On the fact that his PPP was part of the ruling coalition, Bilawal contended that this was the exact reason to “listen” to him as he was a “major stakeholder”.
“These are not reasons to discount what I am saying […] I am telling you that this is the situation; these are my proposals to rectify it. You may disagree with my suggestions, but at least listen. You should have the courage to listen to the truth.”
The Bhutto scion added, “Because I am a stakeholder, I am saying: come to your senses.”
Bilawal emphasised that he spoke in the context of the US-Iran war, which has impacted economies around the world due to rising energy costs.
“Elites do not have the idea of what kind of difficulties Pakistanis are facing,” he said, highlighting the need for politicians to set aside their partisan political perspectives and think about how to “fix the system”.
The PPP chairman asserted it was his right to provide his perspective on what shape a “reset” should take if someone was suggesting that a reset was needed, indirectly referring to Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi’s July remarks.
