# 'He who claims equity must come with clean hands': Asif hits back at Bilawal

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 13:07:33 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045397/he-who-claims-equity-must-come-with-clean-hands-asif-hits-back-at-bilawal
- Collected: 2026-11-18 08:10:50 UTC

---

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif joined others from the ruling PML-N on Wednesday in responding to PPP Chairperson Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari following the latter’s hard-hitting diatribe over several issues.
“He who claims equity must come with clean hands,” Asif reminded Bilawal in a post on X, as the wrangling between coalition partners PML-N and PPP appeared to deepen.
The exchange has particularly heated up after Bilawal launched a no-holds-barred speech the previous day that covered the question of new provinces. The Bhutto scion also expressed support for several opposition demands and questioned past decisions and actions taken by the ruling coalition.
Bilawal referred to Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi’s recommendation of holding a referendum to decide on the creation of new administrative units and said: “In our history, a referendum has always been carried out for an undemocratic act and never a democratic one.”
The PPP chairperson also remarked that the governance system had not collapsed, but was rather “made to collapse” — seemingly reacting to Naqvi’s statement in July that the current “system” of governance had “collapsed” and needed a “reset”.
Asif began his response to Bilawal by addressing this matter.
He said: “No system can reset without public support. If you are talking about a system collapse, then all of us, as people connected to this system, must also take stock of our responsibilities.
The defence minister also appeared to throw his weight behind the proposal for holding a referendum on new province as he said that there would surely be a debate on “system reset” in Parliament, but the public too could not be “deprived of its fundamental right”.
“The pinnacle of legitimacy would be reached if the public opinion is also taken into account along with that of Parliament.”
Seemingly reacting to Bilawal’s support for the release of incarcerated former prime minister and PTI founder Imran Khan and other demands by the opposition, Asif added: “Holding politicians accountable in this country is nothing new, and no politician exempt from accountability.”
