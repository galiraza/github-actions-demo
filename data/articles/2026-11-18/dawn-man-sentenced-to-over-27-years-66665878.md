# Man sentenced to over 27 years in US for plot to assassinate Trump

A US judge has sentenced a Pakistani man, found guilty of plotting to assassinate President Donald Trump and other prominent US politicians, to more than 27 years in prison, the Department of Justice (DOJ) said on Tuesday.
“Earlier today, in federal court in Brooklyn, Asif Merchant, 48, of Pakistan, was sentenced by US District Judge Eric R Komitee to 330 months in prison for attempting to commit an act of terrorism transcending national boundaries and murder for hire,” the DOJ said in a statement.
Prosecutors said Merchant tried to recruit people in the US in a plan targeting Trump and others in retaliation for Washington’s assassination of Iranian military commander Qassem Soleimani in 2020, when Trump was in his first term.
Targets in the 2024 plot also included then-President Joe Biden and Nikki Haley, who ran against Trump that year for the Republican presidential nomination, federal prosecutors said.
A federal jury in March convicted Merchant of travelling to the US to arrange for political assassinations on American soil, according to the Justice Department.
That trial in the New York City borough of Brooklyn started in late February.
Merchant testified at his trial that he joined the plot with Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps but did so unwillingly, to protect his family in Tehran.
Merchant said he was never ordered to kill a specific person but that his Iranian handler named three people in the course of conversations in the Iranian capital.
Law enforcement thwarted the plan before any attack occurred. A person Merchant contacted in April 2024 to help with the plot reported his activities and became a confidential informant, the DOJ said. Merchant was arrested and pleaded not guilty that year.
Tehran has denied that it targeted or plotted to attack Trump or other US officials.
US-Israeli attacks on Iran since February 28 have killed thousands and displaced millions. Many top Iranian leaders, including then-supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, have been assassinated, too.
