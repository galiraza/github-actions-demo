# Nawaz hails his govt’s ‘serious’ work on AJK development as PML-N charts 100-day plan

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 19:49:54 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045412/nawaz-hails-his-govts-serious-work-on-ajk-development-as-pml-n-charts-100-day-plan
- Collected: 2026-11-18 16:08:16 UTC

---

The PML-N said its top leadership, including Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, discussed development and reconstruction in AJK with the region’s president and premier, both of whom belong to the PML-N.
Addressing the meeting, Nawaz hailed the efforts of his government in the development of AJK, particularly the formulation of a 100-day programme to develop the region.
“This is the first time, I think, that a party’s government and the party’s leadership are on the same page regarding participating in AJK’s development with such seriousness,” he said, adding, “I have never seen such enthusiasm before.”
He highlighted Amir Muqam, Rana Sanaullah, Chaudhry Tariq and Khawaja Saad Rafique’s efforts in particular.
He also expressed appreciation for the contributions of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz, who he said had provided green buses for Muzaffarabad and Mirpur as well as clinics on wheels and laptops. “This is a very good spirit and we should appreciate it,” he said.
In addition, he highlighted that PM Shehbaz had also agreed to allocate resources to the programme, calling it a “very good thing” that there was so much passion surrounding the decisions to contribute to the region’s development.
Nawaz regretted that there were previous governments in AJK that had “not cared” about it at all and forgotten its promises to the public. He highlighted the contrast between then and the current time.
He added that, now that Gillani had become the prime minister in the region, he felt that he himself had become prime minister.
“The zeal that is in my heart, I see that very same zeal inside his heart,” he said, adding that he would continue to visit AJK from time to time and hold meetings to discuss development.
Nawaz also said that while he had been premier, and Raja Farooq Haider had been AJK PM, “our sole focus used to be on development”. In particular, he noted that one project that they wanted to launch was for a motorway from Muzaffarabad to Mirpur.
“With this, your entire Azad Kashmir interior would have opened up […] If there ever was a finest project in the history of Azad Kashmir that was ever conceived, it was that project,” the PML-N leader said, requesting PM Shehbaz to now give that project “top priority”.
He also lamented that motorways linking major cities were unable to be completed during his premiership although they had reached the so-called “tender stage”, which if completed would have created motorway linkages all the way from Peshawar to Karachi. He expressed that he would ask for this to be completed as soon as possible.
He recalled other highlights of his premiership, including a highway built from Gwadar to Quetta in Balochistan and a Rs50 billion motorway built from Gilgit to Skardu in the Gilgit-Baltistan region. He also called attention to the Lowari Tunnel project in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, which he said had been left incomplete for about 50 years before he ensured its completion during his time in government.
In contrast, he added, “for the last 15 years, a party’s government has continued to form inside KPK, and what did they achieve there?”
Regarding the country’s progress, he said, “Once our GDP rate was 7.5 per cent, and inflation was at a minimum. The electricity tariff used to be, I don’t know, one or two rupees.”
He also noted that during his tenure, the rupee had not broken 104 against the dollar, while the interest rate was 5.25pc and reserves had reached $24bn.
“It has risen from 24 to 27 (billion) today. But instead of 27, had this disruption not occurred, these would have been above 50 today […] way above 50,” he added, referring to the PTI government’s tenure during which they had returned to IMF loans and hiked the interest rate to about 22 or 23 per cent.
Highlighting that his government had also ended hours of loadshedding within three years, he also commended PM Shehbaz for having set up power plants with great speed.
Nawaz called for a “comparison” of major political parties in TV discussions when talking about performance, saying, “The PML-N’s performance is better than the performance of all parties in the country.”
He commended the work of PM Shehbaz, Punjab CM Maryam and DPM Dar for their “great work”, calling for all the party’s successful projects to be replicated in AJK. He added that this would benefit “the people of Azad Kashmir, its residents, the children, the elders, the poor, and all segments of society”.
During the meeting, the party’s leadership discussed public welfare, development projects and the implementation of election promises with the AJK leadership, according to a post by the party on X.
Punjab CM Maryam Nawaz also attended the meeting, along with federal and provincial ministers, including Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal and Information Minister Attaullah Tarar.
The federal and Punjab governments reaffirmed their commitment to supporting AJK’s development and prosperity, the party’s statement added.
PM Shehbaz, AJK PM Gillani and Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz also addressed the meeting, with their remarks live-streamed on state media.
PM Shehbaz noted that the PML-N was provided a chance to serve the people of AJK in the July elections.
He assured Gillani that the federal government would provide “whatever financial assistance” is needed to fulfil the plans announced by the AJK PM.
“Whatever you need from the Centre regarding this, we are here,” he said, noting that Deputy PM Ishaq Dar, Kashmir Affairs Minister Amir Muqam and others had made great efforts for AJK’s progress.
The premier announced that a Daanish School will be inaugurated next month in Bhimber to provide good quality education to deserving children.
The prime minister paid tribute to the “martyrs of occupied Kashmir” and those who were currently in jail there.
“Their sacrifices will soon bear fruit,” he said, affirming that the government and the people of Pakistan will continue their political and moral support for India-occupied Kashmir.
In her remarks, CM Maryam noted it was “good to see” that her father had begun giving practical shape to his pledge to remain involved in AJK’s journey of development.
She said the Punjab government was ready to help in AJK’s development if needed.
Maryam expressed confidence that the AJK premier’s government would ensure the completion of various projects, including the Apna Ghar scheme, electric buses, Cath labs and Clinics on Wheels, ahead of their schedules.
“They have given timelines for when we are starting based on those timelines. I think they should also keep the timeline of when they will finish and deliver it in mind because that is more important,” she said.
The Punjab CM termed skill development a “low-hanging fruit”, adding that a model was ready to train and assist people so that a person can earn their own living after six months of training.
She highlighted her regular monitoring of projects through the Punjab Information Technology Board, stressing that 24/7 monitoring was extremely important.
“I think you should pay a lot of attention to law and order reforms, police reforms,” Maryam said.
She emphasised that the people of AJK should “feel that such a government has come into power that is not only doing work for their progress, for their development, but is also accessible to them”.
“This accessibility is very important; it changes everything. Secondly, you should speak outside with the public very often and give them confidence about what you are doing and what you want to do,” the Punjab CM advised the AJK PM.
In his address, Gillani highlighted planned development projects over the next 100 days, including the launch of Suthra Kashmir and ‘Apni Chhat Apna Ghar’ programmes in the region.
He said that the government guest houses would be revamped completely in the next six months to promote tourism, and that beautification projects for Muzaffarabad and divisional headquarters would be completed within three months.
He noted that Green Bus services had been launched in Mirpur and Muzaffarabad and would roll out across six cities for improved travel facilities. Additionally, he highlighted plans to launch two separate scholarships for those who could not afford higher education for their children: one for government universities and one for top private universities. He also highlighted the Daanish schools and university being built in the region, thanking the premier for his generosity in the initiative.
“The infrastructure of three cities where Green Buses will operate — Muzaffarabad, Bagh, and Mirpur — will be completed in 100 days,” he said. “These are all the things that will ensure the people’s trust in the government is restored.”
Later in the day, PM Shehbaz inaugurated the reconstructed Rathoa Haryam Bridge in AJK by unveiling the plaque, the PM Office said.
Nawaz, along with the AJK president and premier, was also present on the occasion.
On the occasion, the prime minister said the completion of the Rathoa Haryam bridge is a major milestone for development and prosperity in the region.
He noted that the bridge would ease commuting and transportation between Mirpur and its surrounding areas.
He further observed that the project would provide local residents with modern travel facilities and boost commercial and economic activities in the region.
The visit comes nearly three months after the PML-N came to power in AJK in August, following phased elections in which Nawaz played an active role in the campaign.
Speaking in Muzaffarabad during the election campaign in July, Nawaz had asserted that the city should be transformed into the most beautiful city of Pakistan. He said that whatever CM Maryam was doing in Lahore and other cities of Punjab should also be “copied” in Muzaffarabad and other cities of AJK.
He had also promised AJK residents that every development project would be implemented and that PML-N would resolve “all the issues” if it won the elections.
In his speech, he had also cautioned his party leadership in AJK that he would visit AJK every three months to see whether the party’s manifesto was being implemented.
After PM Gillani’s election, Nawaz handed him a comprehensive 100-day reform agenda, stressing that essential public services, including healthcare, education, sanitation and reliable roads, were basic rights of the people of AJK and should also reach remote areas rather than remain confined to major urban centres.
