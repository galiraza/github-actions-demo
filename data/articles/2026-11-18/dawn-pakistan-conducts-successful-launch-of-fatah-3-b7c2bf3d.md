# Pakistan conducts successful launch of Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile: ISPR

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 20:35:41 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045425/pakistan-conducts-successful-launch-of-fatah-3-supersonic-cruise-missile-ispr
- Collected: 2026-11-18 16:08:16 UTC

---

The Pakistan Army on Wednesday successfully launched the Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile, according to the military’s media wing.
In a statement, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said, “The Pakistan Army successfully conducted the launch of the Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile, marking another significant milestone in strengthening Pakistan’s national defence capabilities.
“The weapon system is equipped with advanced navigation and propulsion systems and is capable of engaging targets at long range with high precision and lethality at supersonic speed,” it said.
It added that the missile would “significantly enhance the operational flexibility and strike range of the Pakistan Army’s Rocket Force Command, further strengthening the country’s overall conventional deterrence and defence posture”.
According to the statement, the Army Rocket Force Command commander, senior officers from the armed forces, scientists and engineers witnessed the launch.
It added that President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, and the services chiefs congratulated the team on the successful launch.
They appreciated the dedication and commitment of all personnel involved in achieving this significant milestone in Pakistan’s defence development, the statement concluded.
Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar termed the development a “testament to the relentless hard work, skill and innovation of our teams in strengthening Pakistan’s defence capabilities and national deterrence”.
“The entire nation takes immense pride in their unwavering commitment to keeping the country strong and secure,” he said.
Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi congratulated the nation on the successful launch. According to the interior ministry, he said that the launch represented a “significant advancement in the defence sector”.
“The successful test of the Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile marks a major milestone in the country’s defence capabilities,” Naqvi said.
Earlier this month, the Pakistan Air Force performed a successful flight test of the country’s first indigenously developed air-to-air missile, Fa’az.
ISPR said that the flight test marked “another significant milestone in the advancement of national aerospace and defence capabilities”.
In October, Pakistan successfully tested two missiles; the Fatah-2 extended-range hypersonic ballistic missile was tested on Oct 13 and the indigenously developed extended version of the Fatah-IV ground-launched cruise missile was tested on Oct 6.
