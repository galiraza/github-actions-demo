# Policeman martyred after being abducted, tortured in Bannu

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 11:28:15 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045384/policeman-martyred-after-being-abducted-tortured-in-bannu
- Collected: 2026-11-18 08:10:50 UTC

---

BANNU: A policeman was abducted and tortured to death by unidentified armed men near Taziri Chowk in Bannu on Wednesday, police said.
According to police, the incident took place within the jurisdiction of Basia Khel police station, where armed men abducted policeman Javed and later tortured him to death.
Police said an investigation into the incident was underway and efforts were being made to trace and arrest those responsible.
The latest killing in the area comes a day after Head Constable Zahid Khan of the Elite Force was killed by unidentified armed men near the Afghan refugee camp. He was performing duty at the Central Jail.
Several police personnel have been targeted in similar attacks in Bannu in recent months, with incidents of targeted killings and abductions reported across the district.
On June 29, unidentified armed men pulled Constable Umar Niaz Khan out of a rickshaw near Lora Pul, and shot him dead while he was travelling to work.
In September, Elite Force personnel Ahmadullah was shot dead at a shop in the Daud Shah area of Peepal Bazaar, within the jurisdiction of the Cantonment police station.
On October 15, Head Constable Raqeeb Khan of the District Security Branch (DSB) was shot dead near Muslimabad on Circular Road. A day later, five policemen were injured in an armed attack on Gambati police checkpost.
On October 31, Counter-Terrorism Department (CTD) driver Constable Sadiq Zaman was shot dead by unidentified armed men at Barmarshil Chowk while he was on leave. The same day, Head Constable Ziaullah, who was posted at Bannu Police Lines, was abducted near Baran Dam in the Karam Garhi area and later killed.
Apart from targeted killings, police posts and personnel in different parts of Bannu have also come under armed and drone attacks, leaving several policemen injured and prompting security operations in affected areas.
Meanwhile, Section 144 has been in force in Bannu since November 14, imposing various restrictions as part of measures to maintain law and order.
Violence in Bannu has included attacks on police and jirga members, prompting targeted operations by police and security forces in various localities to disrupt terrorist networks.
