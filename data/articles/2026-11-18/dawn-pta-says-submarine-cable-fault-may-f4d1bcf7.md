# PTA says submarine cable fault may cause internet connectivity issues

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 23:50:57 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045448/pta-says-submarine-cable-fault-may-cause-internet-connectivity-issues
- Collected: 2026-11-18 21:17:50 UTC

---

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) said on Wednesday that a fault in one of the international submarine cable systems may result in users experiencing internet connectivity issues.
“One of the international submarine cable systems, the SEA-ME-WE 5 (SMW5), has reported a fault at about 11km from Pakistan’s shore, which may cause some internet users to experience intermittent connectivity issues and a decline in service quality,” the authority said.
“Transworld Associates is coordinating with the SMW5 consortium to identify the root cause of the fault and determine the estimated time for restoration,” it added.
In the meantime, the authority said internet traffic was “being rerouted through alternative international links to minimise the impact and ensure service continuity to the greatest extent possible”.
It added that it was in close coordination with the concerned stakeholders and will “continue to monitor the situation to facilitate the earliest possible restoration of normal internet services across the country”.
Internet monitor NetBlocks confirmed later that a decline in internet connectivity was causing slow service.
“The incident is consistent with a fault on the SMW5 international subsea cable system with operators now attempting to establish alternative routes,” it said.
On July 1, a fault in the SMW5 international submarine cable system caused widespread connectivity issues in Pakistan. The fault was resolved on July 3.
