# Trump warns Iran over ‘plot’ to target US officials

• Says American authorities take ‘very seriously’ any possible threat to Washington 
• CDF Munir tells FM Araghchi to maintain sustained engagement 
• US strikes Qeshm island; Iran targets Bahrain radar system
MANILA: President Donald Trump said on Tuesday that US authorities take “very seriously” any possible threat to Washington, following reports Iran was planning to target officials, possibly with a radioactive bomb.
Asked about the potential threat during a visit to the Philippines, Trump said: “If there is [such a threat], they will pay a price the likes of which no other country has ever paid before.” “I hope it’s not, but we take it very seriously because they happen to be crazy.”
CNN, citing seven anonymous US officials, reported that US intelligence indicated that Iran was preparing to target senior Trump administration officials by detonating a so-called dirty bomb in Washington.
The report said the sources characterised Iran’s plans as “an effort to give itself options rather than an indicator of an imminent attack”.
A dirty bomb is a conventional explosive laced with chemical or radioactive materials.
Iran’s foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei dismissed the report as “fabricated”, suggesting Washington was “seeking to lay the media groundwork for their sinister designs”.
Negotiations between the United States and Iran to end their war have stalled, with mediator Qatar calling for restraint on Tuesday “to avoid any escalation that could undermine negotiation efforts”.
Sustained engagement
Meanwhile, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir underscored the importance of sustained engagement, mutual trust and close coordination between Pakistan and Iran to promote regional peace and stability.
He made this statement during a meeting with Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi on Monday, the military’s media wing said on Tuesday.
According to a statement issued by the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), matters of mutual interest, regional security dynamics and evolving geopolitical developments were also discussed between the two. Both sides also exchanged views on exploring avenues to address common security challenges, it added.
Araghchi appreciated Pakistan’s “constructive role in promoting regional peace and stability and expressed his country’s desire to further strengthen bilateral relations”, the ISPR said.
Attacks, counterattacks
The United States carried out several attacks on Iran’s southern island of Qeshm in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, while Tehran launched drone attacks on a US military radar system in Bahrain.
“The American attack on locations on Qeshm island caused no damage to residential, commercial, or infrastructural areas,” said Ahmad Nafisi, deputy provincial governor of the southern province of Hormozgan, quoted by the official IRNA news agency.
“The situation on Qeshm island is under control, and all relief, service, and executive agencies are on full alert,” he added.
“The sounds of five to six explosions were heard on Qeshm on Tuesday evening,” IRNA said.
Earlier in the day, Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said they had launched a drone attack targeting a radar system in Bahrain operated by the United States.
“Combat drones belonging to the Revolutionary Guards carried out a precision operation this morning, targeting a radar system operated by US forces at the Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain,” the Guards’ Sepah news website said. It added that the attack was in response to the “repeated violations of Iranian airspace by US military drones”.
Meanwhile, attacks on tankers have increased this month in the strait, and the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency said the crew of a tanker passing through the waterway was unaccounted for after being attacked north of the Omani port city of Khasab.
Iran’s Revolutionary Guards and navy said on Tuesday that a ship carrying LNG and two oil tankers had caught fire in the strait.
