# Anthropic to begin trading after record-breaking market debut tops SpaceX record

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 10:46:32 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045642/anthropic-to-begin-trading-after-record-breaking-market-debut-tops-spacex-record
- Collected: 2026-11-19 08:22:15 UTC

---

Anthropic will begin trading on Thursday on the Nasdaq exchange after pricing its initial public offering at $83 a share, above its expected range and toppling SpaceX’s IPO to set a new record.
Hot demand for the AI giant brushed aside market jitters over the spy scandal that toppled Nvidia’s chief executive, despite lingering concerns of an investment bubble.
The company said on Wednesday that it offered 956 million shares for sale, which gives the maker of the Claude chatbot a valuation of $1.5 trillion, including employee stock options and restricted shares.
Its listing will give ordinary investors their first chance to bet directly on a stand-alone AI giant when it begins trading under the ticker “ANT.”
The offering breaks the record $75 billion that Elon Musk’s SpaceX raised in June at a valuation of $1.77 trillion. SpaceX ultimately raised $86 billion after the banks underwriting the IPO exercised options to buy additional shares.
Anthropic raised $79.4 billion, which could increase to as much as $89.6 billion if its underwriters likewise buy additional shares.
The strong demand comes even as AI stocks have swung sharply, with investors questioning whether heavy spending on the technology will pay off.
Nvidia, the chip giant at the centre of the AI investment boom and an investor in Anthropic and dozens of other AI companies, has been shaken by the resignation of chief executive Jensen Huang after he admitted to an affair with a woman reportedly believed by US intelligence to be a Chinese spy.
“Anthropic’s IPO is a big near-term win for the tech sector and should help restore some investor confidence,” Dan Ives of Yorkville Ives & Co said in a note.
‘Threats to Americans’
Wall Street is also digesting the US midterm election results, which gave control of both chambers of Congress to Democrats, who are more wary of AI.
Earlier estimates had put Anthropic’s valuation as high as $2 trillion, but they fell as AI stocks slid on Wall Street in recent weeks.
Anthropic’s Claude models are among the most powerful in the world, hailed for their problem-solving potential, but also feared for their capacity to destabilise societies and replace human workers.
The five-year-old company’s revenue has surged at a pace few US companies have ever matched, reaching $17 billion in the third quarter, up from $1.4 billion a year earlier.
Much of that revenue comes from customers paying to use its AI models for computer coding.
Siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, now the company’s chief executive and president, founded Anthropic with several colleagues in 2021 after leaving ChatGPT-maker OpenAI.
Anthropic’s IPO filing gives extensive detail to the risks of the technology, reflecting the company’s focus on safety even as it builds ever more powerful models.
US Democratic Senator Elizabeth Warren criticised the listing on X, saying the company “fails to answer for the potential threats to Americans’ retirements and the entire financial system if this IPO goes awry.”
The filing also warns that advanced AI systems “could pose catastrophic or existential risks to society if not developed and deployed responsibly”.
