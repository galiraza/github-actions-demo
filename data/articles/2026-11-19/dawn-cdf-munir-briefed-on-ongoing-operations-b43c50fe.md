# CDF Munir briefed on ongoing operations during Balochistan visit: ISPR

- Published: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 12:44:44 +0500

Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir was briefed on counterterrorism operations during a visit to Turbat, Balochistan, the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said in a press release on Thursday.
CDF Munir received a comprehensive briefing on “the prevailing security environment, operational preparedness and successful ongoing operations against externally sponsored terrorism”, ISPR said.
He was also informed of progress in ongoing socioeconomic development initiatives in Balochistan.
CDF Munir, in interactions with the troops, “commended their high state of operational preparedness, professionalism and unwavering resolve to defend the motherland, amidst challenging operational conditions”, ISPR said.
He also laid a floral wreath at the Shuhada Monument.
The army chief reaffirmed Pakistan army’s “unwavering commitment to stand shoulder to shoulder with the people of Balochistan in their continued pursuit of peace, prosperity and sustainable development”.
Upon his arrival in Turbat, the CDF was received by Corps Commander Balochistan, the ISPR statement added.
ISPR says 22 terrorists killed
In a separate statement, ISPR said security forces had killed 22 terrorists earlier this week, on Nov 16 and 17, during multiple engagements in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
The operations in South Waziristan, Bannu and Tank were conducted on Monday and Tuesday, the statement said, adding that 15 terrorists were killed during an intelligence-based operation (IBO) in South Waziristan.
ISPR said the slain terrorists belonged to “India-sponsored Fitna-al-Khawarij” — a term that the state uses for terrorists belonging to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan.
Moreover, army troops engaged terrorists in two encounters in the Bannu district, during which six terrorists were killed, according to ISPR.
Another terrorist was killed in an encounter in the Tank district, the press release said.
It added that weapons and ammunition had been recovered from the killed terrorists, who were “actively involved in numerous terrorist activities in the area”.
“Sanitisation operations are being conducted to eliminate any other India- sponsored kharji found in the area as the relentless counterterrorism campaign under the vision ‘Azm-i-Istehkam’ (as approved by Federal Apex Committee on National Action Plan) by security forces and law enforcement agencies of Pakistan will continue at full pace to wipe out the menace of foreign-sponsored and -supported terrorism from the country,” the statement said.
President Asif Ali Zardari lauded security forces for the operations, commending their professionalism and bravery, as per a statement issued by his office.
He vowed that Operation Azm-i-Istehkam will continue until foreign-sponsored terrorism is eliminated.
KP and Balochistan, where security forces continue to carry out counterterrorism action, have particularly been affected by terrorism in Pakistan.
On Saturday, ISPR said that four troops, including a major, were martyred while 24 terrorists were killed in IBOs in Balochistan and KP’s North Waziristan.
Furthermore, last week, 74 terrorists were killed in IBOs in KP and Balochistan.
The Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies (PICSS), an Islamabad-based independent think tank monitoring conflict and security developments, reported encouraging trends in the two provinces in its monthly report for October.
Pakistan overall witnessed a significant decline in terrorism-related violence during October, with attacks falling by 37 per cent, deaths resulting from these attacks declining by 57pc, and injuries decreasing by 71pc compared to September, the report said.
It added that the improvement was particularly pronounced in Balochistan and mainland KP.
“Balochistan recorded a substantial reduction in militant violence, with attacks declining by 46pc, from 132 in September to 71 in October. Deaths resulting from militant attacks decreased from 136 to 44, a reduction of approximately 68pc, while injuries declined from 63 to 37, representing a decrease of 41pc,” the report said.
Regarding mainland KP, it said a “significant decline” was witnessed in terrorism.
PICSS recorded 40 attacks during October, compared with 58 in September, representing a reduction of 31pc. Meanwhile, deaths resulting from these attacks declined from 90 to 38, a decrease of approximately 58pc, while injuries fell from 165 to 19, representing a reduction of 88pc.
The merged districts of KP recorded 30 attacks during October, compared with 34 in September, and deaths resulting from these attacks decreased from 33 to 25, while injuries increased from 11 to 13, the report said.
