# EY-led consortium signs financial advisory pact for outsourcing of Karachi, Lahore airports

The Privatisation Commission and a consortium led by Ernst & Young (EY) Middle East on Thursday signed a financial advisory services agreement (Fasa) for the outsourcing of Karachi and Lahore airports.
In August, the commission had approved the appointment of a financial adviser for the outsourcing of both airports, mentioning a “consortium led by EY-Parthenon as the top-ranked interested party”.
It also constituted a negotiation committee to finalise the Fasa with the successful consortium.
“The Privatisation Commission and a consortium led by EY Middle East signed a Fasa today in Islamabad for the outsourcing of Jinnah International Airport, Karachi and Allama Iqbal International Airport, Lahore,” according to Thursday’s press release issued by the commission.
“The transactions envisage bringing in private sector expertise for the operation, management, commercial development and optimisation of both airports through a concession/management contract,” the commission underlined.
Under the Fasa, the EY Middle East-led consortium would provide “comprehensive transaction advisory services, including technical, financial, legal, environmental and commercial expertise, to support structuring and implementation of the transactions in accordance with international best practices through a transparent, competitive and market-driven process”.
The commission termed the signing an “important milestone” in the government’s privatisation programme.
The press release said the signing reflected the government’s commitment to “enhancing efficiency, service quality and long-term sustainability of both airports through private sector participation while ensuring transparency, competitiveness and value for the people of Pakistan”.
Adviser to the Prime Minister on Privatisation and Privatisation Commission Chairman Muhammad Ali, along with senior officials of the commission, witnessed the signing ceremony.
The EY team was represented by Andre Olivenca of EY International and Zeeshan Hussain, the statement added.
In December 2022, the government, under the premiership of Shehbaz Sharif, decided to outsource the management of the Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad airports under a public-private partnership.
Earlier this year, the outsourcing operation for these airports shifted from a government-to-government (G2G) mode to an open bidding process due to high investor interest, aiming for transparency and fair competition.
Both Karachi and Lahore airports are expected to attract over $500 million each in fresh investment.
In October, PM Shehbaz also approved the expansion of Allama Iqbal International Airport and Jinnah International Airport to improve facilities for passengers.
