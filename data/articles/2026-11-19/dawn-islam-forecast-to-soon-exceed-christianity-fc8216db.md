# Islam forecast to soon exceed Christianity as world's biggest religion by middle of next decade

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 11:50:06 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045648/islam-forecast-to-soon-exceed-christianity-as-worlds-biggest-religion-by-middle-of-next-decade
- Collected: 2026-11-19 08:22:15 UTC

---

Islam is expected to surpass Christianity as the world’s biggest religion by the middle of the next decade, the Pew Research Centre projected on Wednesday.
While Christianity has ranked as humanity’s largest religion for centuries, the global Muslim population has grown much faster and is forecast to reach 2.57 billion by 2035, compared with a projected tally of 2.52bn Christians in that year, the Pew study found.
If recent trends hold, Muslims will outnumber Christians 3.6bn to 3bn worldwide by 2070, and Islam will also then account for the largest portion of the global population — 37 per cent, up from 26pc in 2020, Pew said.
Christianity, meanwhile, is expected to hold steady at around 29pc, ranking second in global proportion.
The Pew study also concluded that Pakistan’s Muslim population was expected to double from 227 million in 2020 to 461m in 2070.
“The country will rise from having the second-largest Muslim population to the world’s largest,” it said.
The study attributes the projected rise in the world’s Muslim population mostly to higher fertility rates in Muslim countries, averaging 3.1 children for each Muslim woman, compared with 2.5 children per Christian woman during the period 2020-2025.
In all other religious categories combined, the birth rate in that period averaged 1.6 children per woman, below the minimum rate of 2.1 considered necessary for a generation to replace itself.
While natural population growth — births exceeding deaths — remains the biggest factor in the changing face of Muslim and Christian demographics, religious switching will also play a role, said Pew, a non-partisan, Washington-based think tank.
Switching faiths
Based on past trends, according to Pew, both Islam and Christianity will lose more adherents from switching than they gain, but Muslims are expected to lose far fewer — 8m between 2020 and 2070, compared with 105m for Christians.
The world’s overall population is projected to increase 29pc between 2020 and 2070, with Islam and Christianity the only two religious groups forecast to keep pace with or exceed that growth rate, expanding by 80pc and 30pc, respectively.
Hindus are growing in absolute numbers but are expected to shrink slightly as a share of the world’s population (13.6pc in 2070), with Jews projected to follow a similar pattern (0.17pc in 2070). Buddhists, meanwhile, are forecast to decline in numbers and as a percentage of global population (2.5pc in 2070).
All other faiths combined — including Baha’is, Daoists, Jains, Sikhs and followers of various folk religions — are expected to see a modest rise in numbers, while collectively slipping below 2pc of all humanity by 2070, according to Pew.
Moreover, people with no religious affiliation, a category that includes those identifying as atheist or agnostic, are expected to decline sharply as a proportion of the global whole, from 24pc in 2020 to 17pc in 2070, the study found.
The geographic distribution among religions is also shifting dramatically.
By 2070, for instance, Christianity will rank as the largest religion in the United States but not its majority religion, and the Democratic Republic of Congo will surpass the US as the country with the greatest concentration of Christians, Pew projected.
The numbers of religiously unaffiliated people are expected to grow in most countries in the coming decades, but those increases will be entirely outweighed by the dwindling population forecast for China, where most of the world’s non-believers currently reside.
