# Pakistan, Qatar reaffirm commitment to strengthening bilateral relations in security, counter-terrorism: ISPR

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 20:25:28 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045683/pakistan-qatar-reaffirm-commitment-to-strengthening-bilateral-relations-in-security-counter-terrorism-ispr
- Collected: 2026-11-19 16:15:47 UTC

---

Pakistan and Qatar reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening bilateral relations in the areas of institutional cooperation, security and counter-terrorism on Thursday, the military’s media affairs wing said.
The development cam as Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir met with Qatar’s Interior Minister Sheikh Khalifa bin Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, who is currently visiting Pakistan, in Rawalpindi on Thursday, according to a statement by Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR).
During the meeting, they discussed matters of mutual interest and the evolving regional and global security situation. They also discussed avenues for the enhancement of bilateral security cooperation, ISPR said.
“Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to further strengthening bilateral relations between the two countries, particularly in the fields of security, counter-terrorism and institutional cooperation,” the statement said.
The visiting dignitary appreciated Pakistan’s role in promoting regional peace and stability and acknowledged the longstanding fraternal ties between the two countries, it added.
CDF Munir reaffirmed Pakistan’s commitment to further deepen bilateral engagement with Qatar. He underscored the importance of close cooperation in addressing shared security challenges, the statement said.
MoU signed on security cooperation
The two countries earlier signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) on security cooperation, along with an action plan to prevent drug smuggling.
The MoU was signed between Qatar’s Internal Security Force (Lekhwiya) and the Federal Constabulary, according to the Prime Minister’s Office (PMO).
Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi and Qatari counterpart Al Thani signed the documents. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif also witnessed the signing ceremony, the statement said.
The PMO handout said that the premier “warmly” welcomed the visiting dignitary on “on his first visit to Pakistan and reaffirmed the longstanding brotherly relations between Pakistan and Qatar”.
“He expressed the hope that the visit would further strengthen cooperation between the ministries of interior and relevant institutions of the two brotherly countries,” it said.
PM Shehbaz also conveyed his greetings to the Qatari emir and thanked the country’s leadership for its “unwavering support to Pakistan”.
“He particularly appreciated Qatar’s continued supply of liquefied natural gas, which contributes to meeting Pakistan’s energy requirements,” the statement said.
PM Shehbaz highlighted that nearly 300,000 Pakistanis work in Qatar, where they play an important role in the country’s development and prosperity.
He said that there were extensive opportunities to expand Pakistan-Qatar economic cooperation, particularly in trade and investment, agriculture, livestock, information technology and mining and minerals.
The statement said that the Qatari minister thanked Pakistan for its hospitality. He also conveyed Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani’s congratulatory messages and best wishes to the prime minister.
“He appreciated Pakistan’s role and efforts for promoting peace and stability in the region,” the statement added.
According to a separate statement by the interior ministry, Naqvi and his counterpart also agreed to expand the scope of the exchange programme for police officers.
The two also agreed to enhance cooperation between Qatar’s National Cyber Security Agency and the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency.
“A decision was made to improve coordination between the two interior ministries to enhance bilateral cooperation,” the statement said.
It added that Qatar would also build a modern firing range at the National Police Academy. Naqvi thanked Qatar for providing the Anti-Narcotics Force with modern scanners, adding that “joint operations and information sharing” between the relevant agencies of the two countries were ongoing.
“With joint cooperation between Pakistan and Qatar, 300 drug cases have been busted over the past four years,” Naqvi was quoted as saying.
“We want to ensure that our territory is never used for drug smuggling,” the interior minister said.
“Cybercrime is an international problem, and Pakistan is taking robust measures to combat it,” he added.
Last month, in a meeting in Doha, Naqvi and the Qatari counterpart agreed to strengthen bilateral cooperation in internal security.
