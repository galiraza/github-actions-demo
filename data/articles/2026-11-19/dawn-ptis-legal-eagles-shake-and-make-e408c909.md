# PTI’s legal eagles ‘shake and make up’

• Raja, Khosa deny internal rifts; Gohar maintains all decisions taken on Imran’s directions  
• PTI warns Adiala authorities against violating SC orders on ex-PM’s medical treatment  
• IHC directs proper medical care, hygienic living conditions for Bushra Bibi as per prison rules
ISLAMABAD: PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja and senior lawyer Sardar Latif Khosa — who were reportedly divided over the question of engaging with the establishment — appeared to have resolved their beef on Wednesday, as both leaders shook hands and declared that there was no discord within the party.
Speaking to the media outside the Islamabad High Court, both leaders maintained there was complete discipline and cohesion within the party.
Mr Raja said attempts to create divisions within the PTI would not succeed. He stepped forward and shook hands with Mr Khosa. In response, Mr Khosa smiled and said Mr Raja is his junior (barkhurdar).
It may be recalled that rumours had been circulating within the party that Mr Khosa and his group favoured holding negotiations and increasing engagement with the establishment. Mr Raja’s group, however, was said to favour maintaining a hard line against the establishment and avoiding any gesture that could suggest the PTI had weakened and was left with no option but to hold talks.
Earlier, Mr Raja had acknowledged differences with Mr Khosa and Salman Safdar after their meeting with Imran Khan, amid speculation that the PTI founder wanted major changes within the party.
But on Wednesday, Mr Raja said that Imran Khan’s letter, conveyed by Mr Safdar, was intended to awaken the conscience of the judiciary and the nation and prompt them to act.
“As a party, we will play the role that is our duty. The health of Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi is very important to us,” he said.
Mr Safdar said the case against the PTI founder was controversial, adding that several questions had been raised about the decision in the Al-Qadir case. He said it was concerning that the media had already predicted the outcome of the case.
PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan said he was unaware of the purpose behind PPP chief Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari’s recent press conference. However, he described it as a positive development that everyone had finally accepted the PTI’s position.
He also said the PTI did not believe in power-sharing and that all decisions were taken in accordance with Imran Khan’s directions. He said the PTI would not make any irresponsible statements on the issue of new provinces and would keep its cards close to its chest.
PTI letter to Adiala jail admin
Meanwhile, the PTI on Wednesday sent a letter to the Adiala jail administration, warning it against violating Supreme Court orders on the provision of medical treatment to incarcerated former prime minister Imran Khan and urging his transfer to Shifa International Hospital.
Advocate Khalid Yousuf Chaudhry told Dawn that the PTI had learnt through the media that the jail administration had taken Imran Khan to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (Pims) for treatment.
“It is nothing but contempt of the Supreme Court. We urge that he [Imran] should be immediately shifted to Shifa International Hospital in accordance with the Supreme Court’s orders and provided proper treatment. That is what I have written in the letter,” he said.
The letter comprises 44 pages, including the SC order of Aug 18, directing Mr Khan’s transfer to Shifa International Hospital, the Islamabad High Court order of Aug 6 regarding facilities, Barrister Salman Safdar’s open letter on behalf of the PTI founder and his amicus curiae report submitted to the SC.
The letter, dated Nov 12 but sent to the Adiala jail administration and several other relevant offices on Wednesday, states that the jail administration must ensure implementation of the binding judicial directives issued by the Supreme Court.
Medical care for Bushra Bibi
Meanwhile on Wednesday, the Islamabad High Court directed prison authorities to ensure medical care and hygienic living conditions for Bushra Bibi as per prison rules, while adjourning for seven days the appeals filed by her and her husband against their convictions in the £190 million case.
The couple is serving prison sentences in the Al-Qadir Trust case. On Jan 17, 2025, an accountability court in Islamabad sentenced Imran Khan to 14 years in prison and Bushra Bibi to seven years in the case. Both subsequently challenged their convictions before the IHC.
An IHC division bench comprising Chief Justice Sardar Muhammad Sarfraz Dogar and Justice Muhammad Asif granted Barrister Salman Safdar, counsel for the PTI founder, seven days to prepare arguments on the appeals.
During proceedings, the court also took up a fresh miscellaneous application filed on behalf of Bushra Bibi, raising concerns about her deteriorating eyesight, need for surgery, possible post-operative complications and the medical facilities available to her in prison.
After hearing arguments from her counsel, Sardar Latif Khosa, the bench directed the prison authorities to ensure cleanliness in her cell, arrange an examination by a specialist doctor from a hospital and provide all necessary medical facilities in accordance with jail rules.
The court further directed Islamabad Advocate General Naveed Hayat Malik to ensure that its order was implemented through the relevant prison authorities.
