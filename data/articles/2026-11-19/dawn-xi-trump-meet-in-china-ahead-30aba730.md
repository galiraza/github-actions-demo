# Xi, Trump meet in China ahead of trilateral with Putin

President Xi Jinping and US counterpart Donald Trump began talks in China on Thursday, ahead of a history-making trilateral with Russian leader Vladimir Putin, Chinese state media reported.
Relations between China and the US are “stable and improved,” President Xi Jinping told counterpart Trump in their third face-to-face meeting this year.
“During this period, China-US relations have remained generally stable and improved, bringing tangible benefits to the people of both countries and injecting stability and certainty into the world,” Xi said in Shenzhen.
The agenda for the three-way meeting in Shenzhen was unclear, with hosts China saying it would be about “safeguarding world peace”
Earlier, Trump and Putin held a bilateral meeting in China ahead of the three-way discussion with Xi.
“We look forward to our meeting. We’ve had great relationships for many years, we’ve known each other for a long time, and I think a lot of wonderful things can come out of this or at least, start,” Trump said in Shenzhen, according to a recording of their meeting released by the Kremlin, calling Putin his “friend”.
Prior to the bilateral meeting, the presidents of China, the United States and Russia posed for a photo with other Asia-Pacific leaders ahead of their first trilateral summit, billed by Beijing as talks about “world peace”.
The three-way meeting between Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin at the Kylin Villa in Shenzhen comes with Russia’s offensive in Ukraine approaching the five-year mark.
Trump is bogged down by his war with Iran that has choked oil supplies and pushed up consumer prices worldwide, and an ascendant China is looking to strengthen its hand in East Asia.
The conclave has stoked fears that the three powerful men may seek to divide the globe into spheres of influence, for example by deciding the fate of Ukraine over the heads of Kyiv or Europe.
Before that, Trump and Xi were due to hold bilateral discussions expected to touch on trade ties and potentially common rules for artificial intelligence, under the cloud of the Nvidia spy scandal.
Beijing, Washington and Moscow have given different accounts of the agenda for the meeting on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) forum.
Washington initially signalled that Ukraine was off the table, before Trump said the war would feature. Xi does not view the conflict as China’s problem to solve.
The Kremlin has said that the agenda had not been finalised, but it would be hard to avoid discussing Ukraine at the meeting, which is set to last “no more than an hour”.
China has said only that the trio will hold “in-depth exchanges on safeguarding world peace and promoting strategic stability in major-power relations”.
Critics warned that the optics from the meeting will be damaging for the US president — but a fillip to Xi and Putin.
“In Russia… it will be said after this meeting that Russia belongs to the key countries on the global stage and that the EU countries do not,” Russian political analyst Konstantin Kalachev told AFP.
“Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin are playing Donald Trump like a cheap violin,” Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer said.
Nuclear disarmament
Also up for discussion are the Iran war and nuclear disarmament, according to Washington, after a US-Russia bilateral treaty on atomic weapons expired in February.
Beijing has long maintained that the United States and Russia must reduce their giant nuclear arsenals before it agrees to talks on its smaller stockpile.
“Look, we have 4,000 nuclear weapons. Russia has 3,800 nuclear weapons.
China now has four or five hundred, but they’re going to catch (up),” Trump told Fox News in Shenzhen.
Trump has also that North Korean leader Kim Jong Un “would like” to come to China to see him. The two met three times in Trump’s first term.
Pyongyang — which has drawn closer to Russia in recent years — has not officially responded, with state media showing Kim visiting on Wednesday a naval university.
Airport welcome
Breaking with decades of diplomatic protocol, Xi received Trump at the airport, with the men briefly hugging before a red-carpet greeting complete with cheering children and an air-force flyover.
This reciprocated the airport greeting Trump gave Xi at Joint Base Andrews in September.
“It’s a friendship relationship. It’s a respect relationship,” Trump told Fox News.
The airport welcome showed “great respect, and I appreciate that. That’s a big win for us. It’s a big win for China too,” Trump said.
‘Serene authority’
Other world leaders at the APEC meeting include Japan’s Sanae Takaichi, South Korea’s Lee Jae Myung, Canada’s Mark Carney and Australia’s Anthony Albanese.
Takaichi, on her first visit since angering Beijing a year ago with comments about Taiwan, met Putin – with whom relations are also tense – on Wednesday, Tokyo said.
Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum became a minor star on Chinese social media for taking an economy class flight to Shenzhen, where she requested a panda from Xi.
Despite their differences, all the leaders — including Putin — posed for the APEC “family photo” on Thursday in special scarves.
Apart from the centrepiece trilateral, the group was also expected to discuss other topics including trade and AI.
Xi opened the APEC leaders’ meeting on Thursday with a call to “reduce barriers to trade and investment”.
