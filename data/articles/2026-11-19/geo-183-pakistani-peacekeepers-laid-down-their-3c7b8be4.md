Pakistan has reaffirmed its commitment to international peace and security, highlighting its contributions to United Nations peacekeeping missions over the past seven decades, Defence Minister Khawaja Asif said on Wednesday.
Addressing the Schuman Security and Defence Forum at the European Parliament in Brussels, Asif said Pakistan's active role in promoting global peace and security, particularly through UN peacekeeping operations, was a testament to its sacrifices and services to the international community.
He said 237,000 Pakistani personnel had served in 48 UN peacekeeping missions over the past seven decades, while 183 Pakistani peacekeepers had laid down their lives in the service of international peace.
The defence minister stressed that collective awareness, a common approach and cooperation were essential for developing effective security policies to promote global peace.
He cautioned that sudden reductions in UN peacekeeping operations in conflict-affected areas could further heighten human security risks.
Asif said peacekeeping mandates should be realistic, adequately resourced and aligned with sustainable political solutions.
Countries contributing military and police personnel to UN missions should also have effective representation in shaping these mandates, he added.
Emphasising the need to place human security at the centre of international peace efforts, the minister said peace was not merely the absence of war but required sustainable stability.
He identified climate change, food insecurity and economic vulnerabilities as factors contributing to global conflicts, noting that Pakistan was among the countries directly affected by the risks posed by climate change.
Asif underscored the importance of resolving longstanding disputes in accordance with international law and relevant United Nations resolutions.
He also called for unimpeded humanitarian assistance to conflict-affected areas and the fulfilment of commitments on climate action and development.
Reaffirming Pakistan's commitment to multilateralism, the defence minister said the country was ready to play a bridging role in strengthening an inclusive and effective multilateral international system.
Pakistan would continue to contribute towards upholding human dignity, justice and sustainable peace at the global level, he added.
