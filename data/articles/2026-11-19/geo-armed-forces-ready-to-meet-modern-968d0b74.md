Published November 19, 2026
Chief of the Naval Staff Admiral Naveed Ashraf has said that modern warfare is undergoing significant changes, with artificial intelligence and information technology transforming traditional methods of combat.
Addressing the graduation parade at the Pakistan Air Force (PAF) Academy Asghar Khan in Risalpur on Thursday, he said the armed forces are aware of emerging trends in warfare and prepared to meet the evolving challenges.
The naval chief congratulated the graduating cadets and said it was an honour for him to attend the passing-out parade.
Admiral Ashraf said the PAF had a proud history and had achieved success on numerous occasions. He said that its capabilities and skills were recognised around the world.
The Pakistan Navy, he said, has also made significant progress in adopting new technologies.
The naval chief said modern conflicts were no longer confined to traditional battlefields, extending into electronic warfare, cyberspace, space and cognitive domains.
“More so, autonomous systems, artificial intelligence, and information warfare have reshaped the very texture of how battles are fought and won,” he added.
“Geopolitical dynamics are shifting rapidly, presenting multifaceted security challenges that demand constant vigilance, strategic foresight, and remarkable operational readiness to deter conventional, as well as hybrid and asymmetric threats,” he noted.
He said the armed forces are fully aware of these developments and prepared to meet the emerging challenges.
