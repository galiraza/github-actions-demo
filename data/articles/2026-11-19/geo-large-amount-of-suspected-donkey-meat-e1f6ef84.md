# Large amount of suspected donkey meat seized in Islamabad raid

Published November 19, 2026
Islamabad Food Authority (IFA), alongside capital police, recovered at least 800 kilogrammes of alleged illegal meat, believed to be from different animals and numerous live and dead animals in a raid carried out in Sector D-14 of the federal capital, The News reported.
Acting on a tip-off regarding the unauthorised preparation and storage of meat, an IFA enforcement team raided the property on November 16.
According to the First Information Report (FIR) lodged by an IFA official, inspectors discovered the 800kg cache of suspected illicit meat alongside 12 live donkeys, one dead donkey, five live dogs, 17 turtles, a jackal, and a pangolin.
Officials reported that the occupants of the house failed to produce any valid licenses or legal documentation authorising the processing or storage of meat on the premises. Furthermore, the FIR noted that the facility was operating in severe violation of established hygiene standards and food safety regulations.
Following a formal assessment submitted by an IFA veterinary specialist, a criminal case was registered at the Golra Police Station on November 17. Suspects face charges under Sections 11, 12, 13, and 14 of the Islamabad Capital Territory Food Safety Act, 2021. Police have initiated legal proceedings and launched a broader investigation to identify and apprehend all individuals connected to the network.
The seizure marks the latest in a string of similar enforcement actions in the capital. In July 2025, authorities conducted a comparable raid in the Tarnol area, resulting in the recovery of more than 50 donkeys and a substantial quantity of suspected illicit meat.
