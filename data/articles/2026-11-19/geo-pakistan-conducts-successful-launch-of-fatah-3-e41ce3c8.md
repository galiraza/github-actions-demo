RAWALPINDI: Pakistan Army has conducted a successful launch of Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile, marking another significant milestone in the advancement of national defence capabilities, the military’s media wing said in a statement on Wednesday.
According to Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), the weapon system is equipped with advanced navigation and propulsion systems and is capable of engaging targets at long range with high precision and lethality at supersonic speed.
“Fatah-3 will significantly enhance the operational flexibility and strike reach of the Pakistan Army’s Rocket Force Command, further strengthening the country’s overall conventional deterrence and defence posture,” it added.
The launch was witnessed by Commander Army Rocket Force Command and senior officers of the Pakistan Armed Forces, along with scientists and engineers, said the ISPR.
President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Chief of Army Staff and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir and the services chiefs commended the successful launch of Fatah-3. They appreciated the dedication and commitment of all those who contributed towards achievement of another significant milestone in development of national defence capabilities of Pakistan.
