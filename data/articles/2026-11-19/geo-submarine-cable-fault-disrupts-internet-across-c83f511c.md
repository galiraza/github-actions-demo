# Submarine cable fault disrupts internet across country: PTA

- Source: Geo

---

Published November 18, 2026
Internet users across Pakistan are currently experiencing intermittent connectivity and slow speeds after a fault was reported in one of the country’s international submarine cable systems, the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) said on Wednesday night.
In a statement, the PTA said that the fault has occurred in the SEA-ME-WE 5 (SMW5) cable system, about 11 kilometres from Pakistan’s shore which may cause some internet users to experience intermittent connectivity issues and a decline in service quality.
Transworld Associates (TWA), which operates the cable landing in Pakistan, was coordinating with the SMW5 Consortium to identify the root cause of the fault and determine the Estimated Time for Restoration (ETTR), said the PTA.
In the meantime, PTA said internet traffic is being rerouted through alternative international links to minimise the impact and ensure service continuity to the greatest extent possible.
The authority added that it is in close coordination with all concerned stakeholders and will continue to monitor the situation closely to facilitate the earliest possible restoration of normal internet services across the country.
However, no timeline for complete restoration has been announced yet.
