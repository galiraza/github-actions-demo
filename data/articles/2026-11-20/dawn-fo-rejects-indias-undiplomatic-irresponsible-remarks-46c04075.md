# FO rejects India's 'undiplomatic, irresponsible' remarks about Pakistan's mineral wealth

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 18:42:50 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045921/fo-rejects-indias-undiplomatic-irresponsible-remarks-about-pakistans-mineral-wealth
- Collected: 2026-11-20 15:53:36 UTC

---

The Foreign Office (FO) on Friday categorically rejected the “undiplomatic and irresponsible” remarks made by India at the United Nations regarding the country’s mineral wealth.
At the UN Security Council (UNSC) Arria-formula meeting on natural resources and conflict, on Thursday India’s Permanent Representative to the UN Ambassador Parvathaneni Harish had “directly accused the Pakistan military of looting the country’s natural resources for personal gain and triggering domestic political turmoil to keep control over them”, according to Indian news agency ANI.
The remarks had come after Pakistan raised the Indus Waters Treaty (IWT) during the meeting, which has been held in abeyance by India following the attack in occupied Kashmir’s Pahalgam that killed 26.
In response to Harish’s remarks, Pakistan’s First Secretary Ansar Shah rejected the “frivolous and highly inappropriate remarks”.
On Friday, the FO spokesperson was also asked about the Indian remarks during his weekly press briefing, to which he rejected the “undiplomatic and irresponsible” comments.
“Such baseless accusations merit no attention and were immediately and effectively rebutted by Pakistan’s representative during the same meeting,” he said, adding that the remarks constituted “an unacceptable interference in Pakistan’s internal affairs” and were antithetical to the UN Charter.
In a detailed statement released shortly after, the FO said: “Such diatribe, so typical of India, can only be rubbished.”
“Instead of addressing Pakistan’s legitimate concerns with respect to India’s hydro-terrorism, India has resorted to unsubstantiated insinuations about Pakistan’s institutions and leadership,” the statement said.
The statement highlighted that India’s hostility towards Pakistan’s development of its mineral resources was “equally evident in its persistent efforts to undermine Pakistan’s stability”.
“Pakistan has repeatedly raised concerns, supported by evidence, regarding India’s sponsorship and support of terrorist activities in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, aimed at disrupting development and depriving local populations of the benefits of their natural resources,” the statement said.
“India’s continuing descent into a Hindutva-inspired extremism-fascist polity, led by a religious fundamentalist regime is well known,” it added.
“Indian accusations and distortion of facts are a futile attempt to hide its international isolation, and only aimed at distracting attention from India’s domestic situation and its weaponisation of shared resources, as evident from its illegal and unilateral action to hold the IWT in abeyance,” the statement said.
The FO said that in August, the Court of Arbitration ruled that the treaty remains binding and fully in force and there was no legal basis for its unilateral suspension or termination.
“India’s refusal to accept this ruling raises serious questions about its commitment to treaty obligations and international law,” it said.
“Instead of misusing the platform of the UN for peddling lies, India will be well advised to implement its obligations under the UNSC resolutions on Jammu and Kashmir, where Indian occupation forces continue to perpetrate gross violations of human rights for decades,” it said.
“A state that is a serial violator of UN Charter, UNSC resolutions, sovereignty of neighbouring states, and binding treaty obligations, that patronises systematic discrimination against its Muslim, Sikh, Christian, and Dalit minorities, engages in disinformation and terrorism as instruments of state policy and remains in forcible occupation of a territory whose people it denies the internationally guaranteed right to self-determination, has no standing whatsoever,” the FO remarked.
“Instead of pointing fingers, India should introspect and change its behaviour,” it concluded.
