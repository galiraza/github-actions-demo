# Indian man jailed over US murder plot against Sikh separatist

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 23:33:17 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045944/indian-man-jailed-over-us-murder-plot-against-sikh-separatist
- Collected: 2026-11-20 20:59:10 UTC

---

An Indian man was sentenced on Friday to more than 19 years in a US prison for plotting to hire a hitman to kill a Sikh separatist on American soil, allegedly on behalf of Indian intelligence.
The case strained ties between Washington and New Delhi, which denied any involvement in Nikhil Gupta’s foiled ploy to assassinate dual US-Canadian citizen Gurpatwant Singh Pannun.
Gupta pleaded guilty in February to all three counts he faced: murder-for-hire, conspiracy to commit murder-for-hire, and conspiracy to commit money laundering.
US federal prosecutors alleged that an Indian intelligence officer, who remains at large, directed the plot and recruited Gupta in May 2023 to hire a hitman to carry out the murder.
Pannun, the intended target, is affiliated with a New York-based group called Sikhs for Justice that advocates for the secession of Punjab.
Canada separately alleged in 2023 that India arranged a plot on its soil that ended in the killing of a Sikh separatist, who was a naturalised Canadian citizen.
Hardeep Singh Nijjar, 45, was shot dead by masked gunmen outside the Sikh temple he presided over after being wanted by Indian authorities for alleged terrorism offences and conspiracy to commit murder — accusations he denied.
India dismissed Ottawa’s allegations, which sent relations into freefall, with both nations expelling a slew of diplomats.
