# Musadik Malik arrives in New Delhi to attend SCO dialogue forum

- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 11:23:58 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-20 08:01:54 UTC

Federal Minister for Climate Change and Environmental Coordination Dr Musadik Malik arrived in New Delhi on Friday to attend the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum.
His visit is a first by a Pakistani minister since former foreign minister Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari attended the SCO meeting in the Indian state of Goa in May 2023, and two years after India’s Foreign Minister Subrahmanyam Jaishankar attended the bloc’s meeting in Islamabad in 2024.
Musadik was received at the airport by Pakistan’s Chargé d’Affaires Saad Waraich, according to the Pakistan High Commission in India.
Dr Malik will lead the Pakistan delegation at the forum, being held from Nov 20 to Nov 22.
The gathering will bring together representatives from Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) member states to discuss issues related to civilisational dialogue and cultural understanding, the Foreign Office said in an earlier statement.
“The government of Pakistan remains committed to promoting dialogue among civilisations and fostering people-to-people contacts within the SCO framework in a spirit of mutual respect,” the statement said.
Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi is also scheduled to address the “Civilisational Dialogue Forum” of the 10-member regional bloc.
The forum was proposed by Modi at the SCO Council of Heads of State summit in Tianjin, China, in September 2025.
Recent high-level engagements between Pakistan and India have largely taken place under the SCO framework; for instance, Bilawal’s visit in 2023 and Jaishankar’s in 2024.
But neither visit had featured formal bilateral talks.
More recently, India’s SCO National Coordinator Alok Dimri visited Islamabad in late September for a preparatory meeting ahead of the next SCO Council of Heads of State summit.
“This is my first time on Pakistani soil; these SCO-related programmes will continue throughout the year and culminate in the Islamabad summit,” the Indian official told PTV News at the time.
Pakistan has assumed the rotating chairmanship of the SCO Council of Heads of State for the 2026-27 term and is scheduled to host the organisation’s summit in Islamabad in August 2027.
Relations between India and Pakistan have remained strained, particularly since the four-day military conflict in May 2025, with official engagement largely confined to multilateral forums.
