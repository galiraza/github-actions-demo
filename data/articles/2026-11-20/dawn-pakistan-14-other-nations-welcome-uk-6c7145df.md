# Pakistan, 14 other nations welcome UK, Australia sanctions on Israeli settlements

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 10:08:49 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045854/pakistan-14-other-nations-welcome-uk-australia-sanctions-on-israeli-settlements
- Collected: 2026-11-20 08:01:54 UTC

---

Pakistan, along with 14 other countries, has welcomed the imposition of sanctions by the United Kingdom and Australia in response to Israeli settlement activities in the occupied Palestinian territory and expressed hope that the decisions would encourage others to follow suit.
Britain said on Tuesday it would impose sanctions on eight Israeli construction companies involved in building settlements in the occupied West Bank, as part of its effort to protect the idea of a two-state solution.
Australia followed by imposing sanctions on three Israeli individuals and four organisations for illegal settlement expansion and settler violence in the West Bank on Wednesday, saying those actions were extinguishing the possibility of a two-state solution.
In a joint statement on Thursday, the foreign ministers of Pakistan, Jordan, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, Indonesia, Qatar, the United Arab Emirates, Turkiye, Kuwait, Algeria, Niger, Palestine, Libya, and Maldives welcomed the decisions.
They also welcomed the United Kingdom’s decision to sanction an organisation obstructing the delivery of humanitarian aid to Gaza, the statement said.
“The ministers also expressed support for all efforts to ensure accountability, including through appropriate international measures and the imposition of sanctions on entities and individuals responsible for settlement activities, including the E1 settlement plan, as well as those that support, facilitate or implement settlement expansion and annexation policies,” it added.
The E1 settlement project would bisect the Israeli-occupied West Bank and cut it off from East Jerusalem, fragmenting the land Palestinians hope will form the heart of a state.
In the joint statement, the ministers renewed their “strongest condemnation of Israel’s continued settlement policies in the occupied Palestinian territory, particularly the ‘E1’ settlement plan and related settlement activities east of occupied East Jerusalem”.
The statement said the ministers considered the measures by the UK and Australia to be in line with international law and relevant United Nations resolutions, including the “obligation of all states not to recognise as legal the situation arising from Israel’s unlawful occupation and not to render aid or assistance in maintaining the situation created by Israel’s occupation”.
In this regard, they reiterated their call for Israel to rescind all measures taken in connection with settlement activities and other measures aimed at altering the geographic and demographic character of the occupied Palestinian territory.
Moreover, they condemned the “Israeli military escalation in Gaza” and called for “the full and immediate implementation of President Trump’s Comprehensive Plan to End the Gaza Conflict, to ensure security and stability, address the humanitarian situation, facilitate reconstruction and recovery, and lay the foundations for lasting peace”.
The ministers underscored that Gaza was an integral part of the occupied Palestinian territory and that the only viable path to achieving a just, lasting, and comprehensive peace in the territory was the “implementation of the two-state solution, through the realisation of an independent, sovereign, contiguous, and viable Palestinian State, based on the Jun 4, 1967 lines, with East Jerusalem as its capital, in accordance with international law and the relevant United Nations resolutions”.
Sanctions
The UK’s measures on Tuesday targeted eight companies, including Har Hevron Development Company Ltd, Hemdat Shomron Ltd and SAG (Velvel) Construction and Development Ltd, for asset freezes and director disqualifications.
The UK government announced the sanctions package shortly before the final deadlines for the E1 tender.
On Wednesday, Australia also imposed sanctions in response to settlement activities in the West Bank, with Foreign Minister Penny Wong saying in a statement: “The sanctioned individuals and organisations have actively participated in the planning, coordination and establishment of new settlements, including raising funds, recruitment of volunteers and providing support to violent settlers.”
Daniella Weiss, leader of the settler organisation Nachala and an advocate for the expulsion of Palestinians from Gaza, is among those sanctioned. Another sanctioned individual, Eliav Libi, has accelerated the expansion of settlements in the West Bank through his construction company.
The measures prohibit Australians and Australian businesses from trading with, or providing support to, the sanctioned individuals and organisations.
Wong also said that violence at the E1 settlement project in recent weeks had undermined the prospect of a two-state solution.
She said she had also spoken to Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Saar about Australia’s latest round of sanctions.
“I outlined our position … I said we do not agree with, we oppose the settlement activities. We have had a long-standing position on this,” Wong told ABC Radio.
