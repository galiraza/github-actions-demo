# Pakistan Shaheens begin Asia Cup Rising Stars title defence with 61-run win over UAE

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 19:59:35 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045930/pakistan-shaheens-begin-asia-cup-rising-stars-title-defence-with-61-run-win-over-uae
- Collected: 2026-11-20 15:53:36 UTC

---

Defending champions Pakistan Shaheens began their Asia Cup Rising Stars campaign with a convincing 61-run victory over the United Arab Emirates (UAE) in their opening Group ‘B’ fixture at the University of Doha for Science and Technology on Friday.
Pakistan Shaheens will take on arch-rivals India in their next match on Nov 22 (Sunday).
Batting first, Pakistan Shaheens were bowled out for 245 in 46.4 overs, with Shamyl Hussain and Maaz Sadaqat leading the scoring with half-centuries.
Shamyl, who was named Player of the Match, struck a rapid 62 off 39 balls, hitting three fours and seven sixes. He shared an 87-run opening stand with Maaz, who contributed 53 off 63 deliveries, including three fours and two sixes.
Captain Abdul Samad scored 39 off 49 balls, while Farhan Yousaf (28 off 38 balls) and Mohammad Imran (22 off 21 balls) made useful contributions to the total.
For UAE, Basil Hameed was the standout bowler, claiming four wickets for 37 runs, while Awais Ali Shah and Ibrar Ahmad picked up two wickets apiece.
In reply, UAE were dismissed for 184 in 40.5 overs as Pakistan Shaheens’ bowlers maintained control of the chase. Fast bowler Ali Raza struck early, removing three top-order batters to leave UAE struggling at 16 for three inside the first six overs.
Mohammad Shahdad top-scored for UAE with 45 off 60 balls, including four fours and a six, while Aftab Alam made 39 and Awais Ali Shah added 32.
