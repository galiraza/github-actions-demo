# Pakistan 'unequivocally' condemns Houthi attack on Riyadh’s tallest skyscraper

- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 10:24:08 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-20 08:01:54 UTC

---

Pakistan on Friday “unequivocally” condemned a Houthi attack on Riyadh’s tallest skyscraper, which serves as the headquarters of Saudi Arabia’s sovereign wealth fund.
“This act of aggression constitutes a grave violation of the sovereignty of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia,” the Foreign Office (FO) said in a statement condemning Thursday’s attack, without naming the tower.
Saudi state-run news agency SPA reported, citing the kingdom’s civil defence, that the attack caused material damage to several floors of the building and nearby buildings, but no injuries were reported. The statement carried by the SPA did not identify the tower, but an accompanying photograph published by the agency showed the Public Investment Fund Tower.
The strike blew out windows across seven to eight floors of PIF Tower, bending parts of its metal facade. It was the second time in less than two weeks that a Houthi attack damaged a landmark in Riyadh’s financial district, with state oil company Aramco’s building hit in a Nov 8 attack.
The Houthis have been firing at Saudi airports and oil infrastructure since Yemen’s frozen civil war reignited in July amid the US-Iran conflict.
The renewed fighting has shattered a UN-brokered truce reached in 2022 that largely halted cross-border attacks between the Saudi-led coalition, which backs Yemen’s internationally recognised government, and the Houthis, who control the capital, Sanaa.
Pakistan, which is party to two defence agreements along with Saudi Arabia — the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement and the Saudi Mutual Defence Agreement that empower the parties to treat an attack on any one of them as an attack on all — has expressed its solidarity with the kingdom amid the escalation.
In its statement on Friday regarding Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, the FO said: “The continued targeting of civilian infrastructure and economic facilities endangers innocent lives and poses a serious threat to regional peace and security. Such reprehensible attacks must cease forthwith.”
It added that Pakistan stood firmly with the government and people of Saudi Arabia. “It supports the kingdom’s legitimate right to defend its sovereignty and territorial integrity and to safeguard its citizens and residents. Pakistan has been and will continue to make all efforts for achieving lasting peace and stability in the region,” the statement said.
