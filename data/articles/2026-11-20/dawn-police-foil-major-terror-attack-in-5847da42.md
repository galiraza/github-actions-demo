# Police foil major terror attack in KP’s Lakki Marwat, claim suspected suicide bomber killed

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 16:37:25 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045905/police-foil-major-terror-attack-in-kps-lakki-marwat-claim-suspected-suicide-bomber-killed
- Collected: 2026-11-20 15:53:36 UTC

---

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police said on Friday that they had foiled a major terrorist attack in Lakki Marwat district and killed a suspected “Afghan-origin” suicide bomber, according to a statement.
In a press release, police said the operation was carried out within the jurisdiction of Bargi Police Station in Lakki Marwat.
The police claimed that they had been receiving intelligence for some time about a Mazda truck allegedly carrying a large-scale improvised explosive device (IED) intended to cause widespread destruction.
Acting on the intelligence, Lakki Marwat police intensified their operations and increased patrolling and the number of checkpoints, the press release added.
“The vehicle in question was spotted today within the jurisdiction of Bargi Police Station, prompting local police to take immediate action,” the statement said, alleging that “one suicide attacker was killed during the operation”.
Following reports of the encounter, personnel from the Counter-Terrorism Department (CTD), local police and the Quick Response Force (QRF) reached the scene and launched an extensive operation, it added.
The press release added that bomb disposal teams had also arrived at the scene and begun inspecting the vehicle.
“A large quantity of explosives was found in drums inside the vehicle,” it said.
The police statement said the deceased suicide bomber was believed to be an Afghan national, adding that the identification process was underway.
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Inspector General of Police Zulfiqar Hameed praised Lakki Marwat Police for foiling a major tragedy through timely action.
“By thwarting the nefarious designs of Fitna al-Khawarij, our brave personnel have saved dozens of precious lives,” IGP Hameed said in a statement.
“The brave personnel of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police remain steadfast in protecting the public. We will not rest until Fitna al-Khawarij is made an example of,” he added.
The state uses the term Fitna-al-Khawarij for the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP).
On Nov 14, the police foiled a terrorist attack on Kingar Pul Police Checkpost in Bannu district, eliminating four terrorists in retaliation.
