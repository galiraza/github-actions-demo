# Security withdrawn from Fazl, family members

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 07:46:59 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2045802/security-withdrawn-from-fazl-family-members
- Collected: 2026-11-20 08:01:54 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: Police and Federal Constabulary (FC) personnel deployed to protect JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman and other members of his family have been withdrawn from the residences they guarded in Islamabad and Dera Ismail Khan, police and party sources told Dawn on Thursday.
JUI-F spokesman Aslam Ghouri confirmed the development to Dawn, claiming that the FC and police personnel deployed with the Maulana had been called back.
According to him, FC and KP police officials deployed at the Maulana’s homes in Dera Ismail Khan and Islamabad were simultaneously withdrawn on Wednesday evening.
He further alleged that the Maulana’s house in Islamabad had been cordoned off by the capital police for some time, and security personnel had also harassed some visitors.
He also claimed that the party’s Central Secretariat in Islamabad had also been “besieged” by police.
An official of Islamabad police, however, said that only one police guard had been called back.
Meanwhile, JUI-F leaders from KP alleged that security personnel deployed to protect the JUI-F chief and members of his family — including his son Asad Mehmood and brother Maulana Attaur Rehman — had also been withdrawn.
They claimed that the personnel had been directed to report back to police lines, and some of them were even instructed to leave their posts “within 15 minutes”. There was, however, no official word from the authorities.
Meanwhile, opposition leaders in the National Assembly and Senate, Mehmood Khan Achakzai and Allama Raja Nasir Abbas, expressed their concern over the reported withdrawal of security from the JUI-F chief, and called for restoring his detail in view of threats to the lives of JUI-F leaders.
Separately, Islamabad police have registered a case under various sections of the Anti-Terrorism Act, the Pakistan Penal Code and the Peaceful Assembly and Public Order Act, 2024, against two JUI-F leaders and more than 200 unidentified workers for allegedly blocking a road outside Farooq-i-Azam Masjid at Sector I-9/4 on Nov 7 when the administration asked the party leaders to call off the event, which they had arranged without obtaining an NOC.
Those nominated in the FIR are JUI-F’s Mufti Abrar and Maulana Iftikhar Hussain. It states that the event had been organised despite imposition of Section 144 in Islamabad. The FIR said when the participants were informed that the gathering was unlawful, the JUI-F leaders became infuriated and provoked 200 to 250 workers and refused to stop the programme.
It alleges that the workers attacked the police contingent, pelted police personnel with stones, and damaged a police vehicle. They also blocked the road by placing hurdles, confining the residents inside the residential flats and causing panic among the locals.
An anti-terrorism court in Islamabad has also issued a non-bailable arrest warrant of Mufti Abrar in connection with the case.
