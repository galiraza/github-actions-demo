# Pakistan's climate change minister in Delhi to attend SCO dialogue forum

Published November 20, 2026
Federal Minister for Climate Change Dr Musadik Malik arrived in New Delhi on Friday to lead Pakistan’s delegation at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Dialogue Forum, according to the Pakistan High Commission in India.
The forum will discuss the shared civilisational heritage and cultural ties among member states, with a focus on promoting regional heritage and strengthening cultural cooperation.
Pakistan’s Chargé d’Affaires in New Delhi received the federal minister upon his arrival.
The Pakistani delegation’s participation aims to promote the region’s shared cultural heritage and further strengthen cooperation among SCO member states.
The November 20-22 visit marks the first trip to India by a Pakistani federal minister since May 2023, when then foreign minister Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari visited Goa for a meeting of the SCO Council of Foreign Ministers.
The meeting comes amid strained relations between Pakistan and India, which have remained locked in a diplomatic stalemate since their four-day military confrontation in May 2025.
Relations between the two countries deteriorated following the killing of 26 people in Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir (IIOJK). New Delhi accused Islamabad of involvement in the attack without offering any evidence, a charge Pakistan denied.
In a statement issued the day earlier, the Foreign Office said Malik would attend the forum at the invitation of India’s Minister of Culture and Tourism.
"Dr Malik will share Pakistan’s perspective on the salience of dialogue among civilisations and Pakistan’s contribution in this area of diplomacy," the statement said.
It added that the minister's statement would also highlight Pakistan's constructive role in the SCO and its diverse civilisational and cultural heritage.
The FO said Pakistan's participation in the forum reflected its contribution as an active SCO member state to promoting dialogue, cooperation and understanding in line with the "Shanghai Spirit".
The Pakistani delegation will also take part in thematic sessions and engage with representatives of SCO member states on the sidelines of the forum.
"Pakistan is currently chairing the SCO Council of Heads of State for the 2026-27 period. In this capacity, it will host several ministerial meetings, expert group meetings and other activities ahead of next year’s summit," the statement added.
