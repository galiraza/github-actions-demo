# 4 guards of Sardar Abdul Rehman Khetran held after recovery of body from Barkhan property

- Published: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 00:55:36 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046146/4-guards-of-sardar-abdul-rehman-khetran-held-after-recovery-of-body-from-barkhan-property
- Collected: 2026-11-21 20:00:18 UTC

---

QUETTA: Police said on Sunday they have detained four guards of MPA and former Balochistan minister Sardar Abdul Rehman Khetran following the reported recovery of a body from his property in Barkhan district.
A separate case has also been registered against Khetran and his two sons over the alleged murder, kidnapping and illegal confinement of one of his former gunmen in a private jail.
According to Koh-Suleman Deputy Inspector General (DIG) Ayaz Baloch, the four security guards were taken into custody during a search operation at Khetran’s fortified residence in Haji Kot on Nov 20. Weapons were also recovered from their possession, he said.
The operation followed the arrest of an employee responsible for managing Khetran’s land, whose identification reportedly helped police locate the burial sites.
Two decomposed bodies were recovered from a graveyard in the Tumnai area, around 40 kilometres from Barkhan, on Nov 19, police said. A third body was recovered during excavation at Khetran’s residence the following day.
Police have shifted the three bodies to Quetta for DNA testing. Khetran has rejected the allegations, describing the police action as “politically motivated”.
On Nov 18, Barkhan police registered a case against Khetran; his two sons, Islam Shah and Abdul Rauf; and three other accomplices on charges of murder, kidnapping and illegal confinement.
The first information report, seen by Dawn, was registered on the complaint of Pyara Khan Bugti, father of Khetran’s former guards, Sahib Din Bugti and Qamar Din.
The FIR invoked sections 34, 302, 342, 343, 364, 365, 368 and 506 of the Pakistan Penal Code (PPC).
The father alleged that his two sons were taken to a private jail four years ago, with Khetran saying that a man, accompanied by his brother and son-in-law, had complained against the brothers.
The father claimed that Qamar Din was released by Khetran a year ago, but he later found out through “people and the media” that Sahib Din was allegedly murdered.
He also accused the MPA of threatening him against reporting the matter to the police.
