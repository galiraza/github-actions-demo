# Barrister Gohar demands fair treatment for PTI workers

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sat, 21 Nov 2026 04:31:47 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046023/barrister-gohar-demands-fair-treatment-for-pti-workers
- Collected: 2026-11-21 07:57:02 UTC

---

• Calls on superior judiciary to intervene over continued detention of party activists  
• Opposes withdrawal of leaders’ security; demands PTI’s reserved seats back
PESHAWAR: Pakistan Tehreek-i-Insaf (PTI) Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan has demanded an immediate end to what he described as injustices against detained party leaders and workers.
He urged the authorities to ensure that no one was detained unlawfully and that all individuals were granted their constitutional rights.
Speaking at a press conference at the Peshawar Press Club on Friday, alongside other party leaders, he said PTI representatives had visited the families of recently arrested party leaders to express solidarity and sympathy.
The PTI chairman said the Peshawar High Court had granted bail to several party workers arrested under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO), but they were still facing difficulties.
He alleged that the party and its workers had been subjected to oppression and hardships over the past four years. He maintained that PTI workers had remained peaceful and would continue their political struggle through peaceful means.
He said the time had come to end the alleged mistreatment of political workers, adding that his party had also raised its voice for judicial independence. He observed that whenever ordinary citizens raised their voices for their rights, the situation became increasingly difficult.
Referring to court orders concerning people detained under the MPO, Barrister Gohar alleged that those arrested under the law were being denied justice despite their constitutional and legal rights.
He said PTI parliamentarians had been elected through public votes and were committed to promoting peace, maintaining law and order and upholding the rule of law in their respective constituencies.
Calling upon the superior judiciary to intervene, he said justice could not be served by keeping people behind bars without lawful justification. He urged the authorities not to discriminate against PTI or subject its members to differential treatment.
Commenting on reports about the withdrawal of security provided to Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman and his family, Barrister Gohar said security arrangements for political leaders should not be withdrawn under any circumstances.
He said protecting the lives and property of citizens was the state’s fundamental responsibility, warning that growing political tensions would further aggravate the situation.
The PTI chairman also called for unity among political forces and the formation of a broader political alliance to steer the country towards stability and a better future. He said political differences should not be allowed to deepen the prevailing tensions and that politics must be pursued in the interest of the people.
Responding to a question about the proposed creation of new provinces, Barrister Gohar said it was premature for the party to announce a final position on the issue.
He said that if a formal proposal or draft emerged, PTI would consult its political allies and party founder Imran Khan before deciding its future course of action. Until then, the party would maintain its existing position, he added. Barrister Gohar also demanded the return of reserved seats to PTI, saying that those who had secured the seats and benefited from the existing arrangement should return them to the party.
