# Capt Safdar seeks Rs10bn in damages from Advocate Mian Dawood over 'defamatory' remarks

LAHORE: Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz’s spouse and former MNA, retired Capt Muhammad Safdar, has served a legal notice to Advocate Mian Dawood, demanding an unconditional public apology, retraction of allegedly defamatory remarks, removal of videos and Rs10 billion in damages.
The notice, dated Nov 20, was issued through a Rawalpindi-based lawyer, Majad Ali Butt, under Section 10 of the Punjab Defamation Act 2024.
Advocate Dawood confirmed to Dawn that he had received the legal notice. He said an appropriate response would be sent to Safdar after examining the notice.
According to the notice, the dispute arose from Safdar’s public demand that former president of the Supreme Court Bar Association (SCBA), Ahsan Bhoon, produce his LLB degree, a matter it says is already before the Supreme Court and the Pakistan Bar Council (PBC).
Last month, PBC declared that the degrees of Bhoon and its other elected members were genuine.
The notice claimed that Safdar had said he would apologise if Bhoon’s degree proved genuine and that Advocate Dawood, an “unrelated third party”, responded with malice.
The notice cited three pieces of content, including a two-minute video uploaded on Sept 24 on YouTube, a news channel interview aired on Oct 29 and a video statement made after the SCBA elections held last month.
It argued that free speech cannot become a “licence for vulgar abuse”, and that Dawood was expected to show restraint.
The notice demanded that the lawyer publish, within 14 days, an apology on the same platforms and in leading English and Urdu newspapers, retract the statements, have the videos removed, pay Rs10bn and refrain from repeating the remarks.
Failing that, Safdar would approach the Special Tribunal under the Defamation Act, the notice warned.
In late September, Safdar called for verification of the degrees of prominent lawyers, including Bhoon, a close associate of Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar.
His remarks also came ahead of the SCBA elections. Bhoon heads the Independent Group, also known as the Asma Jahangir Group. Safdar had announced the formation of his own group, the Quaid-i-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah Group.
Subsequently, Bhoon filed a Rs5 billion defamation suit against Safdar over remarks questioning the authenticity of his LLB degree. The Punjab Defamation Tribunal had admitted the suit for hearing and issued a notice to Safdar.
Meanwhile, the retired Captain had moved a complaint with the Judicial Commission of Pakistan to seek verification of Bhoon’s educational qualifications. Days later, PBC said that the returning officers (ROs) for its 2020 and 2025 elections had gotten the elected members’ degrees verified from their respective universities before notifying them as the council’s members.
