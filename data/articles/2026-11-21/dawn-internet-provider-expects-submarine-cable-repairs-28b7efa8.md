# Internet provider expects submarine cable repairs to be completed by Dec 6

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sat, 21 Nov 2026 09:43:53 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046093/internet-provider-expects-submarine-cable-repairs-to-be-completed-by-dec-6
- Collected: 2026-11-21 07:57:02 UTC

---

Internet service provider Transworld Associates has said repair work on an affected international submarine cable is expected to be completed by December 6, with users likely to experience slower internet speeds during peak hours until the work is finished.
In an email to customers, the company said repair operations were actively underway and that the tentative timeline for completion was Dec 6.
“Further to our earlier notice on November 18, 2026, repair operations for the affected international submarine cable system are actively underway,” the company said.
Transworld said it had also scheduled a maintenance window during which its technical teams would address the existing cable issue. It added that alternative routing arrangements had been made to maintain connectivity during the maintenance activity.
The development follows an earlier announcement by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) that a fault had been reported in the SEA-ME-WE 5 (SMW5) international submarine cable system, approximately 11 kilometres from Pakistan’s coast.
The regulator warned that the fault could cause intermittent connectivity issues and a decline in service quality for some internet users.
The PTA had said Transworld Associates was coordinating with the SMW5 consortium to identify the fault and determine the estimated time for restoration.
Meanwhile, internet traffic was being rerouted through alternative international links to minimise disruption and maintain service continuity, the regulator had added.
Internet monitoring organisation NetBlocks had also reported a decline in connectivity in Pakistan, saying the incident was consistent with a fault in the SMW5 international subsea cable system.
Submarine cables are a critical part of international telecommunications infrastructure, carrying data between countries and continents. Faults in these systems can affect internet speeds and connectivity, particularly when traffic has to be redirected through alternative routes.
Currently, seven submarine cables provide internet connectivity to Pakistan: Asia-Africa-Europe 1 (AAE-1), South East Asia-Middle East-West Europe 4 (SMW4) and India-Middle East-Western Europe (IMEWE), operated by PTCL; and South-East Asia-Middle East-Western Europe 6 (SEA-ME-WE 6), South East Asia-Middle East-West Europe 5 (SMW5) and Transworld Associates 1 (TWA-1), operated by Transworld Associates.
Cyber Internet Services operates the seventh cable named Pakistan and East Africa Connecting Europe (PEACE).
In July this year, a fault in the same SMW5 cable system had caused widespread connectivity issues in Pakistan. The PTA subsequently announced that the fault had been repaired on July 3, following its detection on July 1.
