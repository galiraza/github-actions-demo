# Pakistan rises 17 places in UN e-governance ranking

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sat, 21 Nov 2026 08:02:37 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046012/pakistan-rises-17-places-in-un-e-governance-ranking
- Collected: 2026-11-21 07:57:02 UTC

---

• Moves from Middle to High category in E-Government Development Index 
• Telecommunication infrastructure score jumps 39pc to 0.6590
ISLAMABAD: Pakistan has climbed 17 places to rank 119th among 194 countries in the United Nations E-Government Development Index (EGDI) 2026, a global benchmark that assesses how effectively governments deliver services to citizens online.
The results were published in the UN E-Government Survey 2026.
This marks Pakistan’s second consecutive improvement in the global ranking. The country ranked 150th in 2022 and 136th in 2024, and has now advanced 31 places over the past four years. Its overall EGDI score has increased by 23 per cent, from 0.5096 in 2024 to 0.6264 in 2026.
Pakistan has also progressed from the Middle EGDI category in 2022 to the High EGDI category. The United Nations has specifically highlighted Pakistan’s progress in its regional analysis, recognising the country as one of the largest gainers in Southern Asia, a subregion that has made notable progress in this year’s survey.
Federal Minister for Information Technology and Telecommunication Shaza Fatima Khawaja said: “Pakistan has risen 31 places in two editions of the UN’s benchmark for digital government. That is the result of sustained work across federal and provincial institutions to put public services online and bring connectivity to more of our people.
“The Digital Nation vision is being delivered through various government departments, and Pakistan’s progress has been broad-based. Its score for online services increased by 20 per cent to 0.8422, the second-highest in Southern Asia, reflecting the growing availability of government services through digital channels. Similarly, Pakistan’s score for telecommunication infrastructure increased by 39pc to 0.6590, following a 52pc increase in the previous edition, as connectivity continues to expand across the country.
“Pakistan also recorded the highest score in Southern Asia for online participation and engagement, achieving 0.9655. This indicator measures the extent to which citizens can participate in government processes through online consultations, feedback mechanisms and access to public information.”
The UN survey also links Pakistan’s progress to the Digital Nation Pakistan Act, 2025, noting that the improvement coincides with the enactment of the legislation, which established a more formal institutional framework for national digital transformation.
The Act also established the Pakistan Digital Authority (PDA) to coordinate digital transformation efforts across federal and provincial governments.
In a statement, PDA Chairperson Dr Sohail Munir said the UN had recognised the Digital Nation Pakistan Act as the institutional foundation for coordinated digital transformation.
“Our task now is to build on it — shared digital public infrastructure, interoperable systems and services that work the same way for a citizen in any province,” he said, adding that the National Digital Masterplan provided a unified direction for the initiative, while the latest result gave it momentum.
He said the government would build on this progress in the next phase of the Digital Nation agenda, focusing on extending digital services, connectivity and digital skills to every part of the country.
