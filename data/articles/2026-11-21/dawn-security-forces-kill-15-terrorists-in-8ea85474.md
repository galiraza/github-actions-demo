# Security forces kill 15 terrorists in Balochistan's Mastung district: state media

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sat, 21 Nov 2026 22:17:00 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046137/security-forces-kill-15-terrorists-in-balochistans-mastung-district-state-media
- Collected: 2026-11-21 20:00:18 UTC

---

Security forces have killed 15 terrorists during two separate operations in Balochistan’s Mastung district, state media reported on Saturday.
According to security sources cited by Radio Pakistan, five terrorists were killed in the first operation, while 10 others were eliminated in the second one. Both were conducted against Fitna al Hindustan under ‘Operation Raddul Fitna 3’, which began in August.
The state has designated outlawed Balochistan-based groups as Fitna al Hindustan to highlight India’s role in terrorism and destabilisation across Pakistan.
Furthermore, several terrorist hideouts were destroyed during the operations, while a large cache of weapons and ammunition was also recovered, according to the report. It added that security forces also cleared the areas of Killi Pasand Khan, Musa Khan, Khorasani, and Kaftari of terrorists.
Security forces remain committed to eradicating terrorism from Balochistan, and operations will continue until the last terrorist is eliminated, security sources asserted.
Pakistan has seen a surge in terrorism, especially in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan, since the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan ended its ceasefire deal with the government in November 2022, vowing to increase attacks.
Last week, four troops, including a major, were martyred while 24 terrorists were killed in multiple intelligence-based operations (IBOs) in Balochistan and KP’s North Waziristan.
On Nov 1, a soldier was martyred while 17 terrorists were killed in two IBOs in Balochistan’s Kachhi and Kharan districts.
However, according to Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies, Pakistan recorded fewer militant attacks from Nov 6 to 12, but fatalities and injuries increased sharply, “highlighting the changing intensity and continued volatility of militant threat across the country”.
KP remained the principal centre of violence, accounting for 16 attacks and 44 fatalities. Balochistan recorded 13 attacks, resulting in eight deaths and 19 injuries, while the former tribal districts witnessed a “substantial” reduction in attacks.
