# Govt cuts kerosene, jet fuel and LDO prices

Published November 21, 2026
ISLAMABAD: The government has reduced the prices of jet fuel, kerosene oil and light diesel oil (LDO) by Rs12.06, Rs10.42 and Rs11.44 per litre, respectively.
According to Pakistan State Oil (PSO), the price of jet fuel has been reduced to Rs287.71 per litre from Rs299.77.
Similarly, the price of kerosene oil has declined to Rs273.22 per litre from Rs283.64.
The price of light diesel oil has also dropped by Rs11.44 per litre, bringing the new rate down to Rs220.28 from Rs231.72 per litre.
The revised prices came into effect on November 21.
Earlier, the federal government had also reduced the price of petrol by Rs1 per litre and increased the price of high-speed diesel (HSD) by Rs2 per litre.
Following the revision, petrol will be available at Rs322 per litre, while diesel will cost Rs357 per litre.
In May, the PSO announced a significant reduction in jet fuel prices, slashing rates by Rs111.44 per litre to Rs330.20 from Rs441.66 per litre after a sharp rise in March, when rates surged by 82%.
In March, diesel prices were also increased, prompting Pakistan Railways to announce fare adjustments.
Fares for economy-class passenger trains were increased by 5%, while those for air-conditioned (AC) classes rose by 10%.
A railway spokesperson said that the department would absorb the additional operational costs of passenger services, while freight charges were increased by 20%.
Pakistan began experiencing the direct economic repercussions of the war involving the United States, Israel and Iran, as escalating tensions in the Gulf pushed global oil prices higher and threatened energy supply routes.
Heavily reliant on imported fuel, the country continued to face rising costs amid concerns over potential disruptions to shipments through the Strait of Hormuz.
