# 3 TTP-linked terrorists killed in village on outskirts of Peshawar: CTD

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 13:38:13 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046302/3-ttp-linked-terrorists-killed-in-village-on-outskirts-of-peshawar-ctd
- Collected: 2026-11-22 15:00:36 UTC

---

PESHAWAR: Three terrorists were killed during an exchange of fire with Counter-Terrorism Department (CTD) and military personnel in the Passani village, located on Peshawar’s outskirts, late on Saturday night, the CTD said in a statement on Sunday.
The statement said the CTD received intelligence about the presence of a group of terrorists belonging to Fitna-al-Khawarij on Passani Road — an area located 23 miles south of Peshawar, falling within the jurisdiction of Mattani police station.
Fitna-al-Khawarij is a term the state uses for terrorists belonging to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP).
The CTD said the terrorists planned to carry out attacks on government installations.
“Based on the confirmed reports, CTD’s Special Weapons and Tactics Teams (SWAT), along with personnel of the Pakistan Army, equipped with modern weapons, reached the area to arrest the terrorists,” the statement added.
However, upon seeing law enforcement and security personnel, the terrorists opened fire, to which the teams responded effectively in self-defence, the statement further said.
Following an exchange of fire, a search operation was launched in the area during which the bodies of three terrorists were found, it said. The CTD identified the terrorists as Abdul Rashid, Saeedullah, and Saeed Nawab — members of the banned TTP.
The statement added that other terrorists present at the site managed to escape; meanwhile, the teams recovered two Kalashnikovs, a pistol, three hand grenades, five magazines and computerised national identity cards (CNICs) from the slain terrorists.
A search operation was under way to arrest the escaped terrorists, the statement added.
As per the CTD statement, KP Inspector General of Police Zulfiqar Hameed and Additional IG CTD Jawad Qamar vowed that terrorists would never be allowed to succeed.
Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif lauded security forces for the successful operation, observing that “timely and decisive action” thwarted a “major threat”, state-run APP reported.
The operations of the security forces against terrorism are a reflection of national resolve and morale, the report quoted him as saying.
KP continues to actively face terrorist threats. Last week, 43 terrorists were killed during operations conducted in KP, including 35 in Mir Ali, North Waziristan district, and eight in Orakzai district, the military’s media wing said.
On the other hand, the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies (PICSS), an Islamabad-based independent think tank monitoring conflict and security developments, has reported encouraging trends in KP in its monthly assessment for October.
In mainland KP, PICSS recorded 40 attacks during October, compared with 58 in September, representing a reduction of 31pc. Meanwhile, deaths resulting from these attacks declined from 90 to 38, a decrease of approximately 58pc, while injuries fell from 165 to 19, representing a reduction of 88pc.
The merged districts of KP recorded 30 attacks during October, compared with 34 in September, and deaths resulting from these attacks decreased from 33 to 25, while injuries increased from 11 to 13, the report said.
