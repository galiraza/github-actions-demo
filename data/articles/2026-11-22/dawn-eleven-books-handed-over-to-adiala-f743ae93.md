ISLAMABAD: Eleven books covering a diverse range of subjects have been handed over to Adiala jail authorities for PTI founder and former prime minister Imran Khan to read.
Khalid Yousaf Chaudhry, who regularly visits jails and courts in connection with Mr Khan’s legal and prison-related matters, confirmed that the books had been handed over to the authorities. “The books cover a variety of subjects, including world and ancient history, Islamic history, Islamic law and jurisprudence, religion, philosophy, Sufism, political thought, international relations, diplomacy and global affairs,” he said.
The collection also includes works on the rise and fall of civilisations, the evolution of Islamic thought, Muslim history, colonial-era legal developments, international power politics, wars and global political developments, he added. “The selection offers a broad range of historical, religious, intellectual and political perspectives, reflecting diverse areas of academic and literary interest.”
