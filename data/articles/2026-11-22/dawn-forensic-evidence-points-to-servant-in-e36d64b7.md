# Forensic evidence points to servant in Hina Javaid murder as probe clears husband

• After nearly three-month delay, police submit charge sheet in high-profile murder case 
• According to the DNA analysis report, Hina Javaid was reportedly sexually assaulted after being murdered by her domestic servant
RAWALPINDI: Rawalpindi police have completed their investigation into the high-profile murder of digital media executive Hina Javaid, who was found dead with signs of torture and strangulation at her husband’s apartment in Lal Kurti in August. The police submitted the challan to the court after about three months.
The development was disclosed by newly appointed City Police Officer (CPO) Rawalpindi Sardar Ghias Gul during his first interaction with media persons on Saturday.
Hina Javaid was murdered on August 20, 2026, at her husband’s house, and her domestic servant has been identified as the main suspect in the case following a forensic DNA match and a report by a high-level inquiry committee.
Hina Javaid’s husband and his father have been cleared of any involvement in the murder by the police investigation, as no evidence linking them to the crime has been found.
At one point during the course of investigation, Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz noted that “DNA test results and polygraph test suggest that the murder and rape were committed by the servant.”
However, he said the investigation was ongoing. On Oct 29, First Lady Aseefa Bhutto Zardari also met with Hina’s family at the Parliament and called for the family’s demand for a judicial commission to be “given due consideration”.
Although Hina Javaid’s husband and her father-in-law were named in the initial FIR registered on murder charges, they were given a clean chit in the police investigation. However, Hina’s husband was arrested, while her father-in-law was only questioned by the police.
It will be up to the court to decide whether Hina’s husband and father-in-law had any link to her murder. The police are required to submit a challan in a murder case within 15 days after a suspect is sent to judicial remand.
Meanwhile, Hina’s family had expressed dissatisfaction over the police investigation and demanded a judicial inquiry.
The family claimed to have proof of evidence tampering by the husband’s brother, who allegedly cleaned the accused’s room, tampered with evidence, and colluded with the police.
The police have already added Section 376 of the Pakistan Penal Code (rape) to the case following the forensic DNA findings.
CPO Sardar Ghias Gul told media persons that the challan had been submitted to the court for legal proceedings. He also acknowledged shortcomings in police prosecution, which result in the release of criminals, and said these would be addressed by appointing the right people to the right positions.
The Punjab government had constituted a three-member high-powered committee to review the investigation into the murder case, in which her husband and a domestic servant had been placed on judicial remand at Adiala Jail.
As part of the investigation, the committee also visited the crime scene, questioned the deceased woman’s husband and the domestic servant in jail, and met members of her family.
The committee comprises Additional Inspector General of the Crime Control Department (CCD) Sohail Chattha, DIG Lahore Shoaib Khurram Janbaz and SSP Investigation Rawalpindi Saifullah.
