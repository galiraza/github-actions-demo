# Journalist’s bail plea rejected

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 05:11:12 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-22 07:44:22 UTC

---

QUETTA: A local court in Quetta on Saturday rejected the bail application of Islamabad-based journalist Shahid Maitla.
The bail application was filed by Rahib Buledi Advocate and other lawyers in the court of Judicial Magistrate-VII.
Judicial Magistrate Akhtar Shah, who had reserved the verdict on the application, announced the decision.
Shahid Maitla was arrested under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (Peca) on a complaint lodged by former caretaker prime minister Anwarul Haq Kakar. After his arrest, Shahid Maitla was shifted from Islamabad to Quetta.He is currently on judicial remand in District Jail, Quetta.
