# Multiple Karachi routes briefly closed as political parties hold demonstration, rally

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 22:34:15 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046320/multiple-karachi-routes-briefly-closed-as-political-parties-hold-demonstration-rally
- Collected: 2026-11-22 20:15:36 UTC

---

Multiple roads were closed briefly in Karachi on Sunday as the Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) staged a demonstration in the city and the Jeay Sindh Rehbar Committee (JSRC) — a conglomerate of four Sindhi nationalist groups — held a rally.
The JUI-F staged its main demonstration at Guru Mandir, while the JSRC organised a rally from Sachal Goth to the press club via Sharea Faisal, some stretches of which had been shut for vehicles, the traffic police said.
Traffic was allowed again on the stretch of Sharea Faisal leading to Drigh Road and Karsaz at 4:16pm after about an hour-long closure. Commuters coming from the airport had been diverted via Rashid Minhas Bridge towards Millennium Mall.
The route from Askari 4 to Drigh Road on Main Rashid Minhas Road was reopened for traffic at around 4:30pm following its reported closure at 3:30pm.
The route from Guru Mandir towards Jamshed Road was reopened for vehicles at around 5pm after being closed to traffic shortly after 2pm.
The stretch of Sharea Faisal from Avari Towers towards FTC was reopened at 8:45pm. Vehicles had been diverted from Avari Towers towards Fatima Jinnah Road and Sindh Club.
Both sides of Deen Mohammad Wafai Road had also been closed to traffic, with vehicles diverted from MR Kiani Chowrangi towards Thana Gali and from Fawara Chowk towards Zainab Market.
Main Korangi Road from CSD towards Sharea Faisal was closed at around 11am due to “security” reasons and was reopened to traffic at 9:30pm.
The alerts for road closures had cited “security” as the reason, except for the blockade of the route between Guru Mandir and Jamshed Road. The alert for this closure said the road had been blocked due to a planned demonstration by the JUI-F.
The JUI-F had said in a statement that while its main demonstration would be held at Guru Mandir, protests would also be held at its headquarters, press clubs and other locations across Sindh against the “violation of the sanctity of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman’s residence, the ongoing smear campaign against him, and the conspiracy to divide Sindh”.
Meanwhile, the JSRC had announced a rally to protest the proposal to create new provinces and divide Sindh.
A four-member PPP committee began efforts on Friday to persuade nationalist parties to postpone their planned rallies in Karachi, but failed to do so.
Over the past few months, Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi has whipped up a political storm since he called for the formation of new administrative units in the country, saying the current “system” of governance had “collapsed” and needed a “reset”.
The PPP, which is in power in Sindh, has been clear in its stance against carving out any new federating units in the province but has been calling for the creation of a South Punjab province.
The Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan, which is an ally of the ruling PML-N along with the PPP in the Centre and in opposition in Sindh, has been insisting on the creation of new provinces and has thrown its weight behind the proposal to hold a referendum on the matter.
