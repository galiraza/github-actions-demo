# Pakistan Shaheens post 313 against India A in heated Rising Stars Asia Cup clash

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 19:40:51 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046331/pakistan-shaheens-post-313-against-india-a-in-heated-rising-stars-asia-cup-clash
- Collected: 2026-11-22 15:00:36 UTC

---

Pakistan Shaheens made 313 in 49.2 overs after winning a controversial toss and opting to bat against India A in their Asia Cup Rising Stars match here on Sunday, with half-centuries from Maaz Sadaqat, Hasan Nawaz and Farhan Yousaf underpinning their total.
The toss took an unusual turn when match referee initially called tails after India A captain Tilak Varma flipped the coin on Pakistan captain Abdul Samad’s call of heads.
Both captains inspected the coin before Samad pointed out the error, prompting the officials to recheck and correct the decision in Pakistan’s favour.
Samad chose to bat on a pitch both captains described as slightly dry. The sides did not shake hands after the toss, maintaining the protocol observed in recent India-Pakistan encounters.
Pakistan made a brisk start, with openers Shamyl Hussain and Maaz taking 65 runs off the first seven overs. Shamyl led the attack, striking 33 off 23 balls, including four fours and two sixes, as the pair put India’s pace attack under pressure.
Harshit Rana, in particular, responded with an aggressive approach, engaging both openers in heated exchanges as they scored freely.
The verbal contest continued through the powerplay and Rana gave Shamyl an animated send-off after the left-hander was caught by the bowler at short third off Ayush Badoni in the eighth over.
Rana struck again five balls later, trapping wicketkeeper Rohail Nazir leg-before for a duck to leave Pakistan 67-2.
Maaz and Hasan Nawaz then steadied the innings with a 54-run stand for the third wicket.
Maaz, registering his second consecutive half-century, made 59 off 63 balls, with six fours and a six, before being caught off Suryansh Shedge in the 20th over.
Hasan took charge, reaching his fifty off 43 deliveries with a six off Vipraj Nigam. He added 65 runs with Abdul Samad, who contributed 28 off 35 balls before being run out in the 31st over following sharp fielding by India captain Rinku Singh and wicketkeeper Kumar Kushagra.
Hasan was caught by Badoni off Shedge in the 39th over for 79 off 82 balls, an innings featuring five fours and four sixes, with Pakistan on 230-5.
Farhan Yousaf then struck 56 off 48 balls, hitting four fours and two sixes, while Mohammad Imran added a brisk 23 off 14 deliveries, including two sixes.
Farhan helped Pakistan cross 300 in the 47th over before being caught by Kushagra off Prince Yadav while attempting a ramp shot.
Pakistan’s lower order offered little resistance as Prince removed Ali Raza for two to end the innings at 313.
For India, Rana, Prince, Shedge and Vipraj Nigam claimed two wickets each, while Badoni took one. Rana conceded 77 runs in his 10 overs.
