# Portuguese foreign minister arrives in Pakistan for official visit: FO

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 11:15:34 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046304/portuguese-foreign-minister-arrives-in-pakistan-for-official-visit-fo
- Collected: 2026-11-22 07:44:22 UTC

---

Minister of State and Foreign Affairs of Portugal Paulo Rangel arrived in Pakistan on Sunday for an official visit, the Foreign Office (FO) said.
Rangel is visiting Pakistan at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar from November 22 to 23. He was received upon arrival by Additional Secretary Europe Ambassador Ayesha Ali.
According to FO, Rangel will hold delegation-level talks with DPM Dar as well as meetings with Pakistani leadership.
“He is accompanied by a business delegation for the inaugural Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum,” FO said, expressing hope that the visit will provide an opportunity to review the full spectrum of the bilateral relationship.
The visit comes as Pakistan and Portugal mark the 77th anniversary of their diplomatic relations, established on Nov 7, 1949, FO spokesperson said in a statement on Saturday.
The two leaders will review bilateral relations and discuss ways to expand trade and investment.
“The visit will also feature a Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum. Companies from both countries will hold business-to-business meetings to explore commercial partnerships and investment opportunities in agri-food, renewable energy, pharmaceuticals, information technology and textiles,” the spokesperson had added in his statement.
Foreign Minister Rangel’s visit will provide an opportunity to build on the longstanding ties between Pakistan and Portugal and to broaden bilateral cooperation across diverse sectors.
