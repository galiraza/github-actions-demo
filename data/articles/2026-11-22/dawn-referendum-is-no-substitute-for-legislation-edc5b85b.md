# Referendum is no substitute for legislation: PTI

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 07:36:25 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046211/referendum-is-no-substitute-for-legislation-pti
- Collected: 2026-11-22 07:44:22 UTC

---

• Challenges Bilawal to return reserved seats, remove Kundi 
• Accuses PPP leadership of playing ‘good cop, bad cop’
ISLAMABAD: The PTI on Saturday dismissed proposals for a referendum on new provinces as an absurd and futile exercise that could not replace a majority vote or a constitutional bill in the national or provincial assemblies, while urging PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari to return reserved seats to the party and remove Faisal Karim Kundi as Khyber Pakhtunkhwa governor.
In a statement, PTI Central Information Secretary Sheikh Waqas Akram said floating the idea of a referendum instead of introducing legislation in parliament validated the party’s long-standing position that the sitting assemblies lacked legitimacy and had been formed on a “stolen mandate and usurped seats” through Form-47 results.
“Because the current lawmakers are not true public representatives, any referendum held under the present setup would be as fraudulent as the assembly itself,” he claimed.
Outlining the PTI’s internal consultation process, Mr Akram said the issue of a referendum would be deliberated upon by the parliamentary party, political committee and core committee, though the final authority rested solely with incarcerated PTI founder Imran Khan.
He made it clear that the PTI would formulate its policy on the creation of new provinces only after party leaders were granted direct access to Imran Khan, whose approval would be final.
He noted that while Imran Khan and the PTI had consistently advocated smaller administrative units, the party would never endorse decisions imposed without constitutional legitimacy, democratic consensus and public consent. He warned that imposing governments on people who had rejected them would defeat the purpose of administrative reform.
The PTI information secretary expressed gratitude to Mr Bhutto-Zardari for expressing concern over the health of Imran Khan, demanding his release and calling him a political prisoner. He said the PTI thanked all those who had spoken out against the “unjust” treatment meted out to the party founder.
Turning his attention to the PPP leadership, Mr Akram said President Asif Ali Zardari had always engaged in the politics of expediency and claimed that the father-son duo was currently playing a good cop, bad cop routine that the public recognised well. While acknowledging the PPP chairman’s recent, belated admission of harsh political realities, he stressed that words alone could not establish credibility without a clear break from the government’s “unconstitutional” measures.
Challenge to Bilawal
To prove his sincerity and show that his remarks were not merely an attempt to extract political concessions or a greater share in government, Mr Akram challenged Mr Bhutto-Zardari to take two immediate confidence-building measures: ensure the return of all usurped reserved seats to the PTI and remove Faisal Karim Kundi as KP governor or expel him from the PPP to prove that the party no longer supported measures against the province’s elected government.
He argued that the PPP could not absolve itself of responsibility for the coercion and mistreatment faced by the PTI while remaining part of the ruling arrangement.
Reflecting on the past three years, Mr Akram said systematic electoral manipulation, undue interference and controversial constitutional amendments had virtually paralysed parliament and the judiciary. He noted that even a major government ally had refused to openly endorse the amendments.
Genuine democratic change, he emphasised, required respect for the people’s electoral mandate, ensuring that parliament served the public and allowing the judiciary to rule on merit.
He condemned the disappearance of former KP finance minister Taimur Saleem Jhagra from a police station following his arrest, alongside the arrests of Irfan Saleem and Kamran Bangash, calling these incidents an extreme manifestation of lawlessness.
He reiterated the PTI’s demand that Imran Khan, his wife Bushra Bibi, non-functional KP chief minister Sohail Afridi, provincial assembly speaker Babar Saleem Swati, former Gilgit-Baltistan chief minister Khalid Khurshid Khan, Taimur Saleem Jhagra and all other detained leaders and workers be immediately presented before the courts and released without delay. He asserted that they were being targeted solely for upholding constitutional supremacy and the rule of law.
