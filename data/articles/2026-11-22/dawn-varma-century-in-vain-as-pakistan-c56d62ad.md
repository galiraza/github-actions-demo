# Varma century in vain as Pakistan Shaheens down India A in Asia Cup Rising Stars clash

- Published: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:14:50 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046331/varma-century-in-vain-as-pakistan-shaheens-down-india-a-in-asia-cup-rising-stars-clash
- Collected: 2026-11-22 20:15:36 UTC

---

Pakistan Shaheens overcame a late challenge from Tilak Varma to beat India A by 48 runs in their Asia Cup Rising Stars match in Doha on Sunday, defending a total of 313 after bowling out their opponents with five balls to spare.
Skipper Varma struck a century before becoming the last man dismissed for 100 off 149 balls, while Shahbaz Ahmed made 56 in a seventh-wicket stand that briefly revived India’s hopes.
But Pakistan’s bowlers held their nerve in the closing overs, with Hasan Nawaz claiming three wickets and Ali Raza taking three for 56.
India’s chase had begun to unravel after an explosive start from Abhishek Sharma. The left-hander made 70 off just 42 balls, hitting six fours and six sixes, but his dismissal at 93-3 in the 16th over left the visitors with considerable ground to make up.
Pakistan’s spinners then tightened their grip. Sufyan Moqim bowled Kumar Kushagra for two before Hasan removed Rinku Singh and Suryansh Shedge, leaving India struggling at 129-6 in the 26th over.
Varma, who had struggled to find the boundary during the middle overs, found support in Shahbaz.
The pair added 112 runs for the seventh wicket, taking the score to 241 before Raza broke the stand by having Shahbaz caught by Hasan for 56 off 49 balls.
Raza then bowled Vipraj Nigam for a duck, while Imran Randhawa dismissed Harshit Rana to leave India nine down.
Varma reached his second century of the tournament, but the milestone offered little consolation as Ahmed Daniyal had him caught by wicket-keeper Rohail Nazir in the 50th over to seal Pakistan’s victory.
Controversial toss
Earlier, Pakistan had posted 313 after winning a dramatic toss and electing to bat on a slightly dry surface.
The toss decision was initially called incorrectly by the match referee, who announced tails after Varma flipped the coin on Pakistan skipper Abdul Samad’s call of heads.
Samad pointed out the error and the officials corrected the decision in Pakistan’s favour. The captains did not shake hands, continuing the protocol observed in recent encounters between the sides.
Pakistan’s openers Shamyl Hussain and Maaz Sadaqat gave their side a flying start, putting on 66 for the first wicket. Shamyl made 33 off 23 balls before Ayush Badoni had him caught by Harshit Rana at short third. Rana then trapped Rohail leg-before for a duck, with Pakistan on 67-2.
The exchanges between Rana and the Pakistan batters had added an edge to the powerplay, with the Indian fast bowler involved in heated verbal exchanges and giving Shamyl an animated send-off.
Maaz and Hasan rebuilt the innings, with the former scoring 59 off 63 balls for his second successive half-century.
Hasan went on to make 79 off 82 deliveries, while Farhan Yousaf’s 56 off 48 balls and Imran’s quickfire 23 helped Pakistan push past 300, which was proven enough for the side at the end.
