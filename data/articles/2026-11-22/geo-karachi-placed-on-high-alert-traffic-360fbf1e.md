Published November 22, 2026
KARACHI: Police have placed security on high alert across various parts of the port city ahead of planned protest demonstrations and rallies, officials said on Sunday.
Authorities have issued district-level instructions to maintain law and order and ensure preparedness for any emergency situation.
Heavy police contingents have been deployed in the Red Zone, while security arrangements have also been strengthened at Karachi Press Club and its surrounding areas.
Jeay Sindh Leader Committee has announced holding a rally in the port city today, while Sindh Taraqi Pasand Party (STP) has planned a rally on November 29, The News reported on Saturday.
The Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan (MQM-P) was also scheduled to hold a rally in the city on November 29; however, it later postponed the procession, citing concerns over the law and order situation.
Meanwhile, officials said that personnel from the police and other law enforcement agencies have been stationed at several key locations across the city.
In view of the planned protest rallies, containers have also been moved to FTC bridge, while security has been tightened at Numaish Chowrangi, Sharea Quaideen and Gurumandir, police officials said.
Police personnel have also been deployed in large numbers in Saddar, around Empress Market and along Sharea Faisal.
Karachi traffic police also announced traffic diversions and road closures in several parts of the city ahead of planned protest demonstrations and rallies.
According to traffic police, the road at Gurumandir will remain closed due to a protest by Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F).
Traffic coming from Jail Chowrangi towards Gurumandir will be diverted towards Peoples Chowrangi, while vehicles travelling from Liaquatabad towards Gurumandir will be redirected through Teen Hatti towards PIB Colony.
Separately, the road near CSD on Sharea Faisal has also been closed to general traffic, police said.
The traffic police have advised commuters to use alternative routes to avoid inconvenience and plan their journeys accordingly.
