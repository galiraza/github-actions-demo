Published November 22, 2026
LAHORE: Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi on Sunday issued a stern warning to a "minister" over what he said was repeated criticism, saying he had been patiently listening to the latter’s remarks for the past two-and-a-half years.
Speaking informally to Geo News, Naqvi, without naming the politician, said he had been commenting on him on television almost every day to "gain attention".
“The day I comment on this minister, he will not be able to show his face,” he said in a hard-hitting statement.
Defending the businessmen at the economic forum criticised by the said minister, the interior minister remarked that they were respectable citizens who had served as presidents and office-bearers of chambers of commerce at the federal and city levels.
He said the businessmen were also among the country’s highest taxpayers.
“If these businessmen are so bad, why do you take billions of rupees from them in the name of party funds at every election?” Naqvi questioned.
The debate over the creation of new provinces has intensified in recent days, with political leaders from the ruling coalition expressing differing views on the proposal.
The issue has also triggered an exchange of statements between PML-N and Pakistan Peoples Party leaders, with discussions focusing on the need for political consensus and the constitutional process for creating new provinces.
