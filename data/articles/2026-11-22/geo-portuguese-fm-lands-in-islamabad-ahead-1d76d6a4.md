Published November 22, 2026
Portuguese Minister of State and Foreign Affairs Paulo Rangel arrived in Islamabad on Sunday for a two-day visit to Pakistan at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar, the Foreign Office said.
The Portuguese minister is visiting the country from November 22 to 23, during which he will hold meetings with the country’s leadership, according to the FO.
Rangel is accompanied by a business delegation for the inaugural Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum.
At the Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum, companies from both countries will hold business-to-business meetings to explore commercial partnerships and investment opportunities in agri-food, renewable energy, pharmaceuticals, information technology and textiles.
The visit comes as Pakistan and Portugal mark the 77th anniversary of their diplomatic relations, established on November 7, 1949.
The FO said that DPM Dar will hold a one-on-one meeting with his Portuguese counterpart, followed by delegation-level talks. They will also hold a joint press stakeout.
According to the FO, the talks will also cover cooperation in agriculture, particularly olive cultivation and processing, as well as in renewable energy, maritime connectivity, migration and education.
The two leaders will also exchange views on regional and global developments of mutual interest. In line with their shared commitment to multilateralism, they will discuss cooperation at the United Nations and in other multilateral fora to promote peace and sustainable development.
Rangel's visit will provide an opportunity to build on the longstanding ties between Pakistan and Portugal and to broaden bilateral cooperation across diverse sectors.
