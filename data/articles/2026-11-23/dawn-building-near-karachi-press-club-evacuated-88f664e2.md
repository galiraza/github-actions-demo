# Building near Karachi Press Club evacuated due to hoax bomb threat

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 19:46:48 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046562/building-near-karachi-press-club-evacuated-due-to-hoax-bomb-threat
- Collected: 2026-11-23 18:56:11 UTC

---

KARACHI: A bomb threat at a building near Karachi Press Club (KPC) drew a response from multiple authorities on Monday but was found to have been a hoax, according to police.
Rangers, police and rescue teams rushed to the scene after receiving information that explosive material had been placed at Lakson Square Building No. 2, located opposite the main gate of KPC.
The building was vacated and after a thorough search by bomb experts, it was declared safe.
Deputy Inspector General (South) Syed Asad Raza told Dawn that the building management had received a threat via email that explosives had been placed.
He said the police, Rangers, Rescue 1122 and Bomb Disposal Squad teams rushed to the site. He said that two teams of bomb experts conducted a search of the building premises and found it to be a hoax call.
The South police chief said the bomb experts searched the building completely from the first to the 10th floor, in the presence of the building security staff and the local police.
Other areas of the building were also searched in response to the bomb threat, but no detonating or explosive material was found, he said.
Raza said the police were investigating the case further.
Earlier this month, a train was stopped and evacuated at Punjab’s Khanpur Railway Station following a threat that a bomb was planted on the Peshawar-bound Khyber Mail, travelling from Karachi.
According to the railway authorities, a suspicious bag was reported in one of the coaches of the train but no bomb or explosive material was found.
