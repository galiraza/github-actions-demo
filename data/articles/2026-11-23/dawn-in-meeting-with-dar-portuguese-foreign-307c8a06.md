# In meeting with Dar, Portuguese foreign minister reaffirms commitment to bolstering Islamabad-Lisbon ties

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 13:11:00 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046519/in-meeting-with-dar-portuguese-foreign-minister-reaffirms-commitment-to-bolstering-islamabad-lisbon-ties
- Collected: 2026-11-23 08:33:01 UTC

---

Portuguese Minister of State and Foreign Affairs Paulo Rangel reaffirmed on Monday his country’s commitment to further strengthening bilateral relations with Pakistan, a statement by the Foreign Office (FO) said.
Rangel made these remarks during a meeting with Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar at the FO in Islamabad, where the Portuguese minister arrived on Sunday on a two-day visit.
In today’s meeting, Rangel and Dar reviewed the “longstanding and friendly relations between Pakistan and Portugal as the two countries mark 77 years of diplomatic relations”, the FO statement said.
It added that DPM Dar stressed the importance of enhanced diplomatic engagement, parliamentary exchanges and regular consultations through existing bilateral mechanisms.
For his part, Rangel reaffirmed Portugal’s commitment to further strengthening bilateral ties, thanked Pakistan for its support at the United Nations and appreciated the contribution of the Pakistani diaspora in Portugal to promoting closer ties between the two countries, the statement said.
“Both leaders also exchanged views on regional developments and discussed cooperation at the UN and within the European Union framework,” it said.
Moreover, Rangel appreciated Pakistan’s “constructive role in promoting regional peace and stability”, the statement said, adding that the two leaders also emphasised the importance of dialogue, diplomacy and effective multilateralism in addressing shared challenges.
During his time in Pakistan, Rangel is also scheduled to hold delegation-level talks with DPM Dar. He is accompanied by a business delegation for the inaugural Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum.
The FO earlier said the visit would also feature a Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum, and companies from both countries would hold business-to-business meetings to explore commercial partnerships and investment opportunities in agri-food, renewable energy, pharmaceuticals, information technology and textiles.
Following his arrival on Sunday, Rangel praised Pakistan’s peace efforts in recent months during the US-Israeli war on Iran.
“The world needs you. We have been seeing in the very recent months that the role of Pakistan is decisive for global peace, so we count on you to help all the peoples of the world, reaching peace and prosperity,” he said while speaking to state-run Pakistan TV.
