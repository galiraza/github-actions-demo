# Naqvi complicated provinces debate by 'irrelevant speech at irrelevant forum', says Rana Sanaullah

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 23:51:24 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046575/naqvi-complicated-provinces-debate-by-irrelevant-speech-at-irrelevant-forum-says-rana-sanaullah
- Collected: 2026-11-23 18:56:11 UTC

---

PML-N Senator Rana Sanaullah on Monday said Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi “complicated” the matter of creating new provinces by making an “irrelevant speech at an irrelevant forum”.
In his fresh remarks, Sanaullah stood by his criticism of the interior minister’s choice to start the debate on provinces while addressing the Pakistan Economic Summit in Islamabad in July. Naqvi had whipped up a political storm by calling for creating new administrative units in the country to “reset” the current “system” of governance.
Following recent remarks by his predecessor, Naqvi, without naming Sanaullah, warned, “The day I comment on this minister, he will not be able to show his face.”
Subsequently, appearing on Geo News programme ‘Aaj Shahzeb Khanzada Kay Sath’ on Monday, Sanaullah said it was his right as a colleague and as a political worker to express his opinion on Naqvi’s work.
Sanaullah, also the prime minister’s political adviser, contended that Naqvi’s decision to make the remarks at the business forum “complicated this issue, and today this matter is in such a state that it has become extremely controversial”.
“And now if it comes to Parliament, it will arrive in a controversial state,” the senior politician observed, affirming that his party will make all possible efforts to build a consensus.
The PML-N leader maintained that he never commented on Naqvi’s “personality and thoughts” and never will.
“Is he Quaid-i-Azam that for two and a half to three years, I have supposedly been commenting on his personality and thoughts, and it just doesn’t seem to end?” he quipped.
“Whenever I have expressed an opinion, I have done so while keeping his respect and honour in mind. I have never addressed him as anything less than the worthy interior minister,” Sanaullah said, reusing the term from his previous interview.
Upon the programme host asking if he meant “worthy” — following chatter on social media about Sanaullah’s remark — the PML-N leader responded in the affirmative: “Definitely, worthy as in English.”
Expressing his opinion on Naqvi’s July speech, Sanaullah said the interior minister made “completely irrelevant remarks regarding the prime minister”.
He claimed that later, Naqvi also made indirect remarks about the military chief: “Field Marshal is a national asset and a national hero; [but] attributing this kind of talk to him indirectly, that he is a red line and that he does not want to cross it, so I objected to these things.”
The PML-N leader reiterated his stance that the matter of creating new provinces has to come before the Parliament sooner or later.
The PM’s adviser stressed the importance of building consensus among political parties, expressing hope that an agreement could be reached when the matter is debated in the Senate and the National Assembly.
Asked about other federal representatives, including Khawaja Asif, Tariq Fazal Chaudhry and Tallal Chaudhry, voicing their support for new provinces, Sanaullah said each minister was entitled to their opinion.
“Nothing has been achieved from [discussing] this at forums and seminars, apart from making this issue controversial,” the PML-N senator said.
Sanaullah also commented on PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari’s stance that neither he nor his father, President Asif Ali Zardari, had been approached by the government about the matter after the debate started.
“Then what have you (Naqvi) been doing for four months? At the place where you are present all the time, and he (President Zardari) is your benefactor as well and he holds you so dear; if you could not even take him on board, then this is your total failure,” he asserted.
