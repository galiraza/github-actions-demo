# PM to attend ECO moot in Iran after Russia trip

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 07:50:27 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046394/pm-to-attend-eco-moot-in-iran-after-russia-trip
- Collected: 2026-11-23 08:33:01 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif will undertake his first official three-day visit to Russia from Nov 30, aiming to strengthen bilateral ties and secure energy and investment agreements before travelling to Iran for the 18th Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Summit on Dec 3 and 4.
During his meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin, discussions are expected to focus on energy cooperation, defence, trade, regional security and investment, a source in the Prime Minister’s Office told Dawn.
The revival of Pakistan Steel Mills, established with Russian assistance, is also likely to feature prominently, alongside potential Russian investment in Pakistan’s oil and gas sectors.
The two countries are likely to examine possibilities for Russian cooperation in restoring the industrial facility, alongside broader initiatives aimed at supporting Pakistan’s industrial development.
Following the Russia visit, Prime Minister Shehbaz will represent Pakistan at the ECO summit in Tehran, where leaders of 10 member states will discuss regional trade, connectivity, energy, tran-sportation, investment and industrial cooperation.
The summit comes ahead of Pakistan assuming the ECO chairmanship for 2026-27.
