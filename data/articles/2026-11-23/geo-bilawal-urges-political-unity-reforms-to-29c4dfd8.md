KARACHI: Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has called for political unity and reforms to restore public confidence in state institutions, saying the country must overcome divisions to tackle its economic and other challenges.
Addressing a Youth Card ceremony in Karachi on Monday, Bilawal said politicians must maintain public hope and confidence in the system, warning that if political leaders themselves became disillusioned, they could not expect people to trust it.
“Rulers should abandon the politics of hatred and division and focus on public welfare,” he said, adding that the country could confront every crisis and disaster through unity but would fail if it remained divided.
He said restoring young people’s confidence in the system required a “democratic reset”, including the restoration of parliamentary supremacy and public trust in the judiciary.
Bilawal said the government and opposition must develop mutual trust, while the public should also have confidence in the system.
He stressed that political differences should be set aside in the national interest, particularly as the country faced dangerous inflation and difficult economic conditions.
The PPP chairman also called for the use of modern technology to improve transparency and close avenues for corruption, saying the system should be designed in a way that left no room for corrupt practices.
He said young people wanted a Pakistan free of corruption and that public services should be delivered without causing inconvenience to citizens.
Bilawal urged the government to prepare the country to face future economic challenges and take measures to protect people from an economic crisis.
