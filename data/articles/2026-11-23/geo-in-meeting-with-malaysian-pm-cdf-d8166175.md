Published November 23, 2026
Chief of Army Staff (COAS) and Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir undertook an official visit to Malaysia to strengthen bilateral defence and military cooperation, the military’s media wing said in a statement on Monday.
According to the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), CDF Munir called on Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim at Perdana Putra, Putrajaya, during the visit.
The meeting focused on bilateral relations, defence and security cooperation, and evolving regional and global security dynamics. Both leaders exchanged views on matters of mutual interest and underscored the importance of sustained engagement to promote regional peace, stability and security, it added.
The ISPR said that the Malaysian prime minister warmly welcomed the Field Marshal Munir and acknowledged the longstanding bonds of friendship, mutual trust and cooperation between Pakistan and Malaysia.
He reaffirmed Malaysia’s commitment to further strengthening bilateral relations, particularly in the fields of defence and security.
The CDF appreciated Malaysia’s constructive role in promoting peace and stability in Southeast Asia and reiterated Pakistan’s resolve to further deepen defence cooperation and institutional linkages between the armed forces of both countries, the ISPR stated.
"Both sides reaffirmed their shared commitment to strengthening bilateral defence ties through regular high-level exchanges, enhanced military cooperation and sustained dialogue on regional and international security issues," the statement said.
Upon arrival at the Ministry of Defence Malaysia (MINDEF), Kuala Lumpur, the Field Marshal was accorded a ceremonial Guard of Honour.
General Tan Sri Haji Malek Razak Sulaiman, Chief of Defence Force of Malaysia, along with the Chiefs of the Malaysian Army, Royal Malaysian Navy and Royal Malaysian Air Force, were present on the occasion.
During his meeting with Malaysian Defence Minister Mohamed Khaled Nordin, both sides discussed matters of mutual interest, with particular emphasis on expanding bilateral defence cooperation, strengthening military-to-military engagements and exploring new avenues of collaboration between the armed forces of the two countries.
The Field Marshal will also visit the National Defence Studies Centre (PUSPAHANAS), Malaysia, where a formal ceremony will be held to induct him as the 21st member of its prestigious Hall of Fame, the ISPR added.
