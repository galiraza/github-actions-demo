# Naqvi, PML-Q's Shujaat discuss new provinces, devolution of powers

Published November 23, 2026
Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi met Pakistan Muslim League-Quaid (PML-Q) chief Chaudhry Shujaat Hussain at his residence in Lahore on Monday, with discussions focusing on the creation of new provinces and the devolution of powers.
During the meeting, they discussed the overall country’s situation, the government's measures to maintain law and order and the devolution of powers to the grassroots level.
The debate over the creation of new provinces has gained momentum in recent days, with leaders of ruling coalition parties expressing differing views on the proposal.
The debate gained momentum following Naqvi’s remarks in July, when he called for the creation of new administrative units, saying the existing system of governance had "collapsed" and needed a "reset".
In today's meeting, the interior minister also briefed the PML-Q chief on measures taken to counter terrorism and maintain peace in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
The creation of new provinces is directly linked to public convenience, better governance and administrative efficiency, said the PML-Q chief, adding that strengthening and empowering administrative units could be an effective way forward.
"Pakistan's biggest stakeholders are its ordinary people," he said, adding that if the right steps were not taken today, the future would not forgive us.
Naqvi, meanwhile, said taking all decisions in accordance with the will of the people was the need of the hour.
Meanwhile, Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan said the creation of new provinces requires a constitutional amendment and cannot be achieved through a referendum, while stressing that the matter required consensus among political parties.
"Establishing new provinces requires a two-thirds majority in both the National Assembly and the Senate," Gohar said while talking to the media in Islamabad.
Calling the issue highly sensitive, he said all political parties would have to be taken into confidence to evolve a consensus on the matter.
The PTI chairman said the party would give its position after the government made public its proposed draft of the constitutional amendment. He added that the party's political and legal committees would hold consultation on the proposed legislation.
"We will decide our future course of action in light of the instructions of the PTI founding chairman [Imran Khan]," he said.
Gohar also clarified that Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi had not discussed the issue of new provinces with the PTI.
