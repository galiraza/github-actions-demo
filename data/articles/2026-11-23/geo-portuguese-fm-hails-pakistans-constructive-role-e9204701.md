Published November 23, 2026
Portugal’s Foreign Minister Paulo Rangel appreciated Pakistan’s constructive role in promoting regional peace and stability as he held a tête-à-tête with Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar in Islamabad, the Foreign Office said in a statement on Monday.
The Portuguese foreign minister arrived in Islamabad a day earlier for a two-day visit to Pakistan at the invitation of DPM Dar, as per the FO statement.
The Portuguese minister is visiting the country from November 22 to 23, during which he will hold meetings with the country’s leadership.
In the meeting today, the FO said the two leaders reviewed the longstanding and friendly relations between Pakistan and Portugal as the two countries mark 77 years of diplomatic relations.
DPM Dar underscored the importance of enhanced diplomatic engagement, parliamentary exchanges and regular consultations through existing bilateral mechanisms.
FM Rangel reaffirmed Portugal’s commitment to further strengthening bilateral relations and thanked Pakistan for its support at the UN.
The Portuguese foreign minister also appreciated the contribution of the Pakistani diaspora in Portugal to promoting closer ties between the two countries.
"Both leaders also exchanged views on regional developments and discussed cooperation at the UN and within the EU framework," the FO said.
The two officials underscored the importance of dialogue, diplomacy and effective multilateralism in addressing shared challenges.
Rangel is accompanied by a business delegation for the inaugural Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum.
At the Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum, companies from both countries will hold business-to-business meetings to explore commercial partnerships and investment opportunities in agri-food, renewable energy, pharmaceuticals, information technology and textiles.
Addressing a cultural evening at a local hotel in Islamabad on Sunday, FM Rangel said Portugal desires to build a very strong economic and cultural relationship with Pakistan.
He said Portugal will join the United Nations Security Council in January 2027 and will assume its presidency in the same month, adding that Pakistan and Portugal have been holding extensive discussions in this regard.
Minister Rangel said that on the bilateral front, Portugal wants to build a very strong economic relationship with Pakistan.
