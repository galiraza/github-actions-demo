# Collision between bus, truck kills 11 in UAE

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 11:45:05 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046739/collision-between-bus-truck-kills-11-in-uae
- Collected: 2026-11-24 10:39:25 UTC

---

Eleven people were killed when a bus and a truck collided in Ras Al Khaimah in the United Arab Emirates (UAE), authorities in the Gulf nation said on Tuesday.
“Investigations into the circumstances and causes of the accident are ongoing,” the Ministry of Interior stated on its X account.
According to the ministry, emergency teams were dispatched to the accident site immediately after receiving the report. They launched rescue and emergency response operations in coordination with ambulance services, Civil Defence and other relevant authorities.
The response also included securing the scene and managing traffic, it added.
The ministry said the findings of the investigation would be announced once it had been completed.
It also expressed condolences to the families of the deceased, praying for mercy and forgiveness for the victims and patience and strength for their loved ones.
The ministry urged the public to exercise caution, rely on official sources for information and refrain from circulating rumours or unverified reports about the accident.
It is the second major road accident to occur in the UAE this month.
On November 12, a multi-vehicle pile-up in Fujairah killed 14 people and left 30 others injured after a truck driver lost control of his vehicle and struck several other vehicles.
