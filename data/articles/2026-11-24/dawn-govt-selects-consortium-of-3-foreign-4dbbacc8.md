# Govt selects consortium of 3 foreign banks for $500m finance facility backed by MIGA

- Published: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 21:12:42 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-24 18:35:42 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: The government on Tuesday selected a consortium of three foreign banks to arrange a $500 million trade finance facility, backed by the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA), a member of the World Bank Group.
“Following a competitive selection process and receipt of bids from nine international financial institutions, the Finance Division, Government of Pakistan, has selected the following banks as financing partners for the facility,” the Ministry of Finance said in a statement.
The banks included Commerzbank, JP Morgan and Standard Chartered Bank. It added that this facility opened new avenues for international financing.
According to the ministry, the decision was taken in a virtual meeting held by Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb with the senior leadership of a consortium of international banks and representatives of the agency.
The banks were selected on the basis of the most competitive financing terms offered to the government, it said.
“The one-year facility, renewable on a rolling 1+1+1 basis, will support the financing of essential imports and trade-related expenditures,” the statement said.
It noted that the transaction marked the government’s first-ever credit enhancement transaction with MIGA under its Trade Finance programme, while also representing the first-ever participation of Commerzbank and JP Morgan in commercial lending to the government.
“The strong response and participation from international financial institutions reflect growing confidence in Pakistan’s improving economic outlook and progress towards sustainable growth,” the ministry said, expressing the government’s appreciation for the interest shown by all participating institutions and its gratitude “for their time, effort and engagement throughout the process”.
It said the transaction marked an important step towards “mobilising private international capital through multilateral risk-mitigation instruments, diversifying external financing sources and exploring innovative financing structures, while maintaining prudent debt management and fiscal responsibility”.
The ministry said the government would work closely with MIGA and the selected banks to advance the transaction towards its timely execution, looking forward to building strong and lasting relationships.
