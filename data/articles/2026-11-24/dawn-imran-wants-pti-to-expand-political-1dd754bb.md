# Imran wants PTI to expand political alliance, extend outreach to Fazl and other opposition parties, core committee told

ISLAMABAD: The PTI’s core committee has been informed that incarcerated party founder Imran Khan wanted it to expand its “political alliance” and extend outreach to Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman and other opposition parties.
Imran’s instructions were conveyed to the committee during an overnight meeting that began on Monday and during which lawyer Latif Khosa briefed the members on his meeting with Imran and his spouse, Bushra Bibi, at the Adiala jail earlier this month.
Khosa, along with another of Imran’s lawyers, Salman Safdar, was allowed to meet Imran and Bushra Bibi on Nov 11. The meeting, which was said to have lasted three hours, included discussions on Imran’s health, cases against him and the possible 28th constitutional amendment, the lawyers had said.
The PTI issued a statement after Khosa briefed the party’s core committee on the meeting.
“The core committee was informed that Imran Khan had expressed his complete confidence in the opposition leadership. He directed that the political alliance be further expanded and that outreach be extended to Maulana Fazlur Rehman and other opposition parties, with the objective of forming a broader political alliance and initiating vigorous political activities,” the statement said.
Early in November, leaders of opposition alliance Tehreek-i-Tahaffuz-i-Ayeen-i-Pakistan, which includes the PTI, had met Fazl and the two sides had agreed to strengthen their alliance and parliamentary coordination.
Opposition parties appear to have increased coordination after the Oct 11 imposition of emergency and governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — where the PTI had been in power — which also drew criticism from Fazl.
Meanwhile, tensions between the JUI-F chief and the federal government have escalated since mid-October, with Fazl accusing the Centre of reneging on its commitments despite his efforts to mediate between the ruling PML-N in the Centre and the PTI.
New provinces
Khosa also briefed the PTI core committee meeting, which was chaired by party chairperson Barrister Gohar Ali Khan, on Imran’s instruction regarding the debate on creating new provinces.
The committee was informed that Imran had instructed that the matter concerning the creation of new provinces be “deliberated upon in detail at the forthcoming meeting of PTI’s parliamentary party”.
The recommendations formulated following those deliberations would subsequently be presented to Imran for his decision, the statement said.
Imran’s health
The committee was also briefed on Imran’s health and “expressed serious concern over their medical condition” and demanded the immediate implementation of the Supreme Court’s decision of Aug 18, in which the authorities had been directed to transfer Imran to Shifa International Hospital, a private health facility in Islamabad.
The committee also demanded the immediate implementation of judicial orders concerning Bushra Bibi’s medical treatment.
Earlier this month, the Islamabad High Court had directed proper medical care and hygienic living conditions for Bushra Bibi, in line with prison rules.
The statement also referred to a recent letter written by Imran to top judges, saying that the core committee expressed “deep concern over the apprehensions raised by Imran Khan regarding his health and medical treatment”.
Reiterating its previous demands, the committee “urged the honourable judges to take immediate and serious notice of the matters raised in Imran Khan’s letter and ensure the prompt implementation of judicial orders concerning his medical treatment”, the statement said.
The committee also called on “the chief justice to […] ensure unrestricted access of Imran Khan’s personal physicians to him and facilitate his comprehensive medical examination and appropriate treatment”.
“Imran Khan must also be granted access to his family members and legal representatives. The committee emphasised that his health requires urgent judicial attention and that any further delay is unacceptable,” the statement said.
Detentions
Moreover, the committee “strongly condemned the continued detention” of Sohail Afridi — who has been rendered non-functional as the KP chief minister following the imposition of governor’s rule — and other PTI lawmakers.
“The committee particularly condemned the extension of detention orders under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO) against elected representatives and political workers” and maintained that the “continued detention of elected representatives and political workers, and restrictions on their political activities, are contrary to fundamental constitutional, democratic and human rights”, the statement said.
The core committee appealed to the Federal Constitutional Court and its chief justice to “suitably modify the existing stay order concerning Section 3 of the MPO and provide relief to political leaders and workers who had been granted bail by the Peshawar High Court before Nov 5 but whose release orders remain unimplemented owing to the stay order”.
The committee demanded the immediate removal of legal impediments preventing their release, the statement said.
The committee also decided to observe Nov 26 as the “Martyrs’ Day” to commemorate the loss of lives during the 2024 protests.
In the protests in Islamabad on Nov 26, 2024, deaths were reported among both the protesters and the security personnel.
