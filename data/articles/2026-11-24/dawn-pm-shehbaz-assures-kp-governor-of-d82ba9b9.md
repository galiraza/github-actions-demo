# PM Shehbaz assures KP governor of continued support to eliminate terrorism

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 21:12:55 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046786/pm-shehbaz-assures-kp-governor-of-continued-support-to-eliminate-terrorism
- Collected: 2026-11-24 18:35:42 UTC

---

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday assured KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi of all possible cooperation on behalf of the federal government for the complete eradication of terrorism, durable peace and the welfare of the public in the province.
The premier made these remarks as he met with Kundi in Islamabad on Tuesday, according to a statement by the Prime Minister’s Office (PMO).
During the meeting, he said that the sacrifices of the security forces and KP police in the war against terrorism in the province would not go in vain.
The federal government would continue to “fully support the implementation of the decisions taken at the apex committee meeting to completely eliminate terrorism”, he was quoted as saying.
He reaffirmed the federal government’s commitment to providing full support for the development of KP and the welfare of its people, saying that all possible resources would be mobilised to provide them with healthcare, education and other basic facilities, according to the PMO.
The premier directed that special attention be given to improving social development indicators in KP, including healthcare and education.
He said that attention would also be given to promoting sports facilities in the province and providing young people with opportunities for constructive activities, the PMO added.
Meanwhile, Governor Kundi briefed the prime minister on the ongoing efforts by himself and the KP Advisory Council to improve law and order in the province.
Separately, Kundi also met with President Asif Ali Zardari, a statement by the President’s Office said, where he briefed the president on the situation following Oct 11, particularly the security situation.
The meetings come weeks after the Centre imposed emergency and governor’s rule in KP on Oct 11, citing the provincial government’s “deliberate disengagement” from the fight against terrorism. The decisions were also endorsed by the Parliament amid protests by the opposition.
On Oct 22, PM Shehbaz also chaired a meeting of the federal apex committee in Peshawar. He had vowed to take tough decisions, while reaffirming the Centre’s commitment to bringing peace to terrorism-hit districts in the province.
Last week, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir also reaffirmed the security forces “unwavering resolve” to decisively eliminate terrorism and preserve peace and stability of KP in close coordination with law enforcement agencies.
