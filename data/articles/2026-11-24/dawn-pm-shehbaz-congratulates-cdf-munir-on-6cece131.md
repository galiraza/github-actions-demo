# PM Shehbaz congratulates CDF Munir on Hall of Fame induction at Malaysia’s National Defence Studies Centre

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 14:15:14 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046749/pm-shehbaz-congratulates-cdf-munir-on-hall-of-fame-induction-at-malaysias-national-defence-studies-centre
- Collected: 2026-11-24 10:39:25 UTC

---

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday congratulated Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir on his induction into the Hall of Fame at Malaysia’s National Defence Studies Centre.
“This distinction also reflects the enduring friendship between Pakistan and Malaysia,” the premier said on X.
“Field Marshal’s resolute leadership during Marka-i-Haq, unwavering commitment to national defence, invaluable contributions to promoting peace, and enhancing Pakistan’s standing internationally makes this honour truly befitting,” he added.
CDF Munir is on an official visit to Malaysia. According to the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), the visit aims to further strengthen bilateral defence and military cooperation between the two countries.
On Monday, ISPR said CDF Munir would also visit the National Defence Studies Centre, where a formal ceremony would be held to induct him as the 21st member of its “prestigious” Hall of Fame.
According to PM Shehbaz, the recognition belongs equally to the “brave” men and women of Pakistan’s armed forces, “whose courage, dedication and sacrifices safeguard our beloved homeland, and to the people of Pakistan, whose resilience and unwavering support remain our greatest strength”.
In a meeting with Malaysian PM Anwar Ibrahim on Monday, CDF Munir reaffirmed their shared commitment to strengthening bilateral defence ties in Putrajaya. CDF Munir also held a meeting with Malaysian Defence Minister Mohamed Khaled bin Nordin.
Last week, Malaysian High Commissioner Datuk Mohammad Azhar Mazlan reaffirmed his country’s commitment to strengthening its strategic partnership with Pakistan, highlighting longstanding political, economic, defence and cultural ties between the two countries.
He was addressing a gathering of Pakistani media representatives and members of the Malaysian community at the Malaysian High Commission.
In August, Pakistan and Malaysia agreed to establish a joint working group between their interior ministries to promote institutional cooperation, with a focus on counter-terrorism, counter-narcotics, cybercrime and the prevention of illegal immigration.
