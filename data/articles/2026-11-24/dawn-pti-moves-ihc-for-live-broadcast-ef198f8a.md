# PTI moves IHC for live broadcast of Al-Qadir Trust case hearing

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 13:41:47 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-24 10:39:25 UTC

ISLAMABAD: The Pakistan Tehreek-i-Insaf (PTI) has filed a petition in the Islamabad High Court (IHC), seeking live broadcast of the appeal proceedings in the £190 million Al-Qadir Trust case involving party founder Imran Khan.
Imran and his wife, Bushra Bibi, were convicted and sentenced in January 2025 in the corruption case. An accountability court found them guilty of receiving billions of rupees and hundreds of kanals of land from a real estate firm in exchange for legalising Rs50bn that had been identified and returned to Pakistan by the UK during the previous PTI government.
The petition was filed on Tuesday through Barristers Salman Safdar, Salman Akram Raja and other lawyers.
It argues that the case is a matter of national importance and that live broadcasting of Imran’s appeal proceedings is essential to ensure transparency, uphold justice and maintain public confidence in the judicial system.
Live coverage would allow the public to witness the proceedings firsthand and dispel speculation and misinformation, the petition states. It adds that justice must not only be done but must also be seen to be done.
The petition invokes Article 19-A of the Constitution, which guarantees citizens the right to access information on matters of public importance.
It also cites the live broadcast of the Zulfikar Ali Bhutto reference and proceedings in the Pervez Musharraf case before the Supreme Court. It notes that the IHC has previously issued directions for the live broadcast of proceedings in significant cases.
The petition also raises concerns about Imran’s alleged solitary confinement in prison, the denial of adequate medical facilities and restrictions on meetings with his family members and legal representatives.
It expresses concern over what it describes as prison authorities’ negligence regarding his medical condition, including blood pressure and treatment.
It further points out that details of the verdict and sentence in the Al-Qadir Trust case were reported in the media even before the formal announcement of the judgment, raising questions about the transparency of the judicial process.
The petition argues that live broadcasting would allow an independent assessment of all legal aspects of the case and requested the IHC to order the live broadcast of the complete proceedings of the appeal.
IHC has fixed November 25 for hearing appeals filed by Imran and Bushra against their convictions.
According to the cause list issued by the registrar’s office, a division bench comprising Chief Justice Sardar Mohammad Sarfraz Dogar and Justice Muhammad Asif will take up the appeals.
The PTI founder and Bushra Bibi have challenged their convictions in the high-profile case and sought relief from the high court.
Last month, senior members of the PTI legal team approached the Federal Constitutional Court (FCC), seeking early fixation of a criminal appeal filed by Bushra Bibi in the case.
The FCC rejected her plea seeking suspension of her seven-year sentence, observing that there were no sufficient grounds to interfere with an April 30 IHC order declining to suspend her sentence while her appeal remained pending.
Bushra, along with Imran, had initially moved the IHC for the suspension of her sentence in July last year. But the high court dismissed their requests in May 2026, observing that their appeals against their convictions had already been fixed for hearing.
