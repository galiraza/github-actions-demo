# Rohail Nazir's fifty books Asia Cup Rising Stars semi-finals spot for Pakistan Shaheens

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 22:04:33 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046798/rohail-nazirs-fifty-books-asia-cup-rising-stars-semi-finals-spot-for-pakistan-shaheens
- Collected: 2026-11-24 18:35:42 UTC

---

An unbeaten half-century by Rohail Nazir, followed by a devastating bowling display, helped Pakistan Shaheens defeat Nepal by 96 runs in their final Group ‘B’ match of the Asia Cup Rising Stars at the University of Doha for Science and Technology on Tuesday.
The defending champions remained unbeaten in the tournament, winning all three of their group matches to qualify for the semi-finals, where they will face Bangladesh A on Nov 27.
Batting first, Pakistan Shaheens were in trouble at 66-6 in 16.5 overs before wicket-keeper-batter Rohail steadied the innings with an unbeaten 64 off 83 balls, hitting four fours and three sixes.
After Hasan Nawaz fell for 28 off 59 balls, leaving the Shaheens at 88-7, Rohail added 70 runs for the eighth wicket with Ahmed Daniyal, who scored 16 off 35 balls, to help his side reach 168 before being bowled out in 42 overs.
For Nepal, Rashid Khan claimed five wickets for 39 runs, while Abhishesh Gautam took two.
Nepal’s chase got off to a disastrous start as Muhammad Imran and Mohammad Hasnain reduced them to 15-4 inside six overs. Hasnain struck twice in successive deliveries to dismiss Ishan Pandey and Lokesh Bam without scoring.
Left-arm spinner Sufyan Moqim then claimed three wickets as Nepal slumped to 59-9. Mayan Yadav offered some resistance with a top score of 41 off 50 balls but was trapped leg-before by Maaz Sadaqat as Nepal were dismissed for 72 in 17.3 overs.
Hasnain and Sufyan took three wickets each for 21 and 17 runs respectively, while Imran claimed two for 29.
Rohail was named player of the match for his match-winning innings.
