# Rana Sanaullah says referendum cannot create new provinces

- Source: Geo
- Published: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 22:31:00 +05:00
- Link: https://www.geo.tv/latest/692504-rana-sanaullah-says-referendum-cannot-create-new-provinces
- Collected: 2026-11-24 18:35:42 UTC

---

Published November 24, 2026
Adviser to the Prime Minister on Political Affairs Rana Sanaullah has said a referendum cannot lead to the creation of new provinces, stressing that any such change must be made through a constitutional process.
"Referendum is a means of seeking guidance from the people," he said while speaking on Geo News programme "Capital Talk" on Tuesday, adding that there was nothing wrong with seeking public opinion when differences existed over the issue.
His remarks come amid growing debate over the creation of new provinces, with political leaders from the ruling coalition expressing differing views on the proposal.
Sanaullah said that the debate over new provinces had not started in the right manner, adding that the issue should be brought before parliament so all stakeholders could present their views.
"If this matter had been initiated in parliament on 30 July, we would have reached a conclusion by now," he said.
He maintained that differences over the creation of new provinces could only be resolved when the matter was formally discussed in parliament. He added that the concerns of different provinces would be addressed once the issue came before the legislature.
Commenting on the Pakistan Peoples Party's (PPP) position, Sanaullah said the party had made its stance on the provinces issue clear.
According to Sanaullah, the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz's (PML-N) leadership was trying to bring all sides towards an agreement acceptable to them.
He said that the debate involved other matters, including administrative units and the federal structure, besides the creation of new provinces.
He added that the PML-N would convene a meeting and make a decision through consultation when the matter reached a decisive stage.
Addressing his differences with Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, Sanaullah said he had previously tried to avoid making remarks about him.
He said he had reservations about Naqvi's comments concerning "showing and hiding one's face".
The PML-N leader clarified that he had neither a personal dispute with Naqvi nor any intention of developing one, adding that they had known each other for 22 to 25 years.
He also said that Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif had directed participants at a meeting today to reduce public statements and exchanges of remarks.
Earlier today, PM Shehbaz directed members of the federal cabinet to refrain from making political remarks against one another.
In a statement issued by the Prime Minister's Office (PMO), the premier said the federal government's entire focus was on public welfare projects and that Pakistan was continuing its journey from economic stability towards sustainable growth.
The prime minister directed all ministers to avoid political statements against their colleagues, stressing that matters within the cabinet should be resolved through mutual understanding and consensus.
"Statements by federal ministers against one another create a negative impression and divert attention from the politics of development and progress," he said.
