# Achakzai proposes national govt, comprehensive reforms

LAHORE: Tehreek Tahafuz Ayeen-i-Pakistan (TTAP) head and Leader of the Opposition in the National Assembly Mahmood Khan Achakzai on Tuesday called for the formation of a national government and comprehensive constitutional and political reforms to steer the country out of the mounting crises it was facing.
Addressing a multi-party conference at the Javed Iqbal Auditorium of the Lahore High Court Bar Association (LHCBA), he said decisions having national importance should be made through consultations among elected representatives and all democratic political forces, instead of being left to a handful of individuals.
He stressed the need for constitutional supremacy, parliamentary sovereignty and “restoration of democracy”, saying Lahore and its bar associations had historically played a significant role in the struggle for constitutional rule.
Expressing concern over the deteriorating law and order situation, Achakzai said unrest and bloodshed continuing in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan had now spread to Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK) as well.

Deploring daily casualties in the troubled areas, he said protecting citizens’ life and property should be the foremost responsibility of the state institutions and political leadership.
Referring to PTI founder Imran Khan, Achakzai said the people had given him a majority mandate which was subsequently taken away.
He expressed concern over Imran’s political circumstances and health, alleging that attempts are being made to break his resolve and isolate him. He said that he and Senate opposition leader Allama Raja Nasir Abbas would not allow such attempts to succeed.
Proposing a national government, comprising major state institutions, political parties and other stakeholders, he said the new setup should address the past injustices, introduce constitutional reforms and provide the military a “constitutional and dignified exit from politics”.
He also condemned the alleged detention of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman in Lahore and the denial of permission to attend the conference.
Speaking on the occasion, Allama Abbas said the country needed to return to constitutional rule and respect the people’s mandate, adding that freedom and fundamental rights were divine and nobody had the authority to take them away.
He questioned whether jailed PTI leaders Dr Yasmin Rashid, Umar Sarfraz Cheema, Ejaz Chaudhry and Aleema Khan were terrorists. He also asked about the whereabouts of the suspended KP Chief Minister Sohail Afridi and provincial assembly speaker.
Questioning the federal government’s approach towards the provinces, Abbas asked whether it intended to decide unilaterally how the federating units should remain part of the federation.
He said the existing system had failed as it offered nothing to the poor.
Former president of the Supreme Court Bar Association, Hamid Khan, and other bar leaders also addressed the conference.
