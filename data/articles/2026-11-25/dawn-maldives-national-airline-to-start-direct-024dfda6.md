# Maldives national airline to start direct flights to Pakistan soon, meeting told

ISLAMABAD: Maldivian, the national airline of the Maldives, will soon start direct flights to Pakistan in a bid to boost tourism, trade and people-to-people contacts between the two countries, it emerged on Wednesday,
The announcement was made during a meeting of the Maldives Tourism and Civil Aviation Minister Mohamed Ameen with Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi and Minister of State for Interior, Tallal Chaudry, as per a statement issued by the ministry.
An official launch date and flight schedule have not been announced yet.
“Direct flights will make travel to Maldives much easier for Pakistani citizens,” Naqvi said at the meeting.
He said Pakistani citizens have a “deep interest” in tourism and direct flights would facilitate Pakistani tourists in the Maldives. Well-known Pakistani cricketers, artists and other celebrities will travel to the Maldives on the first direct flight, the meeting was told.
“Pakistani celebrities are extremely popular in Maldives; their arrival will promote tourism,” the Maldivian minister said.
Pakistan and the Maldives also agreed to enhance cooperation in tourism, sports, health, trade, manpower and other sectors, the statement said.
The Maldives minister praised Islamabad’s modern infrastructure and cleanliness system during the meeting.
“I am extremely happy to see Islamabad’s rapid development, beauty and natural scenery,” he was quoted as saying during the meeting.
“Islamabad truly holds great potential as a tourism capital city.”
Naqvi added that the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) was working on a proposed project to build a cricket stadium in the Maldives.
A cricket academy will also be established along with the stadium, which will provide training opportunities to youth, the statement quoted the interior minister as saying.
The Maldives would also welcome manpower from Pakistan, Ameen said, adding that there were vast opportunities for imports of construction material, medicines and other goods from Pakistan, as per the statement.
Naqvi was quoted as saying that he was looking forward to the Maldivian interior minister’s visit to Pakistan.
Pakistan and the Maldives celebrated the 60th anniversary of their diplomatic relations, established in 1966, in October.
To mark the anniversary, Maldivian President Dr Mohamed Muizzu travelled to Islamabad on a three-day visit last month, marking the first by a Maldivian president to Pakistan in 11 years.
During President Muizzu’s visit, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said he had issued instructions for direct flights between Pakistan and the Maldives.
On the occasion, PM Shehbaz had said, “I want to say that connectivity is very important. We don’t have direct flights from Malé to Islamabad or Karachi, so this air link is very important.
“I have already given instructions to our ministry of civil aviation to prepare a quick feasibility [study], be it Pakistan International Airlines, or any other airline in Pakistan, to find the ways and means to take the first flight comprising tourists [and the] business community to Malé,” he announced.
The premier said he had been informed by President Muizzu that Muizzu had given instructions “to his airline to work out a quick plan to start flights from [the] Maldives to Pakistan”.
