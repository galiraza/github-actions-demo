# PM approves NCCIA restructuring, orders overhaul of cybercrime prevention system

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 13:55:46 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046984/pm-approves-nccia-restructuring-orders-overhaul-of-cybercrime-prevention-system
- Collected: 2026-11-25 09:29:58 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Wednesday ordered the modernisation of the system to prevent cybercrime in the country and approved the restructuring of the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA).
A meeting, presided over by the premier, was held to review the NCCIA’s performance, administrative structure and future strategy.
Speaking at the meeting, the prime minister said efforts to prevent and investigate cybercrime should be modernised in line with contemporary requirements.
“Protecting the public from cybercrime is among the government’s top priorities,” the prime minister said. “Restructuring of the NCCIA should be expedited to transform it into an effective, proactive and modern institution.”
The premier directed the appointment of information technology (IT) and artificial intelligence (AI) experts at the NCCIA and all its subordinate offices. “The NCCIA should recruit the best available workforce strictly on merit,” he said.
The prime minister said the agency’s restructuring should focus on the use of AI and modern technologies. He added that all available resources should be utilised to resolve pending cybercrime cases.
He also ordered the formation of a committee comprising Federal Ministers Mohsin Naqvi, Azam Nazeer Tarar, Muhammad Aurangzeb and Shaza Fatima Khawaja to review the reform proposals submitted by the NCCIA.
The committee will submit its recommendations to the prime minister by the end of this week.
The meeting was informed that a proposal had been made to transform the NCCIA into a national-level Pakistan Cyber Police, with recruitment based strictly on merit. A dedicated system for national security in relation to cybercrime has also become operational.
The meeting was further told that all possible measures were being taken under the existing system to resolve cybercrime cases.
The NCCIA’s performance, administrative structure and future strategy were also reviewed.
Federal Ministers Azam Nazeer Tarar, Musadik Malik, Ahsan Khan Cheema, Muhammad Aurangzeb and Mohsin Naqvi attended the meeting.
Minister of State for Interior Tallal Chaudhry, Minister of State for Finance Bilal Azhar Kiani and senior officials of relevant institutions were also present.
On Friday, NCCIA claimed to have uncovered an extensive alleged financial network involved in illegal online gambling, fraudulent business operations and suspected money laundering, with an estimated transaction exposure of up to Rs1 trillion.
Earlier this month, the agency called for stricter regulations governing company registration and bank account verification, warning that loopholes in the existing system are being exploited by illegal call centres, online betting operators and international fraud networks to launder money and defraud victims.
Earlier this month, Naqvi sought the premier’s help in addressing the acute shortage of staff in the NCCIA.
