# Under-construction building collapses near Karachi's Baloch Colony

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 14:27:59 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2046989/under-construction-building-collapses-near-karachis-baloch-colony
- Collected: 2026-11-25 09:29:58 UTC

---

A multi-storey under construction building collapsed near Karachi’s Baloch Colony area on Wednesday, with reports of people being trapped under the rubble.
A Rescue 1122 spokesperson said the department received reports of a building collapse near Karachi’s Baloch Colony, close to Sharea Faisal. The agency’s Central Command and Control Centre subsequently dispatched an urban search and rescue team, along with a disaster response vehicle and an ambulance, to the site.
Edhi Foundation said its ambulances and rescue teams had been dispatched to the incident site.
