# At least three dead, 14 injured as under-construction building collapses in Karachi

Published November 25, 2026
KARACHI: At least three people died and 14 others injured after the upper floors of an under-construction six-storey building collapsed near Baloch Colony in the port city, officials said on Wednesday.
Rescue officials initially reported one death and 10 injuries after parts of the building gave way.
They said 10 injured people had been pulled out of the rubble, including one who was shifted to hospital in critical condition.
Meanwhile, a young worker was also rescued from the rubble, officials said.
The rescued worker, identified as Ahmed, told rescuers that he was working on the fifth floor when the incident occurred. He added that several of his colleagues were also present on the same floor.
Officials at the Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC) said 14 injured people had been brought to the hospital, nine of whom were discharged after receiving treatment.
Heavy machinery has been called in to clear the debris as rescue efforts continue for those still reported trapped beneath the rubble.
Three of the injured individuals have been identified as Nadir Hussain, Shaukat Ali and Ahmed, rescue officials said.
Mayor Karachi Murtaza Wahab reviewed relief operations at the site of the incident and told the media that no negligence would be tolerated in rescue efforts.
He said rescuing those trapped under the debris was the priority of the authorities.
The mayor said the demolition of the building could not be carried out due to a court’s stay order, stressing that the government “must have the full right to take action” against illegal activities.
Wahab said action must be taken against illegal activities wherever they occur “without any discrimination”.
The Karachi mayor said that several people have been arrested over illegal construction of the building, calling for tough decisions to address the issue.
He said he would also speak to the chief minister and the provincial minister for local government regarding action against illegal construction.
Sindh minister Saeed Ghani said labourers were working at the site when the incident happened. He said efforts were underway to rescue the trapped individuals.
The rescue officials said the building started shaking before it collapsed, adding that no one was residing in it.
In a statement, the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) said the building collapsed in Admin Society Block 4, near Baloch Colony area of the metropolis.
It said the building was constructed on a 600-square-yard plot and a ground-plus-five-storey building was under construction on the plot.
Commercial construction was allegedly carried out on a residential plot, it added.
Meanwhile, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif expressed grief over the collapse of a building and the death of one citizen in the incident.
In a statement, the premier prayed for the forgiveness of the deceased and expressed sympathy with the family.
He also called for providing medical assistance to the injured on a priority basis.
Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah visited the site later in the evening, where he received a briefing from officials about the incident and the ongoing response.
During his visit, he questioned officials about how the illegal construction had been allowed to proceed.
Officials responded that stay orders were obtained for the construction activities. However, the chief minister questioned the circumstances surrounding the building's construction.
"This is murder. Take action against those responsible," CM Murad told the officials.
He said initial information suggested that permission had been granted for a ground-plus-one structure, but six floors had subsequently been constructed.
He further maintained that such extensive illegal construction would not have been possible without the involvement or connivance of government officials.
The Sindh chief minister said the building's owner, consultant, contractor and government officials involved in the matter were equally responsible for the deaths.
He vowed to ensure strict punishment for those responsible for the loss of precious lives and justice for the affected families.
Meanwhile, CM Murad said authorities were facing difficulties in establishing the exact number of missing people because different contractors had been working at the site.
He added that around 10 people were reportedly missing based on information provided by affected families.
