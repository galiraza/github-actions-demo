Published November 26, 2026
Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) has alleged that Punjab Police arrested its senior leaders, including party secretary-general and MNA Maulana Abdul Ghafoor Haideri and Punjab chief Maulana Amjad Khan, in Khushab ahead of its planned public gathering in Sargodha on November 26.
Party spokesperson Aslam Ghauri also claimed that police had detained Jamiat Talaba-e-Islam central president Malik Owais and were conducting raids at the residences of other party office-bearers.
Police have yet to confirm the reported arrests of the JUI-F leaders.
The latest confrontation between the government and JUI-F follows a prolonged deterioration in relations between Maulana Fazlur Rehman and the ruling coalition, despite their previous political alliance.
The JUI-F leader was a key stakeholder in the Pakistan Democratic Movement (PDM), the multi-party alliance led by the PML-N and PPP that removed Imran Khan's government through a no-confidence vote in April 2022. JUI-F later joined the political coalition that formed the government under Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif.
Relations changed after the February 8, 2024 general elections, which the veteran politician rejected, alleging the results were manipulated. He distanced his party from the ruling coalition and moved to the opposition benches, while developing parliamentary cooperation with the PTI.
The latest tensions have deepened over the federal government's handling of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, particularly its decision to impose governor's rule and an emergency in the province on October 11, 2026.
Maulana criticised the move as an attack on democracy and accused the Centre of undermining provincial rights. He also alleged that the government had reneged on commitments despite his efforts to mediate between the PML-N-led federal government and the PTI leadership.
The dispute escalated into a public war of words between JUI-F chief and Defence Minister Khawaja Asif and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi.
During a heated Senate session on October 29, Naqvi claimed that JUI-F leaders had previously approached the government seeking governor's rule in KP, triggering protests from the party's lawmakers.
Maulana rejected the government's position on the province and questioned the interior minister's statements and activities.
The confrontation widened in November after police and Federal Constabulary personnel were withdrawn from security duties at JUI-F ameer's residences in Islamabad and Dera Ismail Khan.
The party also protested over alleged surveillance of his residence and the circulation of footage on social media. On November 24, JUI-F moved the Lahore High Court after alleging that police had restricted the cleric's movement at his Lahore accommodation, preventing him from attending a lawyers' event.
The party has maintained that these actions are aimed at pressuring its leadership, while the latest reported arrests in Khushab have further strained relations ahead of its planned Sargodha gathering.
