# PM Shehbaz orders swift NCCIA restructuring, resolution of pending cybercrime cases

Published November 25, 2026
ISLAMABAD: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has directed authorities concerned to expedite the restructuring process of the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) to transform it into an effective and modern institution and align the system for the prevention and investigation of cybercrimes with modern needs.
The premier, chairing a meeting regarding the restructuring of the NCCIA on Wednesday, said that protecting the public from cybercrimes is among the government’s priorities.
He directed the deployment of experts from the information technology and artificial intelligence sectors in the NCCIA and all its subordinate offices, and the hiring of the best and merit-based human resources in the agency.
PM Shehbaz said that the restructuring of the institution should focus on the use of AI and modern technology, while utilising all available resources to resolve pending cybercrime cases.
To review the reform proposals presented by the NCCIA, the prime minister ordered the formation of a committee comprising federal ministers Mohsin Naqvi, Azam Nazeer Tarar, Muhammad Aurangzeb, and Shaza Fatima Khawaja, which would submit its recommendations to him by the end of the current week.
During the meeting, recommendations regarding the restructuring of the NCCIA were presented, and its performance, administrative structure, and future course of action were also reviewed.
In a briefing, the participants of the meeting were told that there was a proposal to convert the NCCIA into a national-level Pakistan Cyber Police, for which the best human resources would be recruited on merit.
It was informed that a specialised system for national security concerning cybercrimes had become operational, and all possible measures were being taken under the existing system to resolve cybercrime cases.
The meeting was attended by federal ministers Azam Nazeer Tarar, Musadik Masood Malik, Ahad Khan Cheema, Muhammad Aurangzeb, and Syed Mohsin Raza Naqvi, Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry, Minister of State for Finance Bilal Azhar Kayani, and senior officers of the relevant departments.
