# CM Murad orders removal of senior officials, suspension of 16 SBCA officers over Karachi building collapse

- Published: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 14:03:56 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-26 09:08:15 UTC

---

KARACHI: Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah ordered sweeping action on Thursday over the collapse of an under-construction building in Karachi’s Baloch Colony the previous day, including the suspension and removal of several officials.
The ground-plus-five-storey building with one penthouse covered 600 square yards, and it collapsed when over 20 workers were present at the site. At least three people were killed while 14 were injured in the incident.
A search operation continues at the site, with the Rescue 1122 spokesperson saying at least 17 people had been reported missing. There has been no official confirmation of the number of missing individuals.
CM orders action
The structure’s construction, on a residential plot, is said to be illegal, about which CM Murad had expressed concern on Wednesday and vowed action.
Presiding over a high-level meeting today, he subsequently ordered the removal of Waseem Shamshad as the Sindh local government secretary and Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) director general. He also directed that Shamshad’s services be surrendered to the federal government, a statement issued by the CM Office said.
The CM further instructed that a recommendation be made to the federal government to suspend Shamshad and order a formal inquiry against him.
The provincial chief executive also ordered the removal of East Deputy Commissioner Nasrullah Abbasi, as well as that of Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) East Zubair Sheikh over “negligence”, the statement said.
He further issued directives for the suspension of 16 SBCA officials and the initiation of an inquiry against them.
CM Murad directed the police to “immediately” arrest the building’s owner, Salman Tahir, and the builder, Zeeshan.
Moreover, he instructed that the building’s architect and structural engineer be included in the incident’s investigation to determine whether the construction complied with approved plans, building regulations and safety standards.
He further directed that a “comprehensive and transparent” probe be carried out and strict action be taken against any other SBCA employee found responsible.
The statement quoted him as saying that any negligence resulting in the loss of lives or causing suffering to people would not be tolerated.
He said public safety must remain the “foremost responsibility” of every government department and warned that negligence in enforcing building regulations would lead to strict action, the statement added.
Sindh Local Government Minister Syed Nasir Hussain Shah, Sindh Home Minister Ziaul Hasan Lanjar, Karachi Mayor Murtaza Wahab, Sindh Chief Secretary Asif Hyder Shah, Karachi Commissioner Hassan Naqvi and other senior officials attended the meeting.
Ongoing search operation
Meanwhile, a search operation at the site of the collapse was resumed today.
A Rescue 1122 statement said a wall of a vacant plot adjoining the site of the incident had been razed to facilitate the operation. “Heavy machinery has been brought to the site through the vacant plot to remove the rubble,” the statement added.
A Rescue 1122 team had entered the collapsed structure and was carrying out the search there as well, the statement added.
Rescue 1122 spokesperson Hassaan Ul Haseeb Khan told Dawn that an information desk had been set up in collaboration with the East district administration and so far, families of 17 missing individuals had approached them.
