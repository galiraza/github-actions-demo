# ECP issues delimitation schedule for 9 KP districts, freezes administrative boundaries

- Published: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 21:00:17 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-26 17:29:52 UTC

---

ISLAMABAD: The Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) on Thursday issued the schedule for the delimitation of village and neighbourhood councils in nine districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
The nine districts included Karak, Hangu, Mansehra, Malakand, Shangla, Chitral Upper, Swat, Bar Swat and Paharpur.
In a separate directive, the commission also ordered that the boundaries of all administrative and revenue units in these districts stand frozen forthwith, with no changes to be made until the culmination of the delimitation process.
The ECP had earlier this month halted all preparations for local government elections in the province until further orders, after the federal government extended the suspension of Article 140-A of the Constitution to the whole province.
Prior to this, the commission had decided to go ahead with holding local government polls in KP districts where delimitation had been completed, excluding the 12 districts with special emergency measures.
On Thursday, however, the ECP issued the schedule for conducting the delimitation exercise in the nine districts.
As per the schedule, all administrative arrangements, including procurement of maps and relevant data as well as the training of delimitation committees and authorities, will be completed between Dec 1 to 9.
The delimitation committees will prepare the preliminary list of village and neighbourhood councils from Dec 10 to Jan 9, 2027. The preliminary list will be published on Jan 10 to invite objections, which may be filed before the delimitation authorities from Jan 11 to 27, 2027.
The delimitation authorities will dispose of the objections by Feb 15 and their decisions will be communicated to the committees by Feb 23. The delimitation committees will notify the final lists of village and neighbourhood councils on Feb 26.
Separate notifications have also been issued for the appointment of delimitation committees and delimitation authorities for the disposal of objections filed against the preliminary delimitation in these districts.
The ECP said that delimitation was required to be carried out keeping in view the existing boundaries of administrative units — such as district, tehsil, qanungo halqa, patwar halqa, revenue estates and the extent of urban and rural local areas. It said any change in the limits of these units while the delimitation process was under way would hamper its smooth proceedings.
The commission, therefore, directed that the boundaries of all existing administrative and revenue units of districts, tehsils and city and tehsil councils in the nine districts shall cease forthwith and no change shall be made till the culmination of the delimitation process in the province.
