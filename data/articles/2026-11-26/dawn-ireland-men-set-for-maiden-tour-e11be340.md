# Ireland men set for maiden tour of Pakistan next month, to play three ODIs

- Published: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 14:01:52 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-26 09:08:15 UTC

---

The Ireland men’s team will tour Pakistan next month for the first time to play three One-day Internationals against the hosts, the cricket boards of both countries announced on Thursday.
The visitors, according to the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB), are scheduled to arrive in the country on January 9, and will take on national developmental side Pakistan Shaheens for three warm-up one-day matches between January 13 and 17.
The ODIs are set for January 20, 22 and 24. All six matches, the PCB said, will be played at Lahore’s Gaddafi Stadium.
The series was originally planned to be a multi-format one before plans were changed with the proximity of the 2027 World Cup in mind.
“While we originally planned a multi-format series, both Boards have agreed that their respective squads would benefit most from playing 50-over cricket given our upcoming participation in the World Cup Qualifier and Pakistan preparing for the World Cup itself later in 2027,” Graeme West, Director of High Performance at Cricket Ireland, said in a statement.
This tour, therefore, is an important milestone for Ireland, who are also all set to feature in the upcoming World Cup Qualifier, through which they’ll try to secure their spot for next year’s showpiece.
“Over the years we have enjoyed a close relationship with the Pakistan Cricket Board and their players, and we look forward to this historic first tour – the first of many in the coming years, we hope,” West added.
For the hosts, meanwhile, the tour would be an opportunity to test their depth – especially through the Shaheens matches – and continue the fine-tuning process of their combinations ahead of the World Cup.
Pakistan made a statement of sorts about their ODI form, convincingly winning a tri-series, which featured England and Sri Lanka along with Shaheen Shah Afridi’s side.
