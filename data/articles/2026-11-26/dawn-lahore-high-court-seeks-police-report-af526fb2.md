# Lahore High Court seeks police report on Maulana Fazl’s confinement

LAHORE: The Lahore High Court on Wednesday sought a report from the capital police chief on a petition challenging the alleged confinement of Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam (JUI-F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman to his temporary residence in the city.
Justice Ali Zia Bajwa heard the petition filed by Advocate Chaudhry Abrar Hussain, a member of the JUI-F legal wing.
The lawyer informed the court that Maulana Fazl had been restricted to his residence in Lahore.
The judge observed that if the JUI-F chief had been illegally detained, the matter was serious and whoever was responsible for the unlawful action would have to face the consequences.
Judge warns of consequences if JUI-F chief illegally detained
Justice Bajwa remarked that if Maulana Fazl had been placed under detention, the authorities would have to provide reasons for the action.
Additional Advocate General Farrukh Javed Lodhi told the court that the petitioner-lawyer had met Maulana Fazl the other day. He said a photograph of the meeting had gone viral on social media.
Advocate Hussain said he had been allowed to meet the JUI-F chief after repeatedly requesting the police for permission. He maintained that Maulana Fazl remained under illegal confinement at his residence and was still not allowed to leave the house.
The petitioner contended that the JUI-F chief’s life and liberty were under threat. He sought directions for his recovery.
The judge directed the Capital City Police Officer to submit a detailed report on the matter by Thursday (today).
The JUI-F chief was scheduled to participate in a multiparty conference hosted by the Lahore High Court Bar Association on Tuesday. However, he could not leave his residence in a private housing society on Multan Road due to deployment of a heavy police contingent.
