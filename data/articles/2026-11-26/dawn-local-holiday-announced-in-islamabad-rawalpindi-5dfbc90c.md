# Local holiday announced in Islamabad, Rawalpindi on Nov 27

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 21:20:10 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047220/local-holiday-announced-in-islamabad-rawalpindi-on-nov-27
- Collected: 2026-11-26 17:29:52 UTC

---

A local holiday has been declared in the Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT) and Rawalpindi for Nov 27 (Friday).
A notification issued by the Islamabad deputy commissioner stated that Nov 27 “has been declared a local holiday within the revenue limits of ICT”.
It noted that all essential services would remain operational during the holiday, including the Metropolitan Corporation Islamabad, the Capital Development Authority, the ICT administration, Islamabad police, the Islamabad Electric Supply Company, the Sui Northern Gas Pipeline Limited and hospitals.
Separately, the Rawalpindi deputy commissioner shared a notification on X stating that a local holiday had been declared in the district for Nov 27. He said that all schools, colleges and offices would remain closed.
The notification said that all “emergency dealing departments/institutions/authorities shall remain operational”.
“Moreover, all annual and supplementary examinations shall be conducted as per the approved schedule in all education institutes of Rawalpindi district,” it said.
Both notifications did not provide a reason for the holiday.
The Federal Constitutional Court (FCC) also issued a notification ordering that no judicial work be conducted on Friday at the principal seat of the court in Islamabad, on account of the closure of the city’s Red Zone and the consequent difficulties expected to be faced by lawyers, litigants and officials of the court in reaching the premises.
“The cause list of the court shall stand cancelled. However, all officers and staff shall work from home and shall not leave the station,” the notification said.
It added that the four branch registries of the court would all remain open for judicial and administrative work.
Meanwhile, a notice of expected partial closure was issued for the Rawalpindi-Islamabad metro system from Nov 26-28, as a result of expected “VIP movement”.
The notice warned of a possible suspension of the line from Kashmir Highway to the Pak Secretariat stations and said the closure of the last four stops was also under consideration.
The metro service from Saddar to Faiz Ahmed Faiz would remain operational, it added.
