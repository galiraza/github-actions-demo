# PTI disowns Achakzai’s call for national govt

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 07:25:04 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047151/pti-disowns-achakzais-call-for-national-govt
- Collected: 2026-11-26 09:08:15 UTC

---

• Salman Akram, Ali Zafar exchange heated words at parliamentary party meeting 
• Party to deliberate on new provinces only after reviewing government’s draft
ISLAMABAD: The PTI on Wednesday distanced itself from a proposal made by Opposition Leader in the National Assembly Mehmood Khan Achakzai regarding formation of a national government in the country.
After a joint meeting of the parliamentary groups of the PTI in the National Assembly and the Senate, one of the participants told Dawn that they discussed the recent statement of Mr Achakzai in which he had proposed formation of a national government in the country and a majority of the members opposed such an idea.
“After a detailed discussion, it was collectively decided that this proposal belongs to Achakzai’s party [Pakhtunkhwa Milli Awami Party], and the PTI will not support any such move,” the participant said on condition of anonymity.
Mr Achakzai had called for a national government and constitutional reforms during a multi-party conference at the Lahore High Court Bar Association.
Sources revealed that in the meeting it had been decided that no party member would issue any policy statement on the issue of new provinces until the government releases its draft proposal.
The party’s parliamentarians decided to have an in-house discussion on the new provinces’ issue sometime next week to finalise its stance.
According to the sources, the meeting was interrupted for a brief time due to exchange of heated arguments between party’s Secretary General Salman Akram Raja and parliamentary leader in the Senate Barrister Ali Zafar.
They said Mr Zafar flared up when Mr Raja stated that he was aware that “what some persons are doing but does not want to mention their names”.
Mr Zafar challenged Mr Raja to mention the names. At this point, the two leaders exchanged some heated arguments when some of the participants, including Aamir Dogar and Faisal Gandapur, intervened and prevented the situation from getting worse.
Without naming, Mr Raja said some of the party members even did not bother to read party’s policy statements due to which each of them started giving different statements.
He said that despite being underground, Information Secretary Sheikh Waqas Akram issued policy statements on a daily basis, and sent them the party’s narrative but they did not read it.
The sources said that PTI’s Sardar Latif Khosa briefed the participants about his recent meeting with the party’s founding chairman Imran Khan in Adiala jail.
Mr Khosa reportedly said that Mr Khan had directed him that the issue of new provinces should be discussed in the parliamentary party meeting.
Meanwhile, talking to Dawn, Sheikh Waqas Akram said the PTI would give its official reaction after analysing the government’s draft for new provinces. He said it had been decided that a coordination committee would be formed to take prompt decisions. The committee, he said, would comprise representatives of the National Assembly, Senate and provincial parliamentary parties.
Meeting with Imran
Meanwhile, the PTI has submitted a list of the party members with the Adiala jail administration for a meeting with Mr Khan today (Thursday). The Islamabad High Court has ordered to arrange the meeting of family and lawyers with Mr Khan on Tuesdays and meeting of PTI leaders on Thursdays.
