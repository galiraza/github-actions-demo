# Rawalpindi ATC defers verdict in November 2024 protest case involving Aleema Khan until Dec 16

- Published: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 13:09:49 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-26 09:08:15 UTC

---

RAWALPINDI: An anti-terrorism court (ATC) in Rawalpindi on Thursday adjourned until December 16 the announcement of its reserved verdict in a case registered against Aleema Khan, sister of incarcerated PTI founder Imran Khan, in connection with the November 26, 2024 protest.
ATC Judge Amjad Ali Shah deferred the verdict for the sixth time without holding any further proceedings.
Aleema’s counsel, Faisal Malik, appeared before the court on Thursday.
The court had reserved its verdict in the case on September 22, fixing Sept 25 as the announcement date. However, the announcement was deferred after Judge Shah went on leave, with October 1 set as the next date.
The verdict has since been postponed several times, with the latest adjournment pushing it to Dec 16.
Aleema’s counsel had also objected to the court reserving its verdict without hearing the defence’s arguments, saying he had not been given an opportunity to present his case.
In September, Aleema was detained under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO) Ordinance ahead of a PTI march towards Islamabad. She remains incarcerated at Lahore’s Kot Lakhpat jail.
The case
The case came to be known as the November 26 protest case. The protest saw more than 10,000 PTI supporters enter Islamabad in 2024, defying a ban on public gatherings and a lockdown, and clash with around 20,000 security personnel deployed to prevent them from advancing.
A day of clashes between security forces and protesters in the city’s Red Zone ended with the PTI leadership retreating, after which the party announced that it was calling off its planned protest sit-in “for the time being”.
Subsequently, cases were registered against several PTI leaders under various sections of the Pakistan Penal Code and Section 7 of the Anti-Terrorism Act. Two of the cases were registered at Taxila police station and one each at Sadiqabad and Naseerabad police stations.
Aleema, along with 10 others, was booked in the case registered at Sadiqabad police station.
