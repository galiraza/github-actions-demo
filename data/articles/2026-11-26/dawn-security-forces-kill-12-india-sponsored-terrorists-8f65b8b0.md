# Security forces kill 12 India-sponsored terrorists in multiple IBOs in Balochistan: ISPR

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 12:38:08 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047181/security-forces-kill-12-india-sponsored-terrorists-in-multiple-ibos-in-balochistan-ispr
- Collected: 2026-11-26 09:08:15 UTC

---

Security forces killed 12 India-sponsored terrorists in four separate intelligence-based operations (IBOs) in Balochistan between November 20 and 23, the military’s media wing said on Thursday.
According to the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), security forces targeted a terrorist hideout in Nushki district on November 20. Following an intense exchange of fire, eight terrorists belonging to the Indian-backed Fitna-al-Hindustan were killed. Weapons and ammunition were also recovered from the site, the ISPR added.
The state has designated Balochistan-based terrorist groups as Fitna-al-Hindustan to highlight India’s alleged role in terrorism and destabilisation across Pakistan.
On November 21, security forces intercepted the movement of terrorists in Sibi district, killing two more, the ISPR said.
In two separate engagements in Panjgur and Kech districts on November 22 and 23, security forces raided terrorist hideouts and killed two more terrorists belonging to Fitna-al-Hindustan, the ISPR added.
The ISPR said the counterterrorism campaign under the vision of “Azm-i-Istehkam”, as approved by the Federal Apex Committee on the National Action Plan, would continue at full pace to eliminate the menace of foreign-sponsored and supported terrorism from the country.
Meanwhile, President Asif Ali Zardari lauded the security forces for carrying out “successful intelligence-based operations” in Balochistan against “terrorists operating under India’s patronage”, a statement issued by his office said.
The statement quoted him as saying: “Security forces’ continued successes in the fight against terrorism demonstrate their commitment and sacrifices for peace and stability in the country.”
He added that the “elimination of Fitna-al-Hindustan and foreign-backed terrorists is essential for national security. Security forces and law enforcement agencies will continue operations against foreign-backed and -supported terrorists in every part of Pakistan”.
Pakistan has seen a surge in terrorism, especially in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. Last week, 15 terrorists were killed during two separate operations in Balochistan’s Mastung district.
On Nov 14, four troops, including a major, were martyred while 24 terrorists were killed in multiple IBOs in Balochistan and KP.
On Nov 1, a soldier was martyred while 17 terrorists were killed in two IBOs in Balochistan’s Kachhi and Kharan districts.
According to Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies, Pakistan recorded fewer terrorist attacks from Nov 6 to 12, but fatalities and injuries increased sharply, “highlighting the changing intensity and continued volatility of militant threat across the country”.
KP remained the principal centre of violence, accounting for 16 attacks and 44 fatalities. Balochistan recorded 13 attacks, resulting in eight deaths and 19 injuries, while the former tribal districts witnessed a “substantial” reduction in attacks.
