# IHC extends stay on Islamabad local government elections by two weeks

Published November 26, 2026
The Islamabad High Court (IHC) on Thursday extended its stay on local government elections in the federal capital for another two weeks, seeking a detailed response from the Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) over the issue.
Hearing a petition challenging the electoral process in Islamabad, IHC Justice Umair Majeed Malik questioned the ECP over the unresolved issue of constituency delimitation.
"Have you not resolved the matter of delimitation?" Justice Malik asked during the hearing.
The judge also referred to complaints raised by petitioners, who maintained that they did not know which electoral wards they belonged to.
The court also referred to the local government law enacted in 2015, observing that delimitation should be carried out in accordance with the law.
In response, the ECP lawyer maintained that the delimitation process had been completed in accordance with the applicable law.
Justice Malik referred to an October 14 order in which the ECP had stated that the delimitation process was complete. However, he asked the commission to satisfy the court that the process had been properly concluded.
The IHC subsequently sought a detailed response from the ECP and adjourned the hearing for two weeks.
The court suspended the local government election schedule in the federal capital on November 13, issuing a stay order against the ECP's notification dated October 21.
The petition submitted by Amjad Ali had contended that new delimitation was carried out in 2024, following which the election schedule was announced. However, a new Local Government Ordinance was introduced in 2026.
The petitioner's counsel contended that after the ordinance ceased to operate, the election schedule was issued without restoring the previous delimitation.
The term of the last elected local government system in the Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT) expired in February 2021. However, fresh elections could not be held due to diverse legal and administrative reasons.
The statutory requirement under Section 219(4) of the Elections Act, 2017, is that the ECP is required to hold new local government elections within 120 days of the term’s expiration.
