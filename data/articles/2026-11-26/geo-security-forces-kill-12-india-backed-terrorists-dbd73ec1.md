Published November 26, 2026
Security forces killed 12 India-backed Fitna al-Hindustan terrorists in multiple intelligence-based operations (IBOs) carried out in Balochistan’s Nushki, Sevi, Panjgur and Kech districts, the military’s media wing said on Thursday.
In a statement, the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said that the terrorists were killed during operations between November 20 and 23.
The military’s media wing said that security forces killed eight terrorists during an operation in Balochistan's Nushki district on November 20.
“On 20th November, a terrorist hideout was targeted in Nushki district. After intense engagement, eight terrorists belonging to Indian backed Fitna al-Hindustan were neutralised. Weapons and ammunition were recovered from the killed terrorists,” the ISPR said.
In another intelligence-based operation on November 21, security forces in Sevi district intercepted the movement of terrorists and killed two Indian-backed militants.
The ISPR said security forces targeted hideouts in two other engagements, on November 22 and 23 in Panjgur and Kech districts, killing two terrorists belonging to the Indian-backed Fitna al-Hindustan.
The military vowed that a relentless counterterrorism campaign would continue at full pace to wipe out the menace of foreign-sponsored and supported terrorism from the country.
Pakistan has witnessed a surge in terror activities, particularly in its Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) and Balochistan provinces, since 2021, when the Afghan Taliban came into power.
According to a report by the Centre for Research and Security Studies (CRSS), the two provinces remained the country's main hotspots for violence during the second quarter of 2026, with KP emerging as the worst-affected region.
The report stated that KP and Balochistan together accounted for nearly 96% of all violence-related fatalities recorded during the quarter. Of the total deaths, more than 61% (475) were reported in KP, while Balochistan accounted for 34% (265).
KP also recorded the highest number of violent incidents, with 151 cases, representing 57% of the nationwide total. Balochistan followed with 94 incidents, or 35%, underscoring the concentration of militant activity in the two provinces.
