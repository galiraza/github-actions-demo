# 3 convicted of taking woman from UK to Pakistan, forcing her into marriage: police

- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 22:09:19 +0500

- Collected: 2026-11-27 21:53:10 UTC

---

Police in the United Kingdom said on Friday that three people had been convicted of taking a young woman from the country to Pakistan and forcing her into marriage.
A statement issued by West Midlands Police noted that the woman was “particularly vulnerable” to exploitation as she did not have the capacity to consent to marriage.
“This conviction is understood to be the first in the UK which centres around a victim not being deemed to have capacity to consent to marriage,” police said.
“Mohammad Bashir, Khatoon Bi and Mobeen Akhtar, all from Birmingham, took advantage of a vulnerable woman in autumn 2024,” the statement said, adding that the police were alerted to the exploitation in early November of that year and immediate inquiries were carried out by their public protection unit.
According to police, the investigators learned that the involved parties were returning to the UK “within days”. Officers detained the three on Nov 7, 2024 at Birmingham Airport on suspicion of forced marriage.
“We also safeguarded the woman and, after speaking to her, we established she was unaware how she had been exploited,” the police said.
Deputy Senior Investigating Officer (DSIO) Mindy Mahil, one of the officers leading the investigation, said, “These three took advantage of a young woman, who was extremely vulnerable, and took her from Birmingham to Pakistan for marriage.
“It became clear to us immediately that the woman was being exploited, and that she did not have capacity to consent to a marriage.”
According to Mahil, the welfare of the woman was the priority throughout the sensitive inquiry.
“Forcing someone into marriage without their consent is abuse and control, and we will always seek to pursue offenders and support victims,” the DSIO said.
Following the trial at Birmingham Crown Court, Bashir, aged 55, Bi, 55, and Aktar, 38, were convicted of the offence and will be sentenced at a later date, the statement said.
