# Airline Norse Atlantic's Norwegian operating unit declares bankruptcy

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 18:26:27 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047462/airline-norse-atlantics-norwegian-operating-unit-declares-bankruptcy
- Collected: 2026-11-27 17:10:04 UTC

---

Airline Norse Atlantic’s Norwegian operating unit has declared bankruptcy, the long-haul budget carrier said on Friday, succumbing to soaring fuel costs wrought by the war on Iran.
Norse Atlantic ASA, which is listed on the Oslo Euronext Expand bourse, and other group companies are not part of the filing and are still attempting to stay in business, the company said in a statement.
Norse Atlantic, which launched in 2022, on Wednesday warned that its financial situation was “critical”, and the following day said it had suspended all flights and that the board could file for bankruptcy unless it urgently secured funding.
Earlier on Friday, Norse said its lessors had terminated their leasing contracts for all 12 aircraft in the carrier’s fleet, demanding that the planes be handed back, a claim contested by the company.
“The Board of Norse Atlantic Airways AS has made the difficult decision to file for bankruptcy despite extensive efforts from the company and the group to find a solution,” Norse CEO Eivind Roald said in a statement.
“The board, management and advisers of the listed entity Norse Atlantic ASA and the UK subsidiary are working to find a solution for the remaining part of the business to continue operations,” Roald said.
The global aviation industry is suffering its worst crisis since the Covid-19 pandemic as the US-Iran conflict, which began at the end of February, disrupts operations, drives up fuel prices, and sends airlines’ costs skyrocketing.
Pakistan International Airlines (PIA), a wet-lease partner of the airline, said earlier that it would shift its UK operations to its own Boeing 777 fleet from Nov 26 after Norse Atlantic abruptly suspended operations, forcing the cancellation of two flights from London and Manchester.
Norse in 2021 signed long-term leases for nine Boeing 787 Dreamliner aircraft from Ireland-based AerCap Holdings and six Dreamliners from Singapore-based BOC Aviation Ltd. It later returned three of the planes to AerCap.
In October, PIA signed an ACMI (aircraft, crew, maintenance, and insurance) lease agreement with Norse Atlantic Airways to acquire two state-of-the-art Boeing 787-9 Dreamliners, one of which arrived in Islamabad on Nov 1.
A PIA spokesperson at the time said the addition of the Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner would mark the start of the expansion of the airline’s fleet as it aimed to meet rising demand.
