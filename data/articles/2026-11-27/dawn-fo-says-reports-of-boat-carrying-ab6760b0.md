# FO says reports of boat carrying Pakistani migrants capsizing off Libya remain unverified

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 15:39:40 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047463/fo-says-reports-of-boat-carrying-pakistani-migrants-capsizing-off-libya-remain-unverified
- Collected: 2026-11-27 17:10:04 UTC

---

The Foreign Office said on Friday that there had been no “independent verification” of the reports of a boat carrying Pakistani migrants capsizing off the coast of Libya and that Pakistan’s embassy in Libya was “actively pursuing the matter” to ascertain facts.
It was reported earlier this week that at least 25 people from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s Bajaur district were feared dead or missing after a migrant boat capsized near Libya.
According to reports, the victims’ families said they had received information from the agents who arranged the travel that a migrant boat en route to Europe had reportedly capsized in the Mediterranean Sea near Libya on Nov 21.
While there has been no official confirmation of the incident, the funeral prayers in absentia of several individuals have been offered.
The FO said in its statement that Pakistan’s embassy in Tripoli was pursuing the matter to ascertain facts, including the presence of Pakistani nationals on the reportedly capsized boat.
“The embassy has contacted the relevant Libyan government authorities, the International Organisation for Migration, and other relevant stakeholders.
“Further information will be shared as soon as verified details become available,” the FO statement said.
Previous boat tragedies
In April this year, more than 80 migrants including 20 Pakistanis were reported missing after a boat carrying around 120 people capsized in the Central Mediterranean after departing from Libya.
Most of the missing Pakistanis had travelled to Libya posing as Umrah pilgrims, while others were on visit visas to Azerbaijan, Egypt and Uganda.
Following the boat tragedy, the FIA intensified its crackdown on human smuggling networks, targeting agents operating illegal overland routes through Iran and Turkiye. The agency also launched investigations to trace the suspects’ assets and sought Interpol’s assistance in pursuing agents based abroad.
In October last year, 18 migrants were killed when a wooden vessel capsized off the coast of Libya, while 64 people, including 12 Pakistanis, were reported missing. The boat capsized off Surman due to high waves, only a few hours after setting sail.
In April 2025, four Pakistanis were among the 11 migrants found dead in a shipwreck near Libya’s Harawa coast.
In June 2023, at least 209 Pakistanis were among the migrants aboard an overloaded fishing boat that capsized and sank in international waters off Greece on June 14.
Witnesses estimated that between 400 and 750 people were aboard the vessel. The official death toll stood at 82, while 104 people survived, including 12 Pakistanis.
