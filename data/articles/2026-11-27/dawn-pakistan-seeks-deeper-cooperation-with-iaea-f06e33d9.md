# Pakistan seeks deeper cooperation with IAEA on nuclear security

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 16:14:28 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047472/pakistan-seeks-deeper-cooperation-with-iaea-on-nuclear-security
- Collected: 2026-11-27 17:10:04 UTC

---

Pakistan looks forward to further deepening cooperation with the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) and international partners in support of sustainable nuclear security, the Foreign Office (FO) said on Friday.
The statement came as the 15th annual meeting of the International Network for Nuclear Security Training and Support Centres (NSSC Network), hosted by Pakistan in collaboration with the IAEA, concluded in Islamabad.
The meeting, held from Nov 23 to 26, brought together international delegates to discuss nuclear security training, capacity building and cooperation, the statement added.
A special event was also held at the headquarters of the Pakistan Nuclear Regulatory Authority (PNRA) to mark 20 years of Pakistan-IAEA cooperation in nuclear security. The event featured an exhibition of indigenous nuclear security technologies developed by Pakistan’s national industry.
Delegates also visited the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), where they were briefed on the country’s capabilities in nuclear engineering, research, education and training.
The programme included technical sessions, expert discussions and thematic presentations on emerging nuclear security challenges, institutional sustainability, human resource development and the future of international cooperation in nuclear security training.
At the concluding session, the chairman of the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission (PAEC) underscored the importance of sustained international cooperation in strengthening nuclear security.
The IAEA’s unit head of the Division of Nuclear Security commended Pakistan for hosting the meeting, describing it as an important milestone in international nuclear security cooperation.
The statement said the meeting had provided a platform for member states to exchange expertise, strengthen national capabilities and identify future priorities.
Pakistan first hosted the NSSC Network’s annual meeting in 2016. Its return to Pakistan after a decade reflected the country’s sustained engagement with the IAEA and continued contribution to international nuclear security cooperation, the statement added.
In a video address at the opening session on Monday, IAEA Director General Rafael Mariano Grossi thanked Pakistan for hosting the meeting and highlighted the network’s role in translating nuclear security principles into practice.
A day earlier, Pakistan had called for keeping decisions on the use of nuclear weapons under human control and cautioned against the use of artificial intelligence (AI) systems to target nuclear command, control and communications systems.
Speaking at an open debate of the UN Security Council, Pakistan’s Permanent Representative to the UN, Ambassador Syed Haider Shah, said emerging technologies posed new challenges to international security and called for appropriate safeguards, regulations and confidence-building measures to prevent miscalculation and escalation.
