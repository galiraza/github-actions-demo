# Pakistan Shaheens cruise into Asia Cup Rising Stars final with five-wicket win over Bangladesh ‘A’

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 23:56:18 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047510/pakistan-shaheens-cruise-into-asia-cup-rising-stars-final-with-five-wicket-win-over-bangladesh-a
- Collected: 2026-11-27 21:53:10 UTC

---

Pakistan Shaheens produced a composed five-wicket victory over Bangladesh ‘A’ in the second semi-final of the Asia Cup Rising Stars on Friday, chasing down a target of 233 with 28 balls to spare to book their place in Sunday’s final against Sri Lanka ‘A’.
The defending champions reached 233-5 in 45.2 overs after Bangladesh ‘A’ had posted 232-8 in their 50 overs. Hasan Nawaz, who made 62 and claimed two wickets, was named player of the match.
The chase was shaped by a measured third-wicket partnership between Hasan and Shahzaib Khan, who steadied the innings after Pakistan had lost both openers inside the first five overs. Shahzaib made 50 from 85 deliveries, while Hasan’s 91-ball 62 included three fours and three sixes.
Pakistan’s start had been unsettled by Bangladesh seamer Ripon Mondol, who removed the openers early and left the Shaheens needing to rebuild.
Hasan countered with calculated aggression, including a six off Iqbal Hossain Emon that eased the early pressure, while Shahzaib concentrated on occupying the crease and rotating the strike.
The pair took the total to 128 before Hasan was trapped leg-before by Md Rubel in the 31st over. Shahzaib continued to anchor the innings, bringing up his half-century before falling in the 35th over to an excellent diving catch by substitute fielder Rizwan at midwicket off Rakibul Hasan.
With the innings requiring fresh impetus, captain Abdul Samad and Farhan Yousaf took charge. Samad struck 44 from 33 balls, adding momentum with forceful strokes through the leg side, while Farhan remained unbeaten on 46 from 39 deliveries.
Their partnership carried Pakistan through a testing middle phase, with the required rate kept within reach despite a couple of quiet overs and a near run-out involving Farhan.
Samad’s dismissal, with the target in sight, did little to derail the chase. Farhan kept his composure, declining unnecessary risks and ensuring the innings moved forward. Pakistan crossed the line in 45.2 overs, with Rohail Nazir alongside Farhan when the winning run was completed.
Earlier, Bangladesh ‘A’ had been lifted by a superb unbeaten 103 from Shahadat Hossain, whose innings rescued a side that had struggled for momentum in the opening half.
He faced 118 balls, hitting seven fours and three sixes, and shared a 95-run third-wicket partnership with Mahidul Islam Ankon to give Bangladesh a platform.
Pakistan’s new-ball bowlers made early inroads, with Ali Raza dismissing both openers inside the first five overs.
Bangladesh made a cautious start and reached 41-2 after 13 overs, before Shahadat and Ankon rebuilt through patience and selective attacking shots. Ankon contributed 30, helping Shahadat settle before the partnership was broken.
Shahadat then found another useful ally in SM Meherob, with the pair adding a half-century for the sixth wicket. Meherob made 28 from 36 balls, but Pakistan struck twice in the 41st over when Hasan dismissed Meherob and Rubel in quick succession.
Sufyan Moqim was central to Pakistan’s recovery, finishing with 3-29 from his 10 overs and preventing Bangladesh from accelerating during the middle overs. Hasan took 2-30, while Raza also claimed two wickets.
Shahadat’s late surge, including a six to bring up his hundred in the final over, pushed Bangladesh beyond 230, but the total proved insufficient against Pakistan’s steady response.
The Shaheens’ disciplined chase ensured that Shahadat’s determined century went in vain and kept their title defence on course.
