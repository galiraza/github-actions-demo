# President Zardari, PML-Q chief Shujaat discuss country's political situation

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 22:35:51 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047504/president-zardari-pml-q-chief-shujaat-discuss-countrys-political-situation
- Collected: 2026-11-27 21:53:10 UTC

---

President Asif Ali Zardari on Friday discussed the country’s political situation in a meeting with former prime minister and Pakistan Muslim League-Quaid (PML-Q) chief Chaudhry Shujaat Hussain.
In a statement, the Presidency said that President Zardari held the meeting with Shujaat at the latter’s residence in Lahore, where Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi was also present.
“National affairs and the political situation were discussed during the meeting,” the statement said.
The president wished the PML-Q leader a speedy recovery, it added.
According to the statement, Punjab Governor Sardar Saleem Haider, federal minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain, provincial minister Chaudhry Shafiq Hussain, former prime minister Raja Pervez Ashraf, Senator Saleem Mandviwalla and Dr Asim also attended the meeting.
The meeting comes in the wake of an ongoing debate over the creation of new provinces and the holding of a referendum, with President Zardari’s PPP opposing the move.
The debate was sparked by Naqvi’s July remarks, wherein he called for the formation of new administrative units in the country, saying the current “system” of governance had “collapsed” and needed a “reset”.
Earlier this week, Naqvi also held a meeting with Shujaat, where the debate over the creation of new provinces and devolution of powers was discussed.
During the meeting, Shujaat said the question of creating new provinces was directly linked to public convenience, good governance, administrative efficiency and the country’s future.
In the meeting, Naqvi and Shujaat discussed the overall political situation, measures to maintain law and order, and the need to transfer power to the grassroots level.
Later that day, Naqvi met Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, with whom he discussed the prevailing situation.
