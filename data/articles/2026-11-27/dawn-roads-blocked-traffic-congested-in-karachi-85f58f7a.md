# Roads blocked, traffic congested in Karachi for 2nd consecutive day

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 18:37:28 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047473/roads-blocked-traffic-congested-in-karachi-for-2nd-consecutive-day
- Collected: 2026-11-27 17:10:04 UTC

---

Karachi saw blocked roads and congested traffic for a second consecutive day on Friday as road closure alerts caused traffic to be diverted due to a protest around the Karachi Press Club (KPC).
The city’s traffic police earlier announced diversions around KPC ahead of an expected protest, advising commuters to use alternative routes to avoid inconvenience.
According to a press release, the traffic heading from MR Kiyani Chowk towards Saddar and Zainab Market would be diverted left from Coast Guards Mess towards the Sindh High Court (SHC) and Passport Office.
It added that Sharea Faisal could be closed temporarily at Avari traffic signal.
Traffic travelling from Sharea Faisal towards the airport would be diverted right from Avari traffic signal onto Fatima Jinnah Road, towards Cantt, School Road, CSD and Kala Pul, the release said.
According to updates issued by the traffic police, the road from Coast Guard towards Zainab Market and Sarwar Shaheed Road was closed to traffic at around 3:20pm in view of security.
Traffic from Coast Guard was being sent towards the Chowrangi Passport Office, while traffic coming from there was being directed towards Paradise Chowk, the traffic police said.
The road was later re-opened to traffic as of an alert at 6:05pm.
Drigh Road towards Karsaz and Sharea Faisal was closed to traffic at around 4pm, with traffic being rerouted from Drigh Road via the Rashid Minhas Flyover towards Millennium.
The road was opened about half-an-hour later, but an alert was issued for heavy traffic pressure as the traffic was only running in one lane, slowing the movement of vehicles.
At 5:22pm, the traffic police issued an update that the flow of traffic had been restored, with all lanes opened for traffic. It noted that peak hours were still ongoing but that traffic was being kept moving.
Another road closure alert was issued for the road from Avari Towers towards Mehran Hotel and Sharea Faisal at 5:10pm. Traffic is being sent via Avari Towers towards Fatima Jinnah Road, police said.
Motorists were earlier advised to avoid roads around the KPC and follow the traffic police’s instructions. The public was also urged to cooperate with law enforcement agencies and traffic police.
Commuters requiring assistance could contact the Traffic Police Rahnuma helpline at 1915, where representatives would be available round the clock to provide guidance.
The latest traffic updates are also available through Karachi Traffic Police’s social media platforms, the statement added.
