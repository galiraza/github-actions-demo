# SBP issues Rs200 commemorative coin to mark Mata Sahib Hasi Devi’s 200th birth anniversary

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Fri, 27 Nov 2026 19:25:58 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047495/sbp-issues-rs200-commemorative-coin-to-mark-mata-sahib-hasi-devis-200th-birth-anniversary
- Collected: 2026-11-27 17:10:04 UTC

---

The State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) has issued a Rs200 commemorative coin to mark the 200th birth anniversary of Pujya Mata Sahib Hasi Devi, the bank said in a statement on Friday.
Mata Sahib Hasi Devi was the fifth head of the historic Shadani Darbar located in the Hayat Pitafi area of Sukkur division’s Ghotki district.
According to the statement, the coin has been issued through the exchange counters of all field offices of the SBP Banking Services Corporation from Friday.
The coin is made of cupro-nickel, comprising 75 per cent copper and 25pc nickel. It measures 30 millimetres in diameter and weighs 13.5 grammes, the statement said.
It added that the face of the coin features a waxing crescent moon and five-pointed star in the centre, facing northwest in a rising position. Along the periphery on top of the crescent and star is inscribed “Islami Jamhuria Pakistan” (Islamic Republic of Pakistan) in Urdu.
Below the crescent, at the top of the two wheat springs with their arms curved upwards, is the year of issuance 2026. The coin’s value and “rupia” in Urdu script are inscribed on the right and left sides of the crescent and star, respectively.
Meanwhile, the back features a picture of Puj Shandani Darbar in the centre. The inscription “Pujya Mata Sahib Hasi Devi” appears at the top along the periphery, while the wording “200th birth anniversary” is written along the lower periphery.
The years 1826 and 2026 are written on the left and right sides of the picture, respectively, marking the 200th birth anniversary, the statement said.
Last month, Pakistan Post also issued two special commemorative postage stamps and a souvenir sheet for the 200th birth anniversary of Mata Sahib Hasi Devi to promote interfaith harmony and Sufi traditions in the region.
