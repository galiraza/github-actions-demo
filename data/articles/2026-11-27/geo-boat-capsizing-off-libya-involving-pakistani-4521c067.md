# Boat capsizing off Libya involving Pakistani migrants yet to be verified, says FO

Published November 27, 2026
ISLAMABAD: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs on Friday said reports of a boat carrying Pakistani migrants capsizing off the coast of Libya had yet to be independently verified, adding that the Pakistan embassy in Tripoli is actively pursuing the matter to ascertain the facts.
In a statement, the Foreign Office (FO) Spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan said the embassy had contacted the relevant Libyan government authorities, the International Organisation for Migration (IOM) and other stakeholders to establish whether Pakistani nationals were among those on board.
"We have seen some media stories pointing to a reported boat capsizing incident off the coast of Libya. There has been no independent verification of such reports so far. We hope and pray it is not true," he said.
According to the reports, the boat carrying migrants attempting to reach Europe capsized in the Mediterranean Sea near Libya on November 21. Families of those on board reportedly received information about the deaths from agents who had arranged their journeys.
At least 25 people from Bajaur are feared dead or missing in the incident, although the exact number of casualties and circumstances surrounding the reported tragedy have yet to be officially confirmed, The News reported.
Meanwhile, police and the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) have launched investigations into suspected human smuggling networks allegedly involved in arranging the migrants' journeys.
The FO spokesperson said further information would be shared as soon as verified details became available.
