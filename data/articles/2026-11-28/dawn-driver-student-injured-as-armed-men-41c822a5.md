# Driver, student injured as armed men open fire on students’ bus in Charsadda

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sat, 28 Nov 2026 12:33:42 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047654/driver-student-injured-as-armed-men-open-fire-on-students-bus-in-charsadda
- Collected: 2026-11-28 08:39:38 UTC

---

CHARSADDA: A driver and a student were injured when armed men allegedly opened fire on a coaster carrying students of Institute of Computer and Management Sciences (ICMS) College in the limits of Charsadda’s Nisatta police station late Friday night, police said.
According to Station House Officer (SHO) Nisatta Khan Zaib, two vehicles carrying around 70 students were returning from a recreational trip to Murree when the incident occurred.
The vehicles were travelling on GT Road after the motorway was closed and had reached the Nisatta area, he said.
SHO Zaib told Dawn that the firing stemmed from a dispute between two students during the trip. He said a case had been registered against two nominated suspects, while several people had been arrested in connection with the incident.
According to the coaster driver, who is the complainant in the case, a minor altercation had taken place between students during the trip. On their way back, the suspects, along with their accomplices, allegedly intercepted the vehicle and opened fire.
The firing left the driver and a student with bullet wounds. They were taken to hospital, where the driver was later referred to Peshawar for further treatment.
The complainant further alleged that the armed men had also tried to enter the vehicle but teachers standing near its door resisted them, preventing further harm to the students.
Meanwhile, District Police Officer Rafatullah Khan took notice of the incident and directed the police to arrest the nominated suspects without delay.
A special team, headed by Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) Sardheri Bilal Haleem Khan and comprising SHO Zaib and other police officials, was formed to arrest the suspects.
According to a statement issued by Charsadda police, raids were being conducted at various locations to apprehend those involved. A case was registered under Sections 324, 427 and 34 of the Pakistan Penal Code.
The president of the Private Colleges Association Charsadda, Sajjad Ali, and the president of the Council of Private Schools Association Charsadda, Mansoor Kausar Khan, condemned the firing.
In a joint statement, they demanded an impartial inquiry and the immediate arrest of all those involved. They warned that all educational institutions in the district would be closed in protest if the suspects were not arrested and justice was not ensured.
They also expressed concern over the safety of students and urged the police to take effective measures to prevent such incidents.
