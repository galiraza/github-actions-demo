# Govt, PPP discuss upcoming Parliament session and 'potential legislatives agendas'

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sat, 28 Nov 2026 20:49:46 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047689/govt-ppp-discuss-upcoming-parliament-session-and-potential-legislatives-agendas
- Collected: 2026-11-28 15:56:43 UTC

---

Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar met with PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari on Saturday, with the upcoming session of Parliament and “potential legislatives agendas” coming under discussion.
According to a handout from the PPP, Dar called on Bilawal at Zardari House in Islamabad. He was accompanied by National Assembly (NA) Speaker Ayaz Sadiq, Parliamentary Affairs Minister Tariq Fazal Chaudhry and Minister of State for Interior Bilal Azhar Kayani.
Senator Sherry Rehman and MNA Naveed Qamar joined the PPP chairman at the meeting, the statement said.
“Bilawal and the government delegation discussed the upcoming session of Parliament and potential legislative agendas,” the handout said.
It added that Bilawal also asked Dar to “personally look into the raid” on Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman’s residence and the “treatment of his family members”.
Meanwhile, Chaudhry said that the overall political situation in the country was also discussed.
The meeting comes in the wake of an ongoing debate over the creation of new provinces and the holding of a referendum, with the PPP opposing the move.
The debate was sparked in July by Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi’s remarks, wherein he called for the formation of new administrative units in the country, saying the current “system” of governance had “collapsed” and needed a “reset”.
On Nov 21, Dar and the NA speaker had also called on President Zardari at President House to discuss the overall political situation.
The meeting followed an apparent easing of tensions between President Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, after the PPP chairman’s remarks at a ‘Meet the Press’ event at the Karachi Press Club.
At the event, Bilawal had opposed the creation of new provinces, warning that dividing Sindh would lead to bloodshed. He also criticised the coalition over several issues, including the treatment of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, PTI founder Imran Khan’s incarceration and the 27th Amendment. He later clarified that he was raising an alarm, not issuing a threat, and complained that the PPP was not being consulted.
The remarks drew sharp reactions from PML-N ministers Khawaja Asif and Tallal Chaudhry, while MQM-P chief Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui urged the courts to take notice.
President Zardari subsequently met PM Shehbaz and Naqvi, with the meeting calling for dialogue and restraint in public statements.
