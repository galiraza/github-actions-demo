# Israeli strike kills two Doctors Without Borders staff in Gaza

- Source: Dawn
- Published: Sat, 28 Nov 2026 12:41:22 +0500
- Link: https://www.dawn.com/news/2047658/israeli-strike-kills-two-doctors-without-borders-staff-in-gaza
- Collected: 2026-11-28 08:39:38 UTC

---

Doctors Without Borders (MSF) said Saturday an Israeli strike killed two staff members at a clinic in Gaza, with the Israeli army alleging that the pair were members of Hamas.
The Israel Defence Forces (IDF) claimed the men, who worked as security guards at the clinic, were Hamas artillery cell commander Moaz Yousef Alitsbach and Ahmed Sufyan Muhammad Ali Abu.
MSF said it was “devastated” by the killings and denounced “a direct hit” on the organisation.
“Our premises were clearly marked with MSF logos, and their location had been communicated to and was known by the Israeli military,” it wrote on X.
World Health Organisation chief Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus slammed the attack.
“Attacks on health facilities, health workers and humanitarian personnel in #Gaza are outrageous and unacceptable,” he posted on X.
The IDF denied targeting a hospital or damaging medical infrastructure. The Israeli military has regularly targeted health facilities in the besieged Palestinian enclave.
The latest attack comes amid a series of Israeli strikes targeting senior Hamas security officials in Gaza. On Wednesday, an Israeli air strike killed Mohammad Abuelouf, a Hamas colonel who headed the group’s internal security service in northern Gaza, along with his wife and two daughters at their apartment in Gaza City’s Beach refugee camp.
On November 24, Israeli air strikes killed at least three people in Gaza, including Adel Al-Helo, a worker with the UN Palestinian refugee agency (UNRWA), according to the agency and local health officials.
Al-Helo was working near a water well in Jabalia refugee camp when the strike hit. UNRWA said its staff were carrying out routine duties related to the well’s operations. The agency said three other people were wounded in the attack.
In a separate strike later that day, two people were killed when an Israeli air strike targeted a vehicle in western Gaza City, medics said.
