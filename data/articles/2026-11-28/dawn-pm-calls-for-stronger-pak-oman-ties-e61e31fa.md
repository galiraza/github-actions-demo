# PM calls for stronger Pak-Oman ties

- Collected: 2026-11-28 08:39:38 UTC

ISLAMABAD: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has called for further strengthening Pakistan’s bilateral cooperation with Oman, particularly in trade, investment, energy and defence.
During a meeting with the Omani ambassador at the Prime Minister’s House, the prime minister highlighted the longstanding brotherly relations between Pakistan and Oman, rooted in shared history, faith and culture.
According to a Prime Minister’s Office news release, PM Shehbaz stressed the importance of translating the positive momentum in bilateral relations into tangible results through increased economic engagement and institutional cooperation.
The two sides also discussed recent regional and global developments. The prime minister emphasised the importance of maintaining peace and stability through dialogue and diplomacy.
The Omani ambassador thanked the prime minister for receiving him, conveyed the greetings of the Omani leadership and reaffirmed his country’s commitment to further strengthening its close relations with Pakistan.
PCSIR role in exports
Separately, PM Shehbaz on Friday directed the Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research to enhance its research capabilities and contribute more effectively to industrial development, export growth and the promotion of small- and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
Chairing a meeting on matters related to the PCSIR, the prime minister said the institution could play a significant role in helping Pakistani products compete in international markets.
He directed all PCSIR laboratories to obtain accreditation in accordance with international standards to facilitate internationally-recognised certification and validation of Pakistani export products.
The prime minister also called for stronger cooperation between the PCSIR and the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC) to improve industrial and technical training opportunities for young people. He sought the report of the third-party validation conducted on the institution.
The prime minister constituted a committee to review the PCSIR’s mandate, performance and future role. The committee will examine the institution’s original objectives, legal responsibilities, achievements and untapped potential, and recommend measures to improve its contribution to the national economy, industrial development and exports.
The meeting was informed that the PCSIR operates 10 research laboratories and units across the country, along with seven technical training institutes, while 12 additional laboratories are nearing completion.
The institution provides industries with modern technologies, research facilities and affordable technical solutions aimed at improving productivity and product quality.
Officials informed the meeting that the PCSIR had provided technical training to more than 12,000 young people. Its research and development activities cover a wide range of sectors, including food, agriculture, biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, textiles, leather, minerals, engineering and energy.
The briefing further highlighted that PCSIR laboratories provide domestic industries and exporters with facilities for product testing, quality verification and calibration of technical instruments.
