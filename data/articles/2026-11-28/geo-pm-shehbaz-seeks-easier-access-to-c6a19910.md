# PM Shehbaz seeks easier access to bank loans for small and medium enterprises

ISLAMABAD: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has directed authorities to simplify loan procedures for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and facilitate their access to finance to boost economic activity, generate employment and promote entrepreneurship.
Chairing a high-level meeting on improving access to credit for the SME sector on Friday, the premier directed that SMEs’ export data available through the Pakistan Single Window, as well as their participation in supply chains through Exim Insurance and digital invoicing, be considered as alternative collateral for securing bank loans.
He also called for the cash flow of SMEs to be considered an alternative criterion for assessing their eligibility for financing and directed a review of the prudential regulations governing bank lending to the sector.
The prime minister further instructed that banks be provided with access to relevant data held by government institutions to speed up the loan disbursement process.
Emphasising the need for comprehensive banking support, he said banks should extend assistance to SMEs not only through loans but also by facilitating the issuance of Letters of Credit (LCs) and bank guarantees.
He added that banks achieving their designated SME lending targets would be encouraged and rewarded.
The meeting was informed that banks were taking steps to strengthen their institutional capacity to accelerate lending to SMEs.
The banks expressed their commitment to providing Rs2,000 billion in loans to SMEs by June 2028 and increasing the number of SME borrowers to 775,000.
The target includes the disbursement of Rs886 billion in new loans and the provision of financial facilities to 449,000 new SME borrowers.
The prime minister observed that SMEs were the backbone of the country’s economy and said facilitating the sector would help expand economic activity, create employment opportunities and promote entrepreneurship.
He also stressed the need to provide SMEs with effective guidance and support in accessing financial resources, preparing business documentation and capitalising on export opportunities.
The meeting was attended by Federal Ministers Rana Tanveer Hussain, Ahad Khan Cheema and Riaz Hussain Pirzada; Prime Minister’s Adviser Haroon Akhtar; Minister of State for Finance Bilal Azhar Kiani; State Bank of Pakistan Governor Jameel Ahmad; chief executive officers of the National Bank of Pakistan, Bank Alfalah and the Bank of Punjab; and senior officials of the relevant institutions.
Meanwhile, chairing a meeting on PCSIR, the prime minister said the Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (PCSIR) could play an important role in promoting export-led growth and increasing the country’s exports.
He said the PCSIR could also contribute significantly to the ecosystem for the development of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and the expansion of exports, a Prime Minister’s Office news release said.
He directed all PCSIR laboratories to obtain accreditation in line with international standards so that internationally recognised certification and validation of Pakistani export products can be facilitated.
The prime minister also directed the PCSIR to make its research role more effective in order to enable domestic products to compete in global markets.
He further directed that cooperation with the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC) be enhanced for the industrial and technical training of youth.
The prime minister called for the report of the third-party validation conducted on the PCSIR. He also constituted a committee to review the mandate of the PCSIR, its performance to date and its potential future role.
The committee will examine the objectives behind the establishment of the PCSIR, its legal responsibilities, the measures taken so far and the possibilities for making full use of the institution’s capacity in the future. It will also determine how the PCSIR can be made more effective in contributing to the national economy, industrial development and the growth of exports.
Meanwhile, Minister for Power Awais Ahmed Khan Leghari on Friday called on Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and briefed him on matters related to the Power Division.
The minister apprised the prime minister of the proceedings and outcomes of the 11th session of the Pakistan-Russia Inter-Governmental Commission (IGC), held during his recent visit to Russia.
The two sides also discussed matters related to the ministry.
Meanwhile, the prime minister directed the Minister for Railways Hanif Abbasi to take effective measures to ensure the timely completion of ongoing projects under the ministry. He issued the directive during a meeting with the minister, who called on him at the Prime Minister’s House.
The two sides discussed ongoing and proposed railway projects, along with other matters related to the ministry.
